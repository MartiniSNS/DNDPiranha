### Rooms and Key Features

#### Entrance Corridor

- **Hazard:** Hanging threads form a web. Each creature that moves through must succeed on a **DC 13 Dexterity check** or become **entangled** (escape DC 13). Entangled creatures have speed 0 and disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity checks until they use an action to free themselves.
    
- **Clue:** A frayed thread glows faintly; cutting it reveals a whisper of a memory and reduces the corridor’s entangling effect for 1 minute.
    

#### Weavers’ Gallery

- **Layout:** Three wall tapestries depict different Vault custodians. Two raised loom platforms in the center are connected by narrow planks.
    
- **Mechanic:** Each tapestry is a semi‑sentient construct. Damaging a tapestry causes it to lash out by animating threads in the room.
    
- **Optional Interaction:** Players can attempt a **DC 15 Arcana or Performance check** to coax a tapestry into revealing a memory instead of attacking. Success grants a clue or a minor boon; failure awakens a Tapestry Weaver.
    

#### Memory Alcoves

- **Contents:** Glass orbs that store short memories. Breaking an orb releases a Memory Phantom that reenacts the memory as an illusory scene and may attack if the memory is hostile.
    
- **Risk/Reward:** Players can absorb a memory (DC 14 Wisdom save). Success grants a flash of insight: advantage on one skill check related to the Vault; failure imposes a short‑term confusion effect.
    

#### Threadworks Chamber

- **Focal Point:** The Reliquary Loom, a massive tapestry that can stitch or unweave a 10‑foot square of reality once per hour.
    
- **Activation:** The loom requires a **Threaded Needle** or a successful ritual (10 minutes, DC 16 Arcana). If activated, the loom can:
    
    - **Stitch:** Repair a broken object or mend a wound for 2d8 HP once per activation.
        
    - **Unweave:** Temporarily erase a small nonliving object from existence for 1 hour.
        
- **Danger:** The loom resists manipulation. Failed ritual checks cause the loom to animate and summon two Tapestry Weavers.
    

#### Archivist Niche

- **Hidden Door:** Behind a tapestry, revealed by pulling a specific glowing thread (Perception DC 15).
    
- **Treasure:** The **Threaded Codex** and a trapped chest. The trap is a memory swap: opening the chest forces a **DC 15 Wisdom save** or lose a minor memory (player‑chosen or random) for 24 hours.
    
- **Codex Use:** The Threaded Codex contains a ritual to temporarily bind a tapestry or to extract a single memory into an Echo‑Glass Vial.
    

#### Staircase Down

- **Seal:** Woven runes block the spiral stairs. The runes require either the Threaded Codex ritual or three distinct memories offered to the Reliquary Loom to unseal.
    

### Traps and Puzzles

#### Thread Snare

- **Trigger:** Disturbing certain hanging threads.
    
- **Effect:** Entangles and pulls a creature into a tapestry surface. The creature must succeed on a **DC 14 Strength save** or be partially pulled in, taking **2d6 psychic damage** and becoming restrained until freed.
    

#### Memory Lock Puzzle

- **Mechanic:** A door with three memory slots. Players must place three memories that form a coherent narrative to open the door.
    
- **Checks:** Each memory placement requires a **DC 13 Insight** to judge fit. Incorrect combinations trigger a Memory Phantom.
    

#### Loom Sequence Puzzle

- **Mechanic:** The Reliquary Loom responds to a sequence of thread pulls. The loom displays three colored threads; players must pull them in the correct order.
    
- **Clues:** Tapestries in the Weavers’ Gallery hint at the order through repeated motifs.
    
- **Failure:** Each failed attempt animates one tapestry.
    

### Encounters and Stat Blocks

#### Tapestry Weaver

- **Role:** Medium construct that animates threads to attack and entangle.
    
- **Suggested CR:** 4
    
- **AC:** 15
    
- **HP:** 68 (8d8+32)
    
- **Speed:** 0 ft., climb 20 ft.
    
- **Attacks:**
    
    - **Thread Lash.** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d6+5) slashing damage and the target must succeed on a **DC 14 Strength save** or be pulled 10 ft and restrained until the end of its next turn.
        
    - **Weave Burst (Recharge 5–6).** The weaver releases a burst of animated threads in a 15‑ft cone. Each creature in the cone must make a **DC 14 Dexterity save** or take 21 (6d6) slashing damage and be entangled. On a success, half damage and not entangled.
        
- **Special Traits:**
    
    - **Threadform.** Resistant to bludgeoning and poison; immune to psychic.
        
    - **Repairing Stitch.** If adjacent to a tapestry, it regains 10 HP at the start of its turn.
        

#### Memory Phantom

- **Role:** Illusory spirit formed from a broken memory.
    
- **Suggested CR:** 2
    
- **AC:** 13
    
- **HP:** 27 (6d8)
    
- **Speed:** 0 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)
    
- **Attacks:**
    
    - **Memory Touch.** Melee Spell Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d6+2) psychic damage and the target must succeed on a **DC 13 Wisdom save** or be haunted by the memory, suffering disadvantage on the next attack roll or ability check.
        
- **Special Traits:**
    
    - **Ethereal Form.** Immune to nonmagical physical attacks. Vulnerable to radiant damage.
        
    - **Reenact.** Once per encounter, the phantom can force one creature to relive the memory, imposing a **DC 13 Wisdom save** or be stunned until the end of its next turn.
        

### Loot and Rewards

- **Threaded Codex** (rare, attunement optional) — A leatherbound book of woven rituals. Contains the ritual to bind a tapestry, extract a memory into an Echo‑Glass Vial, and a one‑time ritual to unweave a small nonliving object permanently. Using a ritual requires 1 hour and components worth 50 gp.
    
- **Time‑Touched Trinkets** (uncommon) — Small charms that grant a single short rewind: once per long rest, the wearer can reroll one attack roll, saving throw, or ability check and must take the new result.
    
- **Echo‑Glass Vials** (common) — Record a single memory or sound for later playback. Useful for puzzles or distractions.
    
- **Threaded Needle** (uncommon) — A silver needle that functions as a key for certain Vault mechanisms and grants +1 to checks when used to manipulate magical textiles.
    
- **Minor Coinage and Curios** — 75–150 gp in assorted ancient coins, a few ornamental buttons worth 5–20 gp each, and a small tapestry fragment that can be sold or used in crafting.
