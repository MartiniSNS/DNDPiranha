[[Thalia Mireweave]]

Special Commissions (quest hooks / story items)
- “Sap‑safe lining” — Thalia can line a garment with treated cloth that resists sap staining and dulls its magical trace. Cost: 40–100 gp + a rare ingredient (Everspring alchemist’s neutralizer or a vial of purified willow extract). Craft time: 2–4 days.  
- “False Identity Wardrobe” — complete disguise set with forged house livery (non-magical) and a pre-written short cover story: 150–400 gp (includes fabric, tailor work, and a contact who will forge a simple letter of introduction for a fee).  
- “Court‑ready Suit” — a tailored coat with hidden pockets to carry small documents or a ledger fragment: 80–250 gp, depending on ornamentation.  
- “Sap‑Bane Trim” (story item): a narrow trim sewn around cuffs/neck made with alchemical treatment — grants advantage on one attempt to remove or resist sap-binding effects on a single target (story-limited, DM-controlled). Requires a quest to gather the treatment components.
