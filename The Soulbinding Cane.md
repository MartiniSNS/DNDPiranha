_Legendary quarterstaff (requires attunement by a warlock)_

A tall, twisted obsidian cane topped with a cage of golden filigree containing a single trapped star. It hums with barely contained pacts and bindings.

**Properties**

- Functions as a **+3 arcane focus** and quarterstaff.
- Your warlock spell attack rolls and save DCs gain a **+3 bonus**.
- **Binding Contract** (3/day): When you hit a creature with a spell attack or force it to make a saving throw against one of your warlock spells, you can attempt to bind it (DC 20 Charisma save). On failure, it is charmed by you for 1 hour and cannot willingly leave your sight. You can also bind elementals, fiends, or celestials more easily.

**Eldritch Seal** You can cast _banishment_, _planar binding_, and _arcane gate_ once per long rest without expending slots. Your patron cannot force you to act against your will while attuned.

**Sentience** Intelligence 20, Wisdom 16, Charisma 22. Chaotic Neutral. Sly and silver-tongued. Seeks to bind and control anything that crosses between worlds.

**Curse**

- You cannot form or maintain any other pacts while attuned.
- Each time you use Binding Contract on a powerful outsider, your patron becomes aware and may demand a price or send enemies.
- The cane slowly drains your hit point maximum by 1 per day until you make a new “sacrifice” (a soul coin or living creature).
