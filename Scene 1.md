# Session 3 — Scene 1: Immediate Aftermath
## The Dust Settles
*Dust motes drift through shafts of fading light. The floorboards of Blackford Tollhouse are scarred—scorch marks from spellcasting, splinters from overturned furniture, and dark stains of blood. Two, perhaps three cultist bodies lie still. Garrin Tollman is either slumped behind his ledger chest or sprawled near the floor depending on the fight's outcome. The air smells of sulfur, old wood, and copper.*

*Outside, the evening deepens. A few townsfolk may have heard the commotion, but none have yet approached. You have perhaps 10–15 minutes before curiosity brings watchers or the cult sends reinforcements.*

---

## Core Scene Sequence

### Opening (DM Narration — read once)

- Two or three bodies in dark cultist gear lie at odd angles—one near the window, one by the door to the back rooms.
- A small bell on the back counter lies on its side, its last ring fading.

The afternoon light is fading fast. You can smell rain coming, and beneath it, something acrid—burnt cloth or powder from a failed spell."

## Immediate Mechanical Beats

### 1) Stabilize Garrin & Assess Damage
**If Garrin is unconscious or dying:**
- **Medicine check DC 12** to stabilize him (Stabilize cantrip, healer's kit, or basic first aid counts).
- **Success:** Garrin is stabilized; he's breathing but won't wake for 1d4 minutes.
- **Failure:** Garrin lapses into unconsciousness; you have one more chance before death saves begin.

**If Garrin is conscious but wounded:**
- He's panicked, bloodied, and may try to flee or ring the bell again (see stat block Reaction).
- **Intimidation DC 14** or **Persuasion DC 13** to calm him and prevent another bell ring.
- If he rings again: **1d6 minutes** before town guards or a neighboring lord's men arrive.

**Roleplay line (Garrin, trembling):**
> "I—I didn't ask for this. They left the satchel, and I looked away. I swear, I didn't know—please, don't kill me. My wife... my wife is in town. Please."

### 2) Search the Cultist Bodies - [[The Shadowed Branch]]
*Each cultist carries typical field gear. Use the following table as a menu—roll or pick what fits.*

#### **Cultist #1 (near the window)**
- **Clothing & Gear:**
  - Dark leather jerkin (worn, stained with blood or ash).
  - A short curved dagger (mundane, ~2 gp value).
  - A satchel (small, leather, weathered).
  
- **Satchel Contents (Investigation DC 11 to find all):**
  - 8–12 gp in mixed coin (small denominations).
  - A wax-sealed envelope (broken, but legible):
    > "Rook ([[Corvin Vale]]) — Next delivery confirmed Archways at moonrise. Await manifest. — S."

  - A torn map fragment showing the Archways of Bridgia and a dotted route back toward Thornwell.
  
- **Hidden (Sleight of Hand DC 13 or Investigation DC 14):**
  - A small iron key tucked in a hidden seam of the satchel. It's stamped with the Blackford arms (opens something in the manor, or a lock box).
  - A coded note scribbled on parchment:
    > "If taken, signal three bells at dusk. Rook will retrieve ledger from secondary cache. Do not abandon post."

#### **Cultist #2 (by the door)**
- **Clothing & Gear:**
  - Rough wool cloak (mud-stained, smells of horse).
  - A well-worn purse (3–7 gp).
  - Thieves' tools (simple, non-magical).
  
- **Personal Items:**
  - A ring with a small twisted-branch carved into the stone (cult sigil). Worth ~5 gp, but more valuable as evidence.
  - A leather journal (small, pocket-sized). The first page bears a name: "Kael Dun" and a note:
    > "Sworn to the Branch, Year 3. Tollhouse duty, watch and report. S. pays 2 gp per week. Keep the books, stay silent, watch for officials."
  - Several pages with tally marks and dates—a personal log of satchel deliveries over the past two months.
  - The final entry: "Tonight—something changed. They felt different. New players on the board. We'll see."

- **Hidden (Sleight of Hand DC 14 to find):**
  - A tiny vial of clear liquid (likely a sedative or poison; smells faintly of bitter almonds). Handle with care; **Constitution save DC 12** or fall unconscious for 1d4 hours if ingested.
  - A hand-drawn symbol on the inside back cover: a crude map of Thornwell with a red mark on the steward's manor.

#### **Optional: Cultist #3 (if a third was present in combat)**
- **Simpler inventory:** 5–8 gp, a dagger, a hand-written ledger scrap similar to Ciaran's fragment.
- **Unique item:** A small bell (identical to the one on the counter) and a striker. Suggests they were a signal-post or courier.

## Searching Garrin & Extracting Information

**Immediate Search (Sleight of Hand DC 12 or Perception DC 11):**
- Garrin carries a leather-bound **tollhouse ledger** (small, ~20 pages, bound in worn leather).
- A ring of keys (5 keys total—one for the ledger chest, one for the manor kitchen, one for a cellar lock, two for unknown purposes).
- A small clay pipe (for evening use).
- A handkerchief with initials "G.T." stitched in.

**Ledger Contents (Investigation DC 12 to parse quickly; DC 15 for full understanding):**
- Pages filled with dated entries of toll collections, merchant names, and goods passed.
- Two pages marked with a small twisted-branch symbol in the corner (hidden unless you're looking closely).
- One entry, dated **seven days ago:**
  > "7/18 — Evening delivery. Satchel left in false bottom as before. S. pays prompt. No questions asked. Rook confirmed next run. — Keep ledger separate."
- A second entry, three days old:
  > "7/21 — New contact visiting. Pale, scarred. Said ledger may be moved soon. Told me to say it burned if questioned."

**Garrin's Confession (once calm, via Persuasion DC 12 or Intimidation DC 14):**
- He admits to accepting bribes (~2 gp per week) to allow satchel deliveries at dusk.
- He was told the payments were "insurance" and that he should ask no questions.
- The "Rook" (a pale man with a scar on his thumb) comes at dusk most weeks.
- He doesn't know who "S." is but suspects it's a steward or official based on the title.
- **If pressed (Intimidation DC 15):** "They threatened my wife—showed me a scrap of her shawl. Said they'd know if I talked. *Please*, I have a family."

## The Tollhouse Interior — Hidden Discoveries

### The False Bottom (Ledger Chest)
**Location:** Beneath the main ledger chest.
**Difficulty:** Investigation DC 13 to notice a faded thorned circle with cross sticks; Thieves' Tools DC 14 to open without damage.

**Contents:**
- A second, unmarked ledger (handwritten, not the official record).
- This **secondary ledger** contains:
  - A list of **monthly payments** to "S. Malric, Steward of Thornwell" (15 gp per month).
  - Entries dating back **six months.**
  - A note in a careful hand:
    > "Steward Malric oversees grain tithes. Annual surplus approx. 40 gp. Branch offers 'insurance'—his daughter secured, safe from scandal. Monthly payment ensures cooperation and silence."
  - Three other names with payments:
    - "M. Blackford — 8 gp/month — for access to manor accounts."
    - "A. Herdsman — 5 gp/month — watch the north roads."
    - "Kael Dun (courier) — 2 gp/week — tollhouse duty."

**Implication:** The Shadowed Branch is systematically blackmailing local officials and paying operatives. This ledger is **critical evidence.**

### The Cellar Access (Behind the Counter)
**Location:** A narrow wooden door behind the bar counter, usually covered with stacked crates or obscured.
**Difficulty:** Perception DC 13 to notice; Sleight of Hand DC 12 to move crates quietly.

**Cellar Interior:**
- A small, cool stone chamber—originally a wine or grain storage.
- Shelves with barrels (legitimate storage for ale or cider).
- **Hidden Nook (Investigation DC 14):** Behind a loose stone in the north wall, a shallow alcove contains:
  - **Correspondence:**
    - Three sealed letters (addressed to "Rook" but never sent) from "S."—payment authorizations and tactical notes.
    - A crude map of the Archways of Bridgia with marked meeting points.
  - **Coin purse:** 25 gp (the cult's operational funds for the region).
  - **A ledger fragment:** Matches the style of Ciaran's piece. References "Rooted Fane" and a date 3 months hence—possibly a planned ritual date.

**Complication (if PCs linger):** A small iron chest in the corner is **locked** (Thieves' Tools DC 15 or the keys from a cultist will open it). Inside:
- Wax seals stamped with the twisted-branch sigil (fresh, ready for correspondence).
- A ritual scroll (Investigation DC 15 to read):
  > "Rite of Binding — for use when leverage fails. Oath-taker kneels, sap is drawn, and the Branch knows them forever. Breach and the sap returns to the heart. Do not use lightly."
  - This hints at a darker side of the cult—compulsion, forced loyalty, ritual magic.

## Physical Clues & Investigation Opportunities

### Sap Smears & Ritual Traces
- **Perception DC 12:** Notice faint sap smears on the cultist's wrists and on the ledger chest's lock mechanism.
- **Arcana/Nature DC 13:** Identify the sap as coming from an old-growth oak or possibly the Whispering Oak itself—suggesting the cult has deep roots in Greenhollow.

### The Wax Seal Pattern
- **Investigation DC 12:** The wax on the sealed envelope matches the impression on Ciaran's original fragment—confirms this is the same operation.
- **Perception DC 13:** Notice the seal has two impressions—a twisted branch and, faintly, a rune that looks like the one on the ledger fragment.

### Blood Trail (Optional, for the dramatic)
- **Survival/Perception DC 12:** A small trail of blood near the back door suggests someone may have fled wounded (if PCs want to pursue).

## Time Pressure — Reinforcement Clock

**Roll 1d6 immediately after the scene is calm:**
- **1–2:** Reinforcements arrive in **3 minutes** (a town guard or cult retrieval team).
- **3–4:** Reinforcements arrive in **8 minutes** (enough time for the party to search and escape).
- **5–6:** Reinforcements arrive in **15 minutes** (generous; but the cult will move the ledger elsewhere if they realize it's been compromised).

**What "reinforcements" means:**
- **Low alert:** A town guard (1 guard, unarmed or armed with a shortsword) arrives to investigate commotion.
- **Medium alert:** 2–3 cultist Hands arrive to recover the ledger or retrieve wounded operatives.
- **High alert:** A Whisperer-level agent appears with 1–2 escorts—seeks to negotiate or threaten for the ledger's return.

## DM Guidance for Scene 1

### Spotlight & Pacing
- **First 5 minutes:** Let players describe their immediate actions (stabilizing allies, searching bodies, securing the scene).
- **Next 10–15 minutes:** Investigation and evidence discovery—reward players who ask specific questions ("Is there a ledger?" "Are there hidden compartments?").
- **Last 5 minutes:** Time pressure kicks in—reinforcements approach or the party decides to flee.

### Skill Check Rewards
- **Investigation DC 12–13:** Find the secondary ledger and cellar access—major plot advancement.
- **Investigation DC 14–15:** Discover the ritual scroll and the Rooted Fane reference—hints at larger cult goals.
- **Sleight of Hand DC 13+:** Pocket small items without alarming Garrin or the town.

### Treasure Summary (loot checklist)
- **From bodies:** ~20–30 gp total, cultist gear (worth ~5–10 gp if fenced).
- **From Garrin:** Tollhouse ledger, ring of keys, clay pipe.
- **From false bottom:** Secondary ledger (critical evidence), three sealed letters, crude map.
- **From cellar nook:** 25 gp (operational funds), correspondence, ledger fragment referencing "Rooted Fane."
- **From iron chest (if opened):** Wax seals, ritual scroll (dangerous but informative).

**Total XP/Milestone:** Recovering the secondary ledger and the cellar evidence = one solid milestone step toward Level 2.

## Example: What a Thorough Investigation Yields

**Party investigates:** Secondary ledger → Cellar → Iron chest → Interrogate Garrin

**What they learn:**
1. The Shadowed Branch operates in a tight grid: Thornwell steward (Malric), Blackford lord's servant, a hedge knight on north roads, and field operatives.
2. Monthly payments total ~30 gp for the region—suggests a mid-level cell, not a major hub.
3. The "Rook" is a trusted courier, pale, scarred thumb, travels at dusk (opportunity to tail).
4. The "Rooted Fane" reference suggests a ritual site, possibly in the Ancient Forest—ominous.
5. The ritual scroll hints that the cult uses darker methods than blackmail—compulsion, binding oaths tied to sap or blood.

**The lead:** All roads point to Thornwell next session—press the steward, secure more evidence, or confront the Branch before the Rooted Fane ritual comes due.
