**PERSONALITY & BACKGROUND**

**Personality Traits:** Stern and protective. Speaks little, every word weighted. Distrusts flashy magic.

**Ideal:** “Only strength and vigilance can keep the wild safe from corruption.”

**Bond:** The Verdant Fringe is mine to protect. I will not let the old crowns or the Shadowed Branch take it.

**Flaw:** Paranoia toward arcane spellcasters. Trusts almost no one.

**Backstory** Thirty-two years ago, Rowan Alderleaf sealed a wild-magic catastrophe by felling a corrupted world-tree. The tree’s heartwood fused with his armor and blade, granting him the title “the Thorned.” He crowned himself King of Sylvandar and claimed [[The Verdant Fringe]]. Now in his prime, he rules with iron-rooted law and sends Thornwardens to hunt unauthorized magic — unaware that [[The Shadowed Branch]] already grows within his borders.
