Region claimed: [[The Golden Reach]] (central riverlands). 
Ruler: King Auric Solcrest the Radiant, LG human (use Knight + Priest stats; openly wears a holy symbol of Lathander). 
Capital: Sunbridge – a city of golden sandstone built atop an ancient bridge that spans the widest part of the river. 
Flavor: Once a theocracy, now a monarchy that claims the rift-sealing was a divine miracle. Magic is tolerated only if it “glorifies the Light.” Their knights wear mirrored plate that blinds foes at dawn.

**[[The Shadowed Branch]] hooks**:

- A rider with a sealed envelope (Session 0 hook) was a Golden Reach paladin carrying proof that the cult has infiltrated the Sunbridge clergy.
- The king’s daughter is betrothed to an Apletersian prince—perfect cover for the cult to smuggle a ritual focus across borders.

**Tie to [[The Verdant Fringe]]**: The Reach’s missionaries recently built a small shrine in [[Greenhollow]]. Maela Fern distrusts them; she calls their “holy light” a little too convenient.
