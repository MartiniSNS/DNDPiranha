## Origins & Purpose
The Shadowed Branch is a secretive cult that has operated from the shadows for generations, weaving its influence into the highest echelons of power. Their primary goal is to control the direction of the kingdom by blackmailing royalty and nobles, ensuring decisions serve the cult’s mysterious agenda rather than the good of the people.

## Methods
- **Blackmail:** The cult gathers secrets, incriminating evidence, and forbidden knowledge on powerful individuals. They use this leverage to manipulate laws, trade, and succession.
- **Agents:** Members infiltrate courts and noble houses as servants, advisors, or even close confidants.
- **Secrecy:** The cult’s true leaders and membership are hidden behind layers of lies, false identities, and coded messages.

## Structure
- **High Priest/Matron:** The enigmatic leader, rarely seen, known only by a title.
- **Whisperers:** Senior cultists who handle blackmail and correspondence.
- **Hands:** Field operatives—spies, thieves, and enforcers.
- **Initiates:** New recruits, often drawn from those desperate for power or protection.

## Influence on the Campaign
- The party’s first clues may come from odd behaviour in Greenhollow’s officials or rumours overheard in The Whispering Oak.
- The cult may attempt to recruit, threaten, or frame the party as they begin to unravel the conspiracy.
- NPCs tied to the cult may appear as allies, foes, or informants.

## Plot Hooks
- A noble begs the party for help, admitting to being blackmailed.
- The cult tries to silence a witness in Greenhollow.
- The party discovers coded letters or symbols linked to the cult.
