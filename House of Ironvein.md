Region claimed: [[DustHaven the Red Cliffs]] (north-western badlands). 
Ruler: Queen Sable Ironvein, N dwarf (use Veteran + Druid stats; stone-shaped her own throne from living cliff-rock). 
Capital: Crimson Hold – carved directly into the red cliffs, reachable only by rope-bridge or griffon. 
Flavor: Harsh, mineral-rich land. The queen rules by strength of arms and earth-magic (rare and respected here). Dwarves and humans live side-by-side; surface-dwellers are called “sky-weak.”

**[[The Shadowed Branch]] hooks**:

- A washed-up ledger page lists a DustHaven noble who sold “red-vein ore” to the cult—ore that can hold necromantic energy.
- The queen’s own rune-smith has gone missing; she quietly offers the party land rights in the cliffs if they retrieve him before the cult finishes a ritual.

**Tie to Verdant Fringe**: DustHaven’s trade caravans stop in Greenhollow for fresh water and timber. One caravan master is already a secret cult sympathizer—easy first villain for level 1.
