# **MAGICAL ITEMS BY CLASS**

## **1. WARLOCK — THE PACT-BOUND**

### **ITEM: Grimoire of Eldritch Whispers**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Spellbook equivalent)

**Description:** A leather-bound tome with pages that seem to shift between black and crimson. The cover bears symbols that hurt to look at directly. When opened, whispers emanate from the pages—not threatening, but insistent.

**Mechanics:**

- **+1 to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs** (from your spellcasting ability modifier)
- **Contains 6 Warlock spells** (the player chooses which ones; they can learn new spells by studying this book)
- **Once per day**, when you cast an eldritch blast, you can add **1d4 force damage** to the blast
- **Attunement:** Yes (you must be attuned to use its benefits)

**Flavor:**

- The book occasionally opens on its own, showing relevant spells for the situation
- Cultists or demons recognize this grimoire; it's worth a significant bounty
- The whispers grow louder during times of danger or moral compromise
- Non-warlocks cannot read the book (text appears as gibberish)

**Why It Matters:** Warlocks have limited spell slots; this gives them a reliable way to enhance their signature ability (eldritch blast) while providing utility spells.

### **ALTERNATIVE: Sash of Infernal Binding**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Worn)

**Description:** A sash woven from shadow-silk, adorned with silver clasps bearing infernal runes. It wraps around the warlock's waist, and when they feel fear, it tightens reassuringly.

**Mechanics:**

- **+1 AC** (from magical protection)
- **Resistance to psychic damage** (from infernal wards)
- **Once per day**, when you make a Charisma check, you can reroll and take the higher result
- **Attunement:** Yes

**Why It Matters:** Gives the warlock survivability and social leverage without overshadowing their spellcasting.

## **2. BARBARIAN — THE RAGE-FUELED**

### **ITEM: Gauntlets of Primal Fury**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Gauntlets)

**Description:** Iron-knuckled gauntlets wrapped in leather that smells of old blood and wild places. When the wearer rages, the metal glows dull red and steam rises from the knuckles.

**Mechanics:**

- **+1 to melee weapon attack rolls** (during rage)
- **When you enter a rage**, gain **temporary HP equal to your Barbarian level** (resets each rage)
- **Once per rage**, when you hit with a melee weapon, you can force the target to make a **Strength save (DC 12)** or be pushed 5 feet away
- **Attunement:** Yes (must be attuned; loses benefits if you remove them)

**Flavor:**

- The gauntlets warm dangerously when combat is near
- Enemies recognize them as the mark of a true warrior
- In peaceful settings, the gauntlets feel cold and dormant
- They occasionally smell of sulfur or ozone

**Why It Matters:** Barbarians need damage output and survivability. These provide both while reinforcing the rage mechanic.

---

### **ALTERNATIVE: Cloak of the Wild Hunt**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Cloak)

**Description:** A cloak of deep green and brown, lined with fur that seems to move of its own accord. It trails behind the wearer like morning mist.

**Mechanics:**

- **+1 to AC** (magical protection)
- **Once per day**, after you rage, you gain **advantage on your next attack roll**
- **While raging**, you gain **+5 feet to your movement speed**
- **Attunement:** Yes

**Why It Matters:** Increases durability and mobility—both crucial for a barbarian staying in the fight.

## **3. RANGER/ROGUE — THE DEXTEROUS SHADOW**

### **ITEM: Boots of Shadow Striding**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Boots)

**Description:** Black leather boots that seem to absorb light. When you move, you leave faint shadows that linger a moment longer than they should. The soles are wrapped in cloth that muffles sound.

**Mechanics:**

- **+10 feet to movement speed** (always active)
- **Advantage on Stealth checks** when moving in shadows or dim light
- **Once per day**, you can move up to your speed and not provoke opportunity attacks (even if you don't hide
- +2 AC for 10 days
- **Attunement:** Yes

**Flavor:**

- The boots feel lighter with each step
- Shadows seem to guide your path
- Guards and hunters instinctively lose track of you in crowds
- The boots whisper encouragement

**Why It Matters:** Rangers and Rogues need mobility and stealth. These enhance both core competencies while remaining balanced.

---

### **ALTERNATIVE: Dagger of Precise Strikes**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Melee Weapon (Dagger), +1

**Description:** A sleek dagger with a blade of folded steel. The handle is wrapped in leather, and the pommel is weighted perfectly. When you hold it, you can feel its eagerness.

**Mechanics:**

- **+1 to attack rolls and damage rolls**
- **Once per day**, when you hit with this dagger, you can use a bonus action to make another attack with it (if you're a Rogue with Extra Attack or Cunning Action, this is especially valuable)
- **Finesse property** (can use Strength or Dexterity)
- **Light property** (can be used for two-weapon fighting)

**Why It Matters:** A straightforward power boost that synergizes with Rogue sneak attack and Ranger precision strike abilities.

## **4. WIZARD — THE KNOWLEDGE-SEEKER**

### **ITEM: Tome of Understanding**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Spellbook)

**Description:** An ancient tome bound in dragon leather, its pages filled with arcane notation and diagrams. The book radiates a soft blue light when opened. The smell of old paper and faint ozone fills the air around it.

**Mechanics:**

- **+2 to spell attack rolls** (from magical knowledge)
- **+1 to spell save DCs** (enemies have harder time resisting)
- **Contains 8 wizard spells** (the player chooses which ones, including cantrips or leveled spells up to 4th level)
- **Can learn new spells** by studying this book (normal rules apply)
- **Once per day**, when you cast a spell from this book, you can add **1d4 to the damage roll**
- **Attunement:** Yes

**Flavor:**

- The book responds to the wizard's thoughts, turning to relevant pages
- Other scholars recognize it as a masterwork
- Enemies fear a wizard carrying such a tome (it's worth a significant bounty)
- The marginalia contains hints and warnings from previous owners

**Why It Matters:** Wizards rely on spells and knowledge. This enhances both while giving them tools they can grow into.

---

### **ALTERNATIVE: Ring of Spell Mastery**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)

**Description:** A simple silver ring inscribed with runes that glow faintly when magic is cast nearby. The band is warm to the touch.

**Mechanics:**

- **Once per day**, you can add **+3 to a spell save DC** for one spell you cast
- **You can cast one wizard cantrip** as a bonus action once per day (even if you've already cast a spell this turn)
- **Advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened** (magical clarity)
- **Attunement:** Yes

**Why It Matters:** Gives wizards tactical flexibility and increases their survivability without overwhelming their damage output.

## **5. FIGHTER — THE STALWART WARRIOR**

### **ITEM: Sword of the Guardian**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Melee Weapon (Longsword), +1

**Description:** A well-crafted longsword with a blade of folded steel. The crossguard bears a symbol of protection—a shield or sun. The blade seems to drink in light, giving it a dark, purposeful appearance. When you hold it, you feel an inexplicable sense of duty and courage.

**Mechanics:**

- **+1 to attack rolls and damage rolls**
- **Once per day**, when you are hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to add **+3 to your AC** against that attack (potentially negating the hit)
- **When you hit with this sword**, you can choose to deal **+1d4 additional damage**
- **The sword glows faintly in the presence of evil** (not bright enough to betray position, but enough to warn the user)
- **Attunement:** No (but bonuses are stronger if attuned)

**Flavor:**

- The blade is warm to the touch, as if alive
- Previous owners of this sword are remembered as heroes
- It feels heavier with purpose, lighter with righteousness
- Other warriors respect a Fighter carrying such a blade

**Why It Matters:** Fighters need reliable damage and survivability. This provides both while giving them a moment-to-moment decision (use the +1d4 or save the parry ability for later?).

- Allies feel safer standing near you

## **6. SORCERER — THE INNATE CASTER**

### **ITEM: Cloak of Elemental Attunement**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Cloak)

**Description:** A shimmering cloak that seems to shift colors with your emotions—blue when calm, red when angry, green when focused, silver when afraid. The fabric feels like silk but is surprisingly warm. When you cast spells, the cloak ripples with matching elemental energy.

**Mechanics:**

- **+1 to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs** (magical attunement)
- **Choose one damage type** (fire, cold, lightning, or acid) when you attune to this cloak
- **Resistance to that damage type** (magical protection)
- **Once per day**, when you cast a spell that deals that damage type, you can add **2d6 extra damage**
- **Attunement:** Yes

**Flavor:**

- The cloak reflects your magical nature
- Enemies recognize your elemental affinity immediately
- The cloak provides comfort in your chosen element
- It occasionally pulses with your heartbeat

**Why It Matters:** Sorcerers need survivability and damage output. This provides both while reinforcing their connection to magic.

---

### **ALTERNATIVE: Staff of the Arcane Apprentice**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Melee Weapon (Staff), +1

**Description:** A wooden staff topped with a crystal that glows with inner light. The wood is smooth and warm to the touch, carved with symbols of magic. The crystal responds to your spellcasting, brightening and dimming with the power you channel.

**Mechanics:**

- **+1 to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs**
- **Can be used as a melee weapon** (+1 to attack and damage rolls, 1d6 damage)
- **Once per day**, when you cast a spell, you can add **1d4 to the spell's damage roll or healing**
- **The staff provides light** (like a magical torch)
- **Attunement:** Yes

**Why It Matters:** Gives sorcerers a melee option (in case spells run out) while enhancing their spellcasting.

---

### **ALTERNATIVE: Ring of Spell Storing (Limited)**

**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Type:** Wondrous Item (Ring)

**Description:** A silver ring with a small crystal set into the band. The crystal can hold magical energy—you can feel it humming faintly when you're near it.

**Mechanics:**

- **Can store one spell** (cast by you or given to you by an ally) of 1st or 2nd level
- **You can cast the stored spell** without using your spell slot (recharge once per day)
- **If you attune to this ring**, you gain **+1 to spell save DCs**
- **The ring can hold a spell for up to 10 days** before it fades
- **Attunement:** Yes

**Flavor:**

- The crystal glows when a spell is stored
- You can feel the magic waiting to be released
- This ring is prized by sorcerers who want flexibility
- Previous owners used it as an emergency measure

**Why It Matters:** Sorcerers have limited spell slots. This gives them a way to prepare for specific situations while gaining an extra use of an important spell.
