[[4-Scene 3]]

Aftermath & Lead to Session 2 (15–25 min) Purpose: Consolidate clues, give clear next steps, tie to the world.

If PCs succeed (protect/expose Hands)

- Reward: Maela slips them a small boon, a free night’s lodging and a minor favor (a town contact).
- Ciaran shares the next lead: ledger points to Blackford tollhouse or a steward in Thornwell. He asks the PCs to retrieve the rest of the ledger or shadow the tollhouse at dusk.

If PCs fail / Ciaran kidnapped

- [[1-Maela Fern - Barmaid of The Whispering Oak]] helps: “You have my loft, but [[The Shadowed Branch]] will close the shutters. They’ll claim anything to hide this.” She gives a lead: “A tollkeeper at [[Blackford]] looked pale when asked about the lord’s accounts.” This pushes the party to Blackford to investigate or plan a rescue.

Closing beats

- Let players spend 5 minutes roleplaying planning.
- Remind them milestone objectives: uncover ledger, find a Hand, and survive the first confrontation.
- Offer downtime: heal up via Maela (bandaging for free if friendly), gather supplies in [[Greenhollow]] (torches, rope), or stake out Blackford.
