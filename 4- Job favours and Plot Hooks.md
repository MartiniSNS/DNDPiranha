[[Haldric Ironhand]]

- Escort a wagon to town so Haldric’s raw iron shipment arrives safely (small pay + goodwill). Hook: bandits or cult couriers target the route.
- Recover a lost shipment reported at Brambleford (he’ll chip in materials or labour). Hook: crate bears cult marks; Haldric asks you to fetch it quietly.
- Identify a “haunted” ingot or cursed piece (claims a strange ingot ruined several blades) — return it to the Iron Hollow or find where it was forged.
- Make a discreet hidden compartment for smuggling a ledger or cipher (cost + favour). Hook: the party needs a way to move incriminating evidence.
- Alter seized cult gear so it can be used as bait for a Whisperer (mod for nonlethal capture).
- Haldric’s own side‑quest: his old bridge‑guard friend at Blackford owes him a debt; the friend’s sudden silence could be connected to a coverup.
