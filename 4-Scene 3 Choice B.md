[[4-Scene 3]]

Setup

- Scene: The Whispering Oak. PCs have just met with Ciaran; he slips away quietly toward the lane. A cloaked figure rises from the tavern’s shadow and follows him at a distance. The party notices and decides to tail the follower instead of confronting in public.
- Reminder DCs from the table: Stealth to follow without being seen DC 12, Perception to spot suspicious moves DC 12, Athletics/Acrobatics for a chase DCs ~12–15 as appropriate.

Initiative / Opening play

- Decide who leads the tail (usually the sneakiest PC). Call for Stealth checks to follow at range:
    - Thalia (rogue) Stealth roll 16 — success.
    - Ronan (fighter) Stealth roll 8 — fails and must hang back or risk being seen.
    - Elena (wizard) rolls quietly and succeeds.
- Thalia shadows the cloaked follower through a dim side street while Ronan lags behind, trying to keep cover.

Tailing beat (skill-play)

- As the follower skirts across lamplight, call a Perception check from any PCs trying to spot handsigns or dropped items (Perception DC 12). Thalia notices a glint of a wax seal impression caught on the follower’s cloak hem (Perception roll success).
- After several blocks the follower slips into a narrow alley and meets two other cloaked figures — Initiates exchanging a small satchel. Give the party a chance to eavesdrop (Passive Perception or active Perception DC 12). Success reveals a fragment of the conversation: “—Blackford tonight. Tollhouse run—S. payment—keep it sealed.”

Decision time

- The PCs can:
    1. Eavesdrop quietly and learn the meeting time and route (no combat).
    2. Intercept the Initiates (short chase/combat).
    3. Withdraw and report back to Ciaran/Maela (discretion).

Example: They choose to intercept.

Chase/engagement

- As the party moves to cut off the Initiates, one Initiate notices them (Perception contest: Initiate beat Ronan’s stealthy approach). Initiative rolled for a brief skirmish:
    - Initiative order sample: Thalia 17, Initiate A 14, Ronan 12, Initiate B 9, Elena 7.
- Combat is short and tactical: Initiates try to flee with the satchel (their mission is delivery). Use environmental obstacles — trash piles (difficult terrain), low scaffolding (half cover).
- Ronan chases and attempts an Athletics check to close the gap (Athletics DC 12). He succeeds and grapples Initiate A, preventing escape.
- Thalia uses a cunning maneuver to cut off Initiate B; Elena casts a small control spell (Sleep or Color Spray) to disable the second Initiate nonlethally.
- The party can capture one or both Initiates or recover the satchel and force them to surrender.

Aftermath & clues

- If they capture or search the satchel (Investigation DC 13): find a crumpled ledger scrap with the same shorthand as Ciaran’s fragment and a partial wax seal of the twisted-branch mark. Also find a pencilled note: “Blackford Tollhouse — S. payment — 15 gp — tonight — bring silence.”
- Interrogation options:
    - Intimidation DC 12 to make them talk now (they may blurt out: “We only move papers — the Hands collect, the Whisperers count.”).
    - Persuasion DC 14 or Insight DC 13 to glean more useful details (a meeting point, courier routes, or the name of a steward).
- Tactical outcome: the Initiates were escorts, not hardened fighters; they’ll name a meeting at the Archways of Bridgia next week and confirm Blackford as a drop point.

If they fail the tail

- If stealth checks fail badly or the party is spotted early, the follower ducks into crowds and slips away. Result: the delivery is completed and the party loses the immediate lead; this raises the cult’s suspicion (Ciaran later reports he was followed and almost trailed to his lodgings — increased danger, possible ambush later).

Roleplay fallout

- Ciaran is impressed if the PCs recovered the satchel and ledger scrap; he shares a small boon (cipher hint or a pencilled map) and asks them to either stake out Blackford at dusk or pay a visit to Thornwell’s steward.
- Maela quietly thanks them and offers free lodging; she warns that trailing cultists in public risks drawing more attention and asks they be careful with any captured Initiates (turn them over to the watch and risk leak, or keep and interrogate in the loft).

Mechanical rewards & XP

- Reward XP for a short stealth/chase + one or two low-CR foes (Initiates ~bandit-level). Nonlethal capture and interrogation yield more story XP and clues than outright killing.
- Resource cost: possible spent spell slots or minor HP loss in chase/combat.

DM tips

- Reward clever, patient play: successful tailing should give tangible intel (time, place, courier routes) that moves the plot forward without immediate violence.
- Let failure still advance the story: losing the follower should create a complication rather than a dead end (Ciaran’s safety compromised, a new ambush later, or the ledger moved to Blackford sooner).
- Use this beat to emphasize tone: subtlety and information over brute force — the cult values prisoners and messages, so capturing a courier is high value.
