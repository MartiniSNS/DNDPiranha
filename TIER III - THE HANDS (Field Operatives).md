Third tier of [[The Shadowed Branch]]

**Hands** — The cult's enforcers, spies, and silent workers. Perhaps 3–5 per major settlement.

- **Role:** Execute blackmail, silence witnesses, retrieve stolen documents, and monitor suspicious activity.
- **Characteristics:** Trained in stealth, intimidation, and violence. Often wear the twisted branch symbol (hidden or marked in ash/sap).
- **Methods:** Night visits, staged accidents, poisoning, abduction for "questioning."
- **Loyalty:** Bound by oath, fear, or blackmail themselves.

**Hand Types:**

- _The Watcher_ — A scout who follows targets, notes patterns, reports to the Whisperer.
- _The Silencer_ — A killer or enforcer who handles urgent threats.
- _The Thief_ — Specializes in retrieving letters, ledgers, or incriminating trinkets.

**Example Hand Activity:**

- Two Hands attempted to grab [[Lord Ciaran Ashvale]] at the [[The Whispering Oak]] in [[Greenhollow]]—a direct action that signals the cult views him as a critical threat.
