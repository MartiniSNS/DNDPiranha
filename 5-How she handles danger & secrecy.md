
[[1-Maela Fern - Barmaid of The Whispering Oak]]

- Discretion costs more if the stakes are high: anything that risks [[The Shadowed Branch]] or nobles will earn suspicion and a higher price. She protects her regulars; betray her trust and the inn becomes a hostile place.
- If the party looks like trouble (open weapons, crude threats), she’ll refuse service, lock the loft, and quietly signal the town watch or one of her trusted patrons.
- If you work for or are linked to [[The Shadowed Branch]], Maela will be careful — she may test you (Insight/Religion/Perception checks at the DM’s discretion) before divulging anything sensitive.
