# Ledger Repeater

## Medium construct, unaligned

**Armor Class:** 15
**Hit Points:** 64 (8d10 + 24)
**Speed:** 20 ft., fly 30 ft.

### Ability Scores
- STR 14 (+2)
- DEX 12 (+1)
- CON 16 (+3)
- INT 10 (+0)
- WIS 14 (+2)
- CHA 8 (-1)

### Challenge
- 5 (1,800 XP)

## Actions

### Echo Strike
Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 2) force damage.

### Scribe's Memory (Recharge 5-6)
A creature within 30 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom save or be stunned until the end of its next turn.

### Archive Sweep
A 15-foot cone of echoing script erupts. Creatures in the cone make a DC 14 Int save or take 10 (3d6) psychic damage and suffer disadvantage on the next save they make before the end of their next turn.
