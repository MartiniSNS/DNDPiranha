# Ledger Repeater

## Medium construct, unaligned

**Armor Class:** 15
**Hit Points:** 64 (8d10 + 24)
**Speed:** 20 ft., fly 30 ft.

### Ability Scores

- STR 14 (+2)
- DEX 12 (+1)
- CON 16 (+3)
- INT 10 (+0)
- WIS 14 (+2)
- CHA 8 (-1)

### Skills

- Perception +4

### Challenge

- 5 (1,800 XP)

## Actions

### Echo Strike
Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 2) force damage.

### Scribe's Memory (Recharge 5–6)
The Repeater reads the memory of a creature within 30 feet. The creature must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom save or become stunned until the end of its next turn.

### Archive Sweep
A 15-foot cone of echoing script erupts from the Repeater. Creatures in the line must succeed on a DC 14 Int save or take 10 (3d6) psychic damage and suffer disadvantage on the next save they make before the end of their next turn.

## Traits

### Repeating Pattern
The Repeater can only be affected by area attacks that also damage the same number of creatures. If it is hit by a single attack, it has advantage on its next saving throw.
