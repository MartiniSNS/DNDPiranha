# Corrupted Herald

## Medium celestial, lawful evil

**Armor Class:** 16 (natural armor)
**Hit Points:** 46 (7d8 + 14)
**Speed:** 30 ft., fly 40 ft.

### Ability Scores
- STR 14 (+2)
- DEX 16 (+3)
- CON 15 (+2)
- INT 12 (+1)
- WIS 17 (+3)
- CHA 15 (+2)

### Challenge
- 4 (1,100 XP)

## Traits

### Voice of the Archive
When the Herald speaks, creatures in 30 feet that can hear it must make a DC 13 Wisdom save or lose their reaction.

### Resistance to Radiant and Psychic
The Herald has resistance to radiant and psychic damage.

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Herald makes two attacks with its radiant flare.

### Radiant Flare
Melee or Ranged Spell Attack: +5 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 3) radiant damage and 4 (1d8) psychic damage.

### Writ of Punishment
One creature within 30 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom save or take 11 (2d10) psychic damage and be unable to speak until the end of its next turn.
