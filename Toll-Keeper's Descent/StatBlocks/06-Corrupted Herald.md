# Corrupted Herald

## Medium celestial, lawful evil

**Armor Class:** 16 (natural armor)
**Hit Points:** 46 (7d8 + 14)
**Speed:** 30 ft., fly 40 ft.

### Ability Scores

- STR 14 (+2)
- DEX 16 (+3)
- CON 15 (+2)
- INT 12 (+1)
- WIS 17 (+3)
- CHA 15 (+2)

### Skills

- Insight +5
- Perception +5
- Religion +3

### Senses

- Passive Perception 15

### Challenge

- 4 (1,100 XP)

## Traits

### Voice of the Archive
When the Herald speaks, a creature within 30 feet that can hear it must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom save or immediately lose its reaction.

### Resistance to Radiant and Psychic
The Herald has resistance to radiant and psychic damage.

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Herald makes two attacks with its radiant flare.

### Radiant Flare
Melee or Ranged Spell Attack: +5 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 3) radiant damage and 4 (1d8) psychic damage.

### Writ of Punishment
The Herald picks one creature within 30 feet. The creature must make a DC 14 Wisdom save or take 11 (2d10) psychic damage and become unable to speak until the end of its next turn.

### Seal the Route
The Herald creates a line of light that marks a 15-foot path. Creatures who cross it must make a DC 14 Dex save or be restrained by an invisible lock of script until the end of their next turn.
