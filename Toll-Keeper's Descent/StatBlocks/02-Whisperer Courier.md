# Whisperer Courier

## Medium humanoid, neutral evil

**Armor Class:** 14 (mage armor)
**Hit Points:** 39 (6d8 + 12)
**Speed:** 30 ft.

### Ability Scores

- STR 10 (+0)
- DEX 16 (+3)
- CON 14 (+2)
- INT 16 (+3)
- WIS 13 (+1)
- CHA 15 (+2)

### Skills

- Deception +4
- Stealth +5
- Investigation +5

### Senses

- Passive Perception 11

### Languages

- Common, Celestial, Infernal

### Challenge

- 3 (700 XP)

## Traits

### Illusionist's Veil
The Whisperer Courier has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to conceal movement or a hidden object.

### Voice of the Rattle
When the Courier speaks, one creature within 30 feet that can hear it must make a DC 13 Wisdom save or be affected by a minor compulsion: it must target the nearest object or creature in its line of sight.

## Actions

### Shortsword
Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

### Sling of Rusted Glass
Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage and the target must succeed on a DC 13 Con save or take 2 (1d4) psychic damage.

### Illusory Diversion (Recharge 5–6)
The Courier creates a false image of itself in an adjacent space. Creatures must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom save or target the illusion instead of the real Courier.

### Sleep (1/day)
The Courier casts Sleep, targeting up to 5 creatures of 5 Hit Dice or lower within 20 feet.

## Reactions

### Misdirect
When a creature attacks the Courier, it can impose disadvantage on that attack if the creature did not see the Courier's real position.
