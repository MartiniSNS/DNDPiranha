# Ledger Absolute

## Large aberration, unaligned

**Armor Class:** 20
**Hit Points:** 180 (24d10 + 48)
**Speed:** 0 ft., fly 60 ft.

### Ability Scores
- STR 20 (+5)
- DEX 14 (+2)
- CON 18 (+4)
- INT 22 (+6)
- WIS 18 (+4)
- CHA 20 (+5)

### Saving Throws
- Int +11
- Wis +9
- Cha +10

### Damage Resistances
- All but force, radiant, and psychic

### Challenge
- 10 (5,900 XP)

## Traits

### Omniscient Record
The Ledger Absolute can automatically detect a lying creature within 60 feet if it attempts to conceal the truth.

### Debt Domain
Creatures within 30 feet take 3 psychic damage at the start of each of their turns unless they are resisting the effect.

## Actions

### Multiattack
Three attacks: two chain lashes and one crushing record.

### Chain Lash
Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 30 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing damage and 7 (2d6) psychic damage.

### Crushing Record
A creature within 60 feet makes a DC 18 Wisdom save or takes 22 (4d10) force damage and is knocked prone.

### Rewrite the Account (Recharge 5-6)
One creature within 60 feet makes a DC 18 Int save or is stunned until the end of its next turn while its history is rewritten.

## Legendary Actions

### Archive Pulse
The Ledger Absolute forces each creature within 30 feet that can hear it to make a DC 15 Wisdom save. On a failure, the target takes 7 (2d6) psychic damage and loses its reaction.

### Record Slip
A creature within 60 feet makes a DC 15 Dex save or becomes restrained by shrinking script.
