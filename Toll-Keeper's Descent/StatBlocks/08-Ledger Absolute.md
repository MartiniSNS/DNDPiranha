# Ledger Absolute

## Large aberration, unaligned

**Armor Class:** 20
**Hit Points:** 180 (24d10 + 48)
**Speed:** 0 ft., fly 60 ft.

### Ability Scores

- STR 20 (+5)
- DEX 14 (+2)
- CON 18 (+4)
- INT 22 (+6)
- WIS 18 (+4)
- CHA 20 (+5)

### Saving Throws

- Int +11
- Wis +9
- Cha +10

### Damage Resistances

- All but force, radiant, psychic

### Senses

- Darkvision 120 ft.
- Passive Perception 14

### Languages

- All

### Challenge

- 10 (5,900 XP)

## Traits

### Omniscient Record
The Ledger Absolute can automatically detect a lying creature within 60 feet if it makes a statement designed to conceal the truth.

### Debt Domain
Creatures within 30 feet of the Ledger Absolute take 3 psychic damage at the start of each of their turns unless they are actively resisting the effect.

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Ledger Absolute makes 3 attacks: two with its chain lashes and one with its crushing record.

### Chain Lash
Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 30 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing damage and 7 (2d6) psychic damage.

### Crushing Record
A creature within 60 feet must make a DC 18 Wisdom save or take 22 (4d10) force damage and be knocked prone.

### Rewrite the Account (Recharge 5–6)
A creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 18 Int save or be stunned until the end of its next turn while the Ledger Absolute rewrites its history in the record.

## Legendary Actions

### Archive Pulse
The Ledger Absolute makes every creature within 30 feet that can hear it make a DC 15 Wisdom save. On a failure, the target suffers 7 (2d6) psychic damage and cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn.

### Record Slip
The Ledger Absolute chooses a creature within 60 feet and causes a burst of writing to appear in its path, forcing a DC 15 Dex save or the creature is restrained.
