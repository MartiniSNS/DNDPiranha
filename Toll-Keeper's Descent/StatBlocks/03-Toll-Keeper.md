# Toll-Keeper of the Ledger

## Medium humanoid, neutral evil

**Armor Class:** 16 (studded leather + warding sigils)
**Hit Points:** 70 (10d8 + 30)
**Speed:** 30 ft.

### Ability Scores

- STR 16 (+3)
- DEX 14 (+2)
- CON 16 (+3)
- INT 18 (+4)
- WIS 16 (+3)
- CHA 17 (+3)

### Saving Throws

- Int +6
- Wis +5

### Skills

- Deception +5
- Insight +5
- Perception +5

### Damage Resistances

- Psychic

### Senses

- Passive Perception 15

### Languages

- All common tongues, Celestial, Infernal

### Challenge

- 5 (1,800 XP)

## Traits

### Ledger Sight
The Toll-Keeper has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects while it can see the target.

### Counterspell Mastery
When the Toll-Keeper sees a creature casting a spell, it can use its reaction to attempt to counter it (DC 10 + slot level).

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Toll-Keeper makes two Ledger Blade attacks.

### Ledger Blade
Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) psychic damage.

### Read the Ledger (Recharge 5–6)
One creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or become unable to take reactions until the end of its next turn and lose its next attack roll if it moves before then.

### Arcane Burst
The Toll-Keeper emits a wave of magical script in a 20-foot radius. Creatures in range must make a DC 15 Int save or take 10 (3d6) psychic damage and be pushed 10 feet.

### Counterspell
The Toll-Keeper can cast counterspell at will, treating all spell levels as 4th-level unless otherwise noted.

## Reactions

### Ledger Call
When a creature within 30 feet hits the Toll-Keeper, it must succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence save or lose the ability to take its next attack.
