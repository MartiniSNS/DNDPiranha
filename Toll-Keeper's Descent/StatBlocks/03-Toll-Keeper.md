# Toll-Keeper of the Ledger

## Medium humanoid, neutral evil

**Armor Class:** 16 (studded leather + warding sigils)
**Hit Points:** 70 (10d8 + 30)
**Speed:** 30 ft.

### Ability Scores
- STR 16 (+3)
- DEX 14 (+2)
- CON 16 (+3)
- INT 18 (+4)
- WIS 16 (+3)
- CHA 17 (+3)

### Skills
- Deception +5
- Insight +5
- Perception +5

### Damage Resistances
- Psychic

### Challenge
- 5 (1,800 XP)

## Traits

### Ledger Sight
Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects while the Toll-Keeper can see the target.

### Counterspell Mastery
When the Toll-Keeper sees a creature casting a spell, it may use a reaction to attempt to counter it (DC 10 + spell level).

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Toll-Keeper makes two Ledger Blade attacks.

### Ledger Blade
Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) psychic damage.

### Read the Ledger (Recharge 5-6)
One creature within 60 feet must make a DC 16 Wisdom save or lose its reaction and be unable to take its next attack until the end of its turn.

### Arcane Burst
A 20-foot radius wave. Creatures in range make a DC 15 Int save or take 10 (3d6) psychic damage and be pushed 10 feet.
