# Ledger Scribe

## Medium humanoid, lawful evil

**Armor Class:** 15 (mage armor)
**Hit Points:** 52 (8d8 + 16)
**Speed:** 30 ft.

### Ability Scores

- STR 10 (+0)
- DEX 14 (+2)
- CON 14 (+2)
- INT 18 (+4)
- WIS 12 (+1)
- CHA 16 (+3)

### Skills

- Arcana +6
- History +6
- Intimidation +5

### Senses

- Passive Perception 11

### Languages

- Common, Abyssal, Celestial

### Challenge

- 4 (1,100 XP)

## Traits

### Traced in Ink
When the Scribe has line of sight to a creature, it can ask a question telepathically and cause a DC 14 Wisdom save or force that creature to answer truthfully for the next minute.

## Actions

### Arcane Quill
Ranged Spell Attack: +6 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 2) force damage.

### Balance of Debts
A creature within 30 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom save or suffer the effect of a forced choice: take 10 psychic damage and lose bonus action, or take 5 psychic damage and be pushed 10 feet.

### Mind-Script (Recharge 5–6)
The Scribe targets one creature it can see within 30 ft. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Int save or the Scribe reads its most secret memory and the creature is stunned until the end of its next turn.

## Reactions

### Record the Moment
When a creature the Scribe can see misses an attack roll, the Scribe can impose disadvantage on the creature's next attack before the end of the turn.
