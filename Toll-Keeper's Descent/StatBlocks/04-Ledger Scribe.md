# Ledger Scribe

## Medium humanoid, lawful evil

**Armor Class:** 15 (mage armor)
**Hit Points:** 52 (8d8 + 16)
**Speed:** 30 ft.

### Ability Scores
- STR 10 (+0)
- DEX 14 (+2)
- CON 14 (+2)
- INT 18 (+4)
- WIS 12 (+1)
- CHA 16 (+3)

### Skills
- Arcana +6
- History +6
- Intimidation +5

### Challenge
- 4 (1,100 XP)

## Traits

### Traced in Ink
When the Scribe can see a creature, it may telepathically ask a question and force a DC 14 Wisdom save or compel a truthful reply for 1 minute.

## Actions

### Arcane Quill
Ranged Spell Attack: +6 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 2) force damage.

### Balance of Debts
A creature within 30 feet makes a DC 14 Wisdom save or takes 10 psychic damage and loses the use of its bonus action, or takes 5 psychic damage and is pushed 10 feet. The creature chooses one effect.

### Mind-Script (Recharge 5-6)
One creature within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 14 Int save or be stunned until the end of its next turn.
