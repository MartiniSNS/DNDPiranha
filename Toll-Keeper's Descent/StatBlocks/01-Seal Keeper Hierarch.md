# Seal Keeper Hierarch

## Medium humanoid, lawful evil

**Armor Class:** 17 (chain shirt, magical warding)
**Hit Points:** 60 (8d8 + 24)
**Speed:** 30 ft.

### Ability Scores

- STR 17 (+3)
- DEX 14 (+2)
- CON 16 (+3)
- INT 13 (+1)
- WIS 15 (+2)
- CHA 12 (+1)

### Saving Throws

- Con +5
- Wis +4

### Skills

- Athletics +5
- Perception +4

### Damage Resistances

- Psychic

### Senses

- Passive Perception 14

### Languages

- Common, Celestial, Infernal

### Challenge

- 4 (1,100 XP)

## Traits

### Warding Stance
While the Seal Keeper Hierarch is standing and not incapacitated, he gains a +2 bonus to AC against attacks from creatures within 5 feet.

### Ritual Discipline
Once per long rest, when hit by an attack, the Hierarch can force the attacker to make a DC 14 Wisdom save or lose its next reaction.

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Hierarch makes two melee attacks with its warding greatsword.

### Warding Greatsword
Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d10 + 3) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) force damage.

### Hold Person (Recharge 5–6)
The Hierarch targets one creature it can see within 30 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns.

### Ritual Burst
The Hierarch emits a burst of warding light in a 15-foot radius. Creatures in the area must make a DC 14 Constitution save or take 10 (3d6) radiant damage and have disadvantage on the next attack roll they make before the end of their next turn.

## Reactions

### Counterward
When a creature within 30 feet casts a spell that targets the Hierarch or the Bulwark Eternal, the Hierarch can use its reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll or DC. The spell is not negated, but the target takes 3 (1d6) psychic damage.
