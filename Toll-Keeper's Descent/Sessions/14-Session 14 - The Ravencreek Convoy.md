# Session 14: The Ravencreek Convoy

## Overview

The party intercepts a convoy carrying the Worldreaver through Ravencreek Canyon. The escort is larger and more organized than earlier cult raids, indicating the cult is now actively moving relics under formal cover.

## Objective

Steal or secure the Worldreaver from a protected convoy and keep it from being taken deeper into Blackford's administration.

## Encounter: Defile Ambush

### Threats
- 4 Cult Hands in dark convoy armor
- 2 Whisperer escorts with ranged magic
- 1 wagon bound in warding chains containing the Worldreaver

### Environment
- narrow canyon road
- sheer walls and overhangs
- shifting shadows
- a triggerable rockslide trap

### Best Tactics
- Isolation of the wagon by attacking the escort
- Use terrain for cover and a mobile choke point
- Allow the barbarian to make the first real claim on the weapon

## Boss: The Whisperer Escort

### Stat Block
- [Whisperer Courier](../StatBlocks/02-Whisperer%20Courier.md)

### Behavior
The Whisperer fights to keep the party from reaching the weapon and will attempt to trigger a magical blast if the wagon is threatened.

## Clues Found

- A manifest note naming “Blackford Ledger House” as the final destination
- An index listing the Worldreaver alongside other relics under the custody code “Master's Ledger”

## Artifact Recovery

The party claims the Worldreaver and now has three of the known keys in their possession.

## End of Session Hook

The captured courier reveals the location of the Blackford tower and the phrase that the “Master receives the key” as soon as it is logged. The cult is prepared to defend the city itself.

## Rewards
- 175 XP
- The Worldreaver recovered
- Strong evidence of a larger governing force
