# Session 14: The Ravencreek Convoy

## Overview

The cult moves the Worldreaver through the mountainous pass known as Ravencreek. A dead-end canyon makes it easy to ambush—but also easier for cultic magic to trap the party.

## Setup

The party intercepts an escort convoy with two wagons, a lead cart, and several disguised escort riders. The wagons are dark and forbidding, wrapped with black cloth and embossed with a branch mark.

## Operation: The Defile Ambush

### Environment

- canyon walls
- narrow road with poor footing
- shifting shadows
- rocks that can be triggered by a failed check

### Threats

- 4 Cult Hands on foot and horseback
- 2 Whisperer escorts using ranged incantations
- 1 warded wagon containing the Worldreaver

### Objective

Secure the Worldreaver and escape before the cult can trigger a magical blast.

### Tactical Events

- Round 1: the lead escort notices the party and begins the false alarm ritual.
- Round 2: cultists try to surround the wagon and keep the barbarian away.
- Round 3+: the party can cut the wagon free or use terrain to close in on the weapon.

## Boss: The Whisperer Escort

### Stat Block

Use the file: "StatBlocks/Whisperer Courier.md"

### Behavior

- He attempts to use illusions to split the party.
- He attacks from range and calls for the wagon to roll away if pressured.
- If reduced below half HP, he gives a final warning: *“The Ledger is already counting your names.”*

## Artifact Recovery

The party takes the Worldreaver. It is hot, heavy, and responsive to the barbarian's presence.

### Clues Found

- A sealed diplomatic note describing the terms of an “asset transfer” to a black tower agent in Blackford.
- A rune marked with the phrase: “Master receives the key, the city records the debt.”

## Complication

If the party overuses force or damages the wagon, a magical ignition trap triggers. It causes 4d10 force damage in a 30-foot radius and may split the route.

## End of Session

The party has the Worldreaver and a direct route to Blackford. The cult's pressure intensifies. The city itself is now likely to be under active surveillance by agents of the Ledger.

## XP

- 175 XP for convoy encounter and artifact recovery
