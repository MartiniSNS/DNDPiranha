# Session 19: The Crimson Marshes

## Overview

The Crimson Marshes are a dead, flooded route point used by the cult to anchor planar traffic. The party finds a place where the world is thin, silent, and under the sway of the Ledger's will.

## Objective

Seal the toll anchor at the center of the marsh and break the route before it admits a larger force into Alderwyn.

## Encounter 1: The Red Muck

### Threats
- bog beasts and cult-shape changes
- hidden pitfalls and poison currents

### Mechanics
- DC 13 Survival or Athletics to cross safely
- failed save may impose difficult terrain or poison

## Encounter 2: The Tollstone

### Boss
- [Erinyes of the Ledger](../StatBlocks/05-Erinyes%20of%20the%20Ledger.md)

### Behavior
The demon is not trying to kill the party quickly—it is trying to hold the route open long enough to force the PCs to retreat.

## Clue Discovery

A hidden ledger reveals the phrase “First Vault” and a note naming a city beyond the veil: the City of Echoes.

## End of Session Hook

The party realizes the next route is not local and not merely a cult problem. The whole network is deliberately built to keep the Ledger alive.

## Rewards
- 200 XP
- The network is degraded, and the stakes become global
