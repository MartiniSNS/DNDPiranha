# Session 19: The Crimson Marshes

## Overview

The Crimson Marshes are a black-water mire where the dead and the living both hesitate before crossing. The cult has hidden a toll anchoring station here, allowing planar traffic to slip through to the broader network.

## Setup

The party enters a marsh at night. Everything breathes. The air is thick with coppery fog, and the trees are hung with black ribbons and old iron nails.

## Encounter 1: The Red Muck

### Threats

- 2 bog beasts or cult-altered creatures
- 1 spectral ferryman

### Mechanics

- Moving through the marsh requires Survival or Athletics checks.
- A failed check can cause 1d10 poison or 1d8 psychic damage and hamper movement.

## Encounter 2: The Tollstone

At the center of the marsh stands the red-stone toll anchor, wrapped in chains and heavy iron wards.

### Boss

Use the file: "StatBlocks/Erinyes of the Ledger.md"

### Behavior

- The blood-red tollstone resonates with the world-pain of creatures crossing the manipulation point.
- The boss uses fear, entrapment, and forced movement to keep the party from sealing the site.

## Clues

The party discovers a cartographic note that identifies the “First Vault” as a location in a literal void beyond the outer planes.

## End of Session

The party seals the site and disables the marsh's route. The network becomes less stable, but more dangerous.

## XP

- 200 XP for the marsh ordeal and boss fight
