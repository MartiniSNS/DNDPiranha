# Session 21: The Ledger Absolute

## Overview

The culmination of the arc changes the frame of the campaign. The party now stands at the threshold of the First Vault and must decide whether to break the Ledger or become part of its accounting.

## Setup

The party reaches the First Vault at the edge of the Planar Network. The structure is immense and silent. Every wall is carved in repeating ledger script, and the air tastes of ash and copper.

## Encounter 1: The Contract of Record

The party must pass through a final hall of sealed contracts. Each contract is tied to a different person, and the party is given a chance to sign or reject it.

### Mechanics

- Each PC makes a DC 16 Charisma or Wisdom save
- On a failed save, a false debt is imposed on them and they are marked by the Ledger
- Casting major magic here invokes a stronger reaction from the vault's defenders

## Encounter 2: The Ledger Absolute

The real antagonist materializes not as a single body, but as a vast archive of living debts layered into a single entity.

### Boss Stat Block

Use the file: "StatBlocks/Ledger Absolute.md"

### Combat Conditions

- The chamber is surrounded by living pages and rotating warding pillars.
- If the party destroys the central registry, the room destabilizes.
- A PC can use the Palantier, Bulwark, or Worldreaver to destabilize the chamber if they choose to sacrifice an action.

### Objective

Defeat the registry and deny the Ledger the authority to write new entries in the material world.

## Final Choice

The party can do one of the following:

- Break the registry and sever the connection to the Ledger entirely.
- Take one of the keys and attempt to become a future steward of it.
- Destroy the artifacts and leave the world without the means of sealing the network.

## Ending Beat

If the party succeeds, the Ledger Absolute recoils, the vault collapses into silence, and the world breathes again. If they fail, the party is marked and the campaign becomes a grim pursuit into the wider Planar Network.

## XP

- 250 XP for final confrontation and conclusion of the arc

## Next Arc Hooks

- The remaining artifacts are not gone—they are scattered and hidden.
- Some of the party carry marks from the Ledger.
- A new route opens to the wider Planar Network, and the campaign now moves to a larger cosmic scale.
