# Session 21: The Ledger Absolute

## Overview

This is the climax of Act II. The party reaches the First Vault, the origin of the Ledger's network, and confronts the force that has been manipulating cults, tollhouses, and artifacts throughout the campaign.

## Related Files

- [Act II Overview](../00-Overview.md)

- [Stat Block: Ledger Absolute](../StatBlocks/08-Ledger%20Absolute.md)

## Objective

Break the vault's center and decide whether the party will destroy the Ledger's account system, submit to it, or use the artifacts to form a new order.

## Encounter 1: The Contract of Record

The chamber is lined with living contracts and moving records. Each monster or hazard is the embodiment of a debt and a vow.

### Mechanics
- each PC must make a DC 16 Charisma or Wisdom save
- a failed save imposes a debt mark and grants the Ledger an advantage in the final battle

## Boss: The Ledger Absolute

### Stat Block
- [Ledger Absolute](../StatBlocks/08-Ledger%20Absolute.md)

### Behavior
The Ledger Absolute manifests as a vast, layered being of paper, chains, and recorded accounts. It speaks in the voice of previous NPCs and in the inner voices of the party's choices.

## Final Choice

The party can:
- destroy the registry and sever the network
- claim one of the keys and become a new steward of the system
- reject the whole model and risk the wider network continuing in other realms

## End of Session

If the party wins, the attack on the vault leaves the world physically improved—but the network is not gone. The final scene should end with a hard reset: the threat remains beyond Alderwyn, and the party has marked themselves in the process.

## Rewards
- 250 XP
- Final conclusion to Act II
- Clear hook for future arcs beyond Alderwyn
