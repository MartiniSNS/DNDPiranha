# Session 15: The Blackford Tower

## Overview

The city of Blackford is now the center of the cult's shadow policy. The party enters under a veil of suspicion and confronts the first true signal that the cult is working as an administrative arm of something larger.

## Setup

The party reaches the black tower at dusk. A broad, sealed stairway leads into a tower of polished black stone. It is heavily guarded but appears to be a legal and administrative building rather than a fortress. That is the point: the cult hides in plain sight.

## Encounter 1: The Gatehouse Inquiry

### Threats

- 2 Cult Hands disguised as magistrate sergeants
- 1 Initiate posing as an archivist

### Objective

The party must pass the first gate without triggering a citywide lockdown.

### Test

- Persuasion or Deception check DC 15
- Or a direct fight with the sergeants
- Success reveals a side route to the lower archive level

## Encounter 2: The Lower Archive

Inside the black tower is a lower archive with bound ledgers, coded manifests, and courier seals.

### Discoveries

- A ledger index listing the names of every shipment connected to Blackford's toll houses
- Null entries labeled “disappeared” or “severed debt”
- A final page showing a serial list of “toll accounts” tied to specific artifacts

### Clue Significance

The party learns the wording: “The Ledger records every debt. The Master settles the balance.”

## Boss: The Toll-Keeper

The party reaches the upper ledger chamber to confront the Toll-Keeper himself.

### Stat Block

Use the file: "StatBlocks/Toll-Keeper.md"

### Behavior

- He uses counterspell and illusion to exert control over the room.
- He underestimates the party and tries to pin them in a legalistic duel.
- He calls the room a “public hearing” and demands their artifacts be surrendered in exchange for safe passage.

### Final Beat

If the party defeats him, he produces a final whisper: *“The Ledger already knows your names. It will be kinder than I was.”*

## Clue Recovery

The party retrieves a sealed courier fragment from the tower's ledger vault. It includes a route to Sunspire's Lower Portal and the phrase:

*“The Ledger Absolute watches the tolls. The keys will be counted in the Seventh Vault.”*

## End of Session

The party leaves with knowledge of a larger force behind the cult. The cult is wounded but not broken.

## XP

- 200 XP for the Blackford tower siege and boss fight
