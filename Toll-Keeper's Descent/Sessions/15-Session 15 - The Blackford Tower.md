# Session 15: The Blackford Tower

## Overview

The city of Blackford becomes the active field of battle. The party must penetrate the black tower, a magic-warded administrative fortress that functions as the cult’s center of legal authority and transport control.

## Objective

Reach the upper ledger chamber and confront the Toll-Keeper before he consolidates the artifacts and executes his ransom threat.

## Encounter 1: The Gatehouse Inquiry

### Threats
- 2 Cult Hands in magistrate colors
- 1 Initiate clerk using forged authority seals

### Objective
Break through the tower's front gate without notifying the city's military or triggering lockdown.

## Encounter 2: The Lower Archive

The lower archive is dense with transaction records, route maps, sealed ledgers, and hidden administrative seals. The party can search, steal, or confront, but the archive is a dangerous web of legal traps and warded paperwork.

### Discovery
The party finds a page that includes the phrase:

> “The Ledger records every debt. The Master settles the balance.”

## Boss: The Toll-Keeper

### Stat Block
- [Toll-Keeper of the Ledger](../StatBlocks/03-Toll-Keeper.md)

### Behavior
He controls the chamber with counterspell rituals, financial leverage, and threats tied to Ciaran's life. He is not a brute, but a bureaucratic tyrant with a strong magical foundation.

## Clue Rewards
- [Blackford Ransom Note](../Clues%20and%20Handouts/03-Blackford%20Ransom%20Note.md)
- A route note mentioning a hidden portal beneath the Sunspire Citadel

## End of Session Hook

The Toll-Keeper utters a final warning and the party now realizes that there is something not just behind him, but beyond him: the Ledger Absolute.

## Rewards
