# Handout: Lord Ciaran's Last Message

> “If you are reading this, then the Master has begun to count us. Return to Blackford. The tollhouse is more than a building—it is a ledger of debts. The cult is not the end of the chain. There is something older than the Branch and more methodical than fear. Do not trust any contract that binds a soul without a witness.”

This message is either discovered in Ciaran's cell, hidden in a ledger drawer, or delivered by a surviving contact. It is written with the urgency of someone who knows the cult is setting a trap and has only a brief time left to act.

## Use in play

This becomes the emotional hinge of the black tower confrontation and gives the party a reason to directly challenge the Toll-Keeper.
