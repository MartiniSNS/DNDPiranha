# Clue: Map of the Planar Network

> A scrap of vellum reveals a route across a series of sites: Sunspire, Blackford, Twilight Crossroads, Crimson Marshes, City of Echoes, and a final unnamed vault beyond the Planar Network.

This map is discovered in the archive beneath the Sunspire Portal or inside the City of Echoes. It reveals that the party has only touched the edges of a system of passageways that extend beyond the world itself.

## What it implies

- There are still more toll points and vaults.
- The party is not merely dealing with a local cult—they are scouting a cosmic network.
- The final confrontation is not the end of the story; it is the beginning of a broader campaign arc.

## Investigation DC

- DC 14 Investigation to identify the route as a network of something ancient and artificial.
- DC 16 Arcana to classify the route as not a typical planar door but an engineered debt structure.

## Use in play

This clue provides the foundation for future arcs and makes the party's artifacts matter far beyond Alderwyn.
