# Clue: Map of the Planar Network

> A scrap of vellum reveals routes connecting Sunspire, Blackford, the Twilight Crossroads, the Crimson Marshes, the City of Echoes, and a final unnamed vault beyond the Planar Network.

This map is discovered in the Sunspire Portal or the City of Echoes. It provides the larger geography of the enemy network and signals the scope of the next arcs.

## Related Session
- [Session 17: The Sunspire Portal](../Sessions/17-Session%2017%20-%20The%20Sunspire%20Portal.md)

## Use in play
This clue gives the party a route map for future arcs and makes the artifacts matter beyond Alderwyn.
