# Clue: Blackford Ransom Note

> “Deliver the Palantier and the Bulwark to the Blackford Ledger House before sunset. If refusal occurs, the town will see the Master’s counting. The life of Lord Ciaran shall be entered as settled debt.”

This note is recovered from the black tower or delivered during the confrontation with the Toll-Keeper. It confirms that the cult expects the party to respond to the Master’s authority and that their friend is being used as leverage.

## What it implies

- The cult values legal language over direct violence.
- The party is being treated like assets in a ledger system.
- The Toll-Keeper uses debt and public shame as tactical weapons.

## Insight DC

- DC 13 Insight to notice the note is trying to pressure the party into acting emotionally rather than strategically.

## Use in play

This note pushes the party into direct action and makes the black tower conflict a moral imperative.
