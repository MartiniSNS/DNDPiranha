# Clue: Blackford Ransom Note

> “Deliver the Palantier and the Bulwark to the Blackford Ledger House before sunset. If refusal occurs, the town will see the Master’s counting. The life of Lord Ciaran shall be entered as settled debt.”

This note is recovered from the black tower during the confrontation with the Toll-Keeper. It confirms the cult is trying to manipulate the party emotionally while also asserting legal authority over life and death.
