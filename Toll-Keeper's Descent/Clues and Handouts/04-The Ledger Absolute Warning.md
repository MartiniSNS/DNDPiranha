# Clue: The Ledger Absolute Warning

> “The Ledger sees every debt. The Master keeps the books. The Seven Keys will be counted. Those who refuse claim their souls as collateral.”

This warning is delivered by the Ledger Scribe after the party reaches the Sunspire Portal or the lower archive. It is the first direct indication that the enemy is not merely a cult but an ancient, wider system.
