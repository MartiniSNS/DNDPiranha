# Clue: The Ledger Absolute Warning

> “The Ledger sees every debt. The Master keeps the books. The Seven Keys will be counted. Those who refuse claim their souls as collateral.”

This revelation is delivered telepathically by the Ledger Scribe after the party reaches the Sunspire Portal or the archives. It is one of the first direct pieces of evidence that the cult's power is being managed by an ancient entity beyond the world.

## What it implies

- The party is not confronting a person but a system.
- The cult is a mechanism for organizing, not for creation.
- There are seven keys, and the party has already found three.

## Arcana / Religion DC

- DC 15 Arcana to recognize the imagery as a cosmological structure rather than cultic superstition.
- DC 14 Religion to understand the phrase “Seven Keys” as a ritual or magical sequence.

## Use in play

This clue escalates the arc from local cult conflict into a wider inter-planar threat.
