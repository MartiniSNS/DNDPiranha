# Clue: Sunspire Transfer Order

> “Sunspire Ledger Vault: Asset Category: Sealed Relic // Designation: The Bulwark Eternal // Custody: Warded by House of Seals // Forwarding Authority: Blackford Master Registry // Next Route: Road to the Lower Portal // Compliance Term: Record all debt before crossing.”

This transfer order is discovered in the Sunspire Citadel after the party defeats the Seal Keeper Hierarch. It confirms that the artifacts are transported under a written legal framework meant to hide their powers and maintain control over the cult's chain of custody.

## What it implies

- The Bulwark is not just lost—it is being moved intentionally.
- The cult is preparing to shift the artifacts through a hidden route.
- A convoy is scheduled through Ravencreek Canyon to deliver the Worldreaver under similar conditions.

## Investigation DC

- DC 13 Investigation to see the list includes more than one relic.
- DC 15 Religion to recognize the phrase “House of Seals” as a long-dead oath network.

## Use in play

This clue leads to the Ravencreek convoy ambush and the recovery of the Worldreaver.
