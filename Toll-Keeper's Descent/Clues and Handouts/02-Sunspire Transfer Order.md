# Clue: Sunspire Transfer Order

> “Sunspire Ledger Vault: Asset Category: Sealed Relic // Designation: The Bulwark Eternal // Custody: Warded by House of Seals // Forwarding Authority: Blackford Master Registry // Next Route: Road to the Lower Portal // Compliance Term: Record all debt before crossing.”

This transfer order is discovered in the Sunspire Citadel after the party defeats the Seal Keeper Hierarch. It confirms that the relics are being moved under a legal framework meant to secure cult control.
