# Clue: Ledger Fragment from the Blackford Archive

> “The Master receives the key. The city records the debt. The ledger is not a ledger, but a law: the toll is an oath, and every oath is a chain.”

This note is recovered from a hidden archive beneath the Blackford Tollhouse. It connects the cult's local structure to a wider metaphysical bureaucracy. It includes the phrase “The Ledger Absolute.”

## What it implies
- the cult is a mechanism, not just a gang
- the tollhouse is both legal and metaphysical infrastructure
- the “Master” is a conduit, not a final authority
