# Clue: Ledger Fragment from the Blackford Archive

> “The Master receives the key. The city records the debt. The ledger is not a ledger, but a law: the toll is an oath, and every oath is a chain.”

This note is recovered from a hidden archive beneath the Blackford Tollhouse. It connects the cult's local structure to a broader spiritual bureaucracy. It is written in the same hand as the earlier cult correspondence and includes a phrase referencing a larger force: “The Ledger Absolute.”

## What it implies

- The cult operates as a system, not just a gang.
- The city’s tollhouse is a legal and metaphysical instrument.
- The “Master” is not a local authority but a conduit.

## Investigation DC

- DC 12 Investigation to see the hand is consistent with known cult records.
- DC 15 Arcana to recognize the use of “ledger law” as a metaphysical pattern.

## Use in play

This clue leads the PCs to Sunspire and the hidden portal routes beneath the ancient citadel.
