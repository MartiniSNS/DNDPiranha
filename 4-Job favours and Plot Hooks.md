[[1-Maela Fern - Barmaid of The Whispering Oak]]

- Small errands: fetch a lost crate from [[Brambleford]], keep watch at [[Ashgrove]], or speak privately with a nervous steward from [[Thornwell]]. These often become leads into larger threads.
