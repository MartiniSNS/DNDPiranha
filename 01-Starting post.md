
Gather close, bold souls, your tale begins in Alderwyn, a kingdom of rolling hills, mist‑laden forests, and the slow, patient weight of ancient secrets. Here magic is not common coin but rumour and relic: reclusive wizards and the ruins of vanished ages whisper of powers long slept. People till the fertile land, sing the old songs, and keep their doors bolted against the dark. But beneath the music and hospitality, something stirs, whispers of kidnappings, forbidden rites, and shadows moving behind noble masks.

You have all been drawn to Greenhollow for your own reasons: coin, curiosity, a favour, or the promise of a story worth telling and the town’s sunlit square is where your paths cross. At its heart stands a great, weathered oak, older than any told memory; wrapped in its gnarled roots is The Whispering Oak, a tavern of lanternlight and stew where travellers trade gossip and secrets. Listen there: the tree’s alcoves keep confidences, the stage lets loose tongues, and every loose remark might be the first stitch you need to unravel the wider weave.

On the market lips are two dangerous names. Some speak of a hidden cabal bent on restoring magic to Alderwyn “by any means necessary”; some even whisper that wielding that very magic, dangerous and unpredictable as it may, could be the only way to stop them. Others point toward a colder threat, the Shadowed Branch, a cult that bends nobles through blackmail and hides behind coded correspondence. Whether these are one and the same, or rival hands tugging different strings, the threads lead into the woods and up to halls of power.

This is your beginning: choose a reason you came to Greenhollow, pick a nook in The Whispering Oak, and listen. Follow a whisper and you may kindle a blaze that changes Alderwyn or learn how deep its roots of secrecy truly run.
