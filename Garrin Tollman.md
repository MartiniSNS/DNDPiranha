Tollhouse Manager — Garrin Tollman
Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral
AC 12 (sturdy travel coat)  
HP 18 (4d8)  8, 24, 32
Speed 30 ft.

STR 10 (+0) | DEX 12 (+1) | CON 12 (+1) | INT 13 (+1) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 12 (+1)

Saving Throws — Wis +4  
Skills — Insight +4, Investigation +3, Perception +4, Persuasion +3, Sleight of Hand +3 (pocketing receipts)  
Tools — Calligrapher’s supplies +4 (ledger knowledge)  
Senses — passive Perception 14  
Languages — Common  
Challenge — 1/4 (50 XP)

Traits
- Ledgerkeeper’s Memory. Garrin has advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks and Wisdom (Insight) checks related to accounts, dates, handwriting, and the contents or provenance of papers he personally handled in the last 6 months.
- Mind for Routes. Garrin can recall and give accurate descriptions of common courier routes, toll times, and regular merchants who pass the bridge. Treat information as reliable unless the route was changed recently.
- Wary of Trouble. Garrin has advantage on Wisdom saving throws to resist being frightened while inside the tollhouse.

Reactions
- Ring the Bell (1/round). When Garrin is attacked, sees a fight start in the immediate area, or suspects an imminent threat, he can ring a heavy iron bell. Creatures of the attacker’s side within 30 feet who can hear the bell must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of their next turn (townsfolk and sudden commotion distract them). The bell also alerts nearby guards or townsfolk — the DM adjudicates response time (typical: one nearby guard in 1d4 rounds; more if Garrin has allies in the area).

Actions
- Quarterstaff. Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d6 bludgeoning damage (1d8 versatile) — Garrin fights to defend the tollhouse but prefers deterrence over killing.
- Throw Ink Jar (improvised ranged). Ranged Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, range 10/30 ft., one target. Hit: 1d4 bludgeoning damage and the target is covered in ink and soot (granted disadvantage on Perception checks that rely on sight for 1 round and makes a loud mess; useful to disrupt a would-be looter or courier).

Equipment
- Ledger chest (locked, sturdy; chest lock DC 15 to pick), heavy bell, quarterstaff, ink jars, quills, small pouch (10 gp typical on hand), several envelopes with receipts.

Roleplaying & Use Notes (DM)
- Garrin is a careful, anxious man who takes pride in the books he keeps. He’s more inclined to bargain, persuade, or be bribed than to draw steel. He will defend the ledger if he thinks the danger threatens the bridge’s safety or his household.
- Default demeanor: courteous, nervous, evasive about anything that smacks of politics. He answers mundane questions about toll rates and schedules freely.
- If under pressure (bribed, threatened, or promised protection), Garrin will cooperate about recent deliveries and who collected payments — but he will protect identities above his clearance unless convinced otherwise.

Information availability & Suggested DCs
- Basic facts (who collects tolls, regular merchants, typical delivery times): no check required; Garrin volunteers these.
- Recent deliveries and who picked them up (descriptions, time of day): DC 12 Persuasion or DC 12 Insight to detect evasions. If Garrin is willing (bribe of 5 gp or favor), he tells specifics (names/codenames, e.g., “a man called Rook”).
- Whether the ledger was moved, hidden, or falsified: Investigation DC 13 to discover a false bottom or tampering; Garrin will admit to hiding pages only under Persuasion DC 14 or if shown proof (ledger scrap, seal).
- Detecting Garrin lying/being blackmailed: Insight DC 14 to sense he is withholding a name; a successful check suggests he fears someone and may be blackmailed. A direct Threat/Intimidation DC 14 will usually force a name (if he can give it without endangering family).

Variant: Compromised Tollkeeper
- Replace Persuasion +3 with +4 and add a secret trait “Marked for Silence”: Garrin will lie to protect the Branch if his family is threatened. He may give false directions or claim the ledger was burned. A DC 15 Insight or a successful Charm/Zone of Truth makes him unwilling to lie; otherwise he may risk misleading the party.

Tactical suggestions
- Garrin is rarely a front-line combatant. Use him as a social obstacle, reluctant informant, or the keeper of the ledger chest. If combat begins, he rings the bell and attempts to stall attackers with improvised throws and his staff until guards arrive.
- If the party exposes the Branch’s pressure on Garrin (e.g., by producing a captured Hand who confesses), he becomes an ally and can provide useful follow-ups: names, times, and a safe place to hide a recovered ledger page.

Sample lines
- Neutral/cordial: “Tolls keep the bridge and my family fed. Ask me about rates, hours, and who passed through — I’ll tell you what I can.”
- Nervous/evading: “I… I answer for the bridge, not for other folk’s business. I don’t know what you’re implying.”
- When cooperative (bribed/protected): “Fine. There’s been a pale assistant for dusk deliveries—he leaves a small packet under the ledger chest’s false bottom. Don’t make a scene, and you can have a look.”

DM cues for investigation
- If players search the tollhouse: a faint smear of sap on a ledger corner or the floorboards (Investigation DC 12) ties the site to Branch tradecraft.
- The ledger chest’s false bottom (Investigation DC 13) can hide pages marked with the twisted‑branch impression; removing those pages without alerting Garrin or triggering a bell requires Sleight of Hand DC 13 or Thieves’ Tools DC 14.

Use Garrin to create social tension: he’s a line-of-information who can become an ally, mislead the party under duress, or spark a chase if the cult moves to recover missing ledger pages.
