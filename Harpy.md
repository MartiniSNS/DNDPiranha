

- **AC** 13
- **HP** 22 (5d8)
- **Speed** 20 ft., fly 40 ft.
- **Abilities:** STR 13, DEX 16, CON 12, INT 8, WIS 13, CHA 8
- **Skills:** Perception +4
## Actions

**Multiattack**: The harpy makes two attacks: one with its claws and one with its club.  
  
**Claws**: Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing damage.  
  
**Club**: Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 3 (1d4 + 1) bludgeoning damage.  
  
**Luring Song**: The harpy sings a magical melody. Every [humanoid and giant](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Monsters?expansion=34047#toc_3) within 300 ft. of the harpy that can hear the song must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom saving throw or be [charmed](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_2) until the song ends. The harpy must take a bonus action on its subsequent turns to continue singing. It can stop singing at any time. The song ends if the harpy is [incapacitated](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Conditions?expansion=34047#toc_6).  
While charmed by the harpy, a target is incapacitated and ignores the songs of other harpies. If the charmed target is more than 5 ft. away from the harpy, the must move on its turn toward the harpy by the most direct route. It doesn't avoid [opportunity attacks](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Rules:Combat?expansion=34047#toc_40), but before moving into damaging terrain, such as lava or a pit, and whenever it takes damage from a source other than the harpy, a target can repeat the saving throw. A creature can also repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns. If a creature's saving throw is successful, the effect ends on it.  
A target that successfully saves is immune to this harpy's song for the next 24 hours.


## Tactics

- **Round 1:** The harpy shrieks and dives at the nearer adventurer, talons flashing. It uses the furniture for cover—overturned cabinets provide half-cover; the harpy swoops between them.
- **Round 2+:** If bloodied, the harpy takes flight, circling and throwing debris (rocks, bones—make Dex saves DC 13 to avoid). It attempts to provoke melee combatants with taunts:
    - _"SHINY! SHINY! YOU HAVE SHINY!"_ (if someone carries obvious treasure)
    - _"YOU SMELL OF FEAR AND COIN!"_
- **Retreat:** If reduced below 5 HP, the harpy attempts to flee through a broken skylight or boarded window (giving the party one last Opportunity Attack). If captured alive, it speaks in broken, bird-like screeches and reveals nothing of value until threatened or fed.
