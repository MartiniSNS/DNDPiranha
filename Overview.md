Carrying on from [[Session 2]]

Session 3 — After the Tollhouse Fight DM voice: you pick up the story with the dust still settling on splintered floorboards, the air thick with smoke and the metallic tang of spilled coin. The last cultists lie still (or slumped), and Garrin Tollman—tollkeeper of Blackford—breathes shallow, or he stares from the floor with glassy eyes. The clock is ticking: reinforcements, the manor’s reaction, and the ledger’s fate all move while you decide what to do.

Primary goals for this session

- Secure the scene and determine [[Garrin Tollman]] status (alive / complicit / dead).
- Recover and examine ledger pages and any physical evidence.
- Decide whether to hold Garrin as prisoner, turn him in, or use him as an informant.
- Follow the trail the evidence points to (Thornwell steward, Archways drop, or Blackford manor vault).
- Manage [[The Shadowed Branch]] response (escape, defend, or face reinforcements).

Set the stakes early: the cult will notice missing operatives or ledger pages within 10–30 minutes unless you act to conceal the scene. Public spellcasting and loud fighting have already damaged your standing in nearby settlements.
