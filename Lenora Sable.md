Owner of [[The Gilded Ledger]]

- Woman in her late 40s, precise, warm when she needs you to feel safe. Reputation: an efficient host and keeper of city gossip.
    - True role options (choose for your table):
        1. Complicit: Lenora is a low‑level Whisperer asset — runs the front and keeps the ledger vault for the Branch.
        2. Coerced: Lenora was blackmailed and hides Branch goods under duress; hates what she’s made to do.
        3. Unwitting front: Lenora thinks she stores merchant ledgers. A Branch agent uses her cellar without her knowledge.
    - In‑play hooks: Persuasion DC 13 to convince her to trust you; Insight DC 13 to sense coercion; she can be bribed or won over with a favor (50 gp or a service).
