## Scene Overview
The adventure begins in [[The Whispering Oak]], a cosy establishment in the heart of [[Greenhollow]]. Each player has received a mysterious letter inviting them to meet here today. The sender is unknown, but the promise of opportunity (or adventure) was enough to bring them.

## Arrival Sequence
- **Players arrive separately, at different times.**  
  Each character can describe how they enter, what they look like, and their first impression of the tavern and its patrons.
- **Atmosphere:**  
  The tavern is lively but not crowded. There’s a roaring fireplace, the scent of stew, and a few locals playing cards or dice.
- **NPCs Present:**  
  - The tavernkeeper - [[1-Maela Fern - Barmaid of The Whispering Oak]]: Warm, talkative, knows most folks in town.
  - A couple of regulars: Friendly or suspicious, as suits your mood.
  - (Optional) The wandering merchant, quietly sipping ale in the corner.

## The Letter - Given by [[Lord Ciaran Ashvale]]
- Each player received a similar letter, written in a careful hand:
    > “Opportunity awaits you at the [[The Whispering Oak]] in [[Greenhollow]]. Meet me at dusk. Tell no one. C.A.”

## Roleplay Prompts
- How does your character feel about the letter?
- Did they tell anyone, or come alone?
- What do they do upon arriving, sit, order food, watch others, ask questions?

## Hook
- As each player arrives and settles, the mysterious contact (an NPC, perhaps cloaked and reserved) eventually appears to address the group, revealing the first thread of the cult’s plot.

## DM Tips
- Let each player have a brief spotlight as they enter.
- Encourage interactions—between PCs, with NPCs, and with the tavern environment.
- Drop subtle hints about odd happenings in town or rumours overheard.
