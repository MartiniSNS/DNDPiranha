[[Haldric Ironhand]]
- Quick sharpen / tune: 1 sp, 10–30 minutes. (Edges, bows tuned)
- Minor repairs (buckles, straps, edge rework): 1–3 gp, same day.
- Major repairs (straighten bent blade, mend a cracked shield): 3–8 gp, 1–3 days depending on severity.
- New mundane weapon/armor (commission): material cost (roughly half market price) + 2–5 gp labour; 3–14 days depending on complexity.
- Custom mundane modifications (hidden compartment, weighted pommel, sling stud, bayonet fitting): 5–25 gp + possible rare material; 3–7 days.
- Emergency jury‑rig (short‑term): 1 gp + 10 minutes — Haldric can jury‑rig a weapon or armor to grant a one‑time mechanical benefit in the next encounter (see Stat Block for mechanical effect).
- Identification / appraisal: 1–2 sp and 10 minutes — Haldric can assess workmanship, likely origin, and approximate value; for unusual materials he may request more time.
- Discount terms: “Maela’s folk” 25% off labour or Haldric will accept a small local favour (fetch a crate, run a message) in lieu of full payment.
