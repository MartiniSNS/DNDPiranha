**Theme:** The Fifth Floor is the **Gossamer Vault**, a dim, vertical chamber of suspended walkways, translucent curtains, and glass‑like filaments that hum with stored memories. Light refracts through hanging crystal motes, casting fractured shadows. The floor emphasizes vertical movement, fragile surfaces, and choices that trade memory for power. **Tone:** eerie, elegiac, and claustrophobic — players feel watched by preserved recollections and must decide what to keep, alter, or sacrifice.

### Layout

A multi‑tiered, roughly circular space built around a central atrium. Movement is along narrow suspended walkways, rope bridges, and fragile glass platforms. Key areas are arranged radially.

- **A. Entrance Spire** — Narrow stair and a small landing with a locked gate of woven glass.
    
- **B. Filament Ring** — Circular walkway around the atrium with hanging memory filaments; several breakable glass platforms over the void.
    
- **C. Memory Spires** — Three vertical alcoves rising from the atrium floor; each contains a preserved memory orb and a minor guardian.
    
- **D. Looming Gallery** — A gallery of suspended tapestries and glass panes that refract sound; contains puzzles and a merchant‑like spirit.
    
- **E. The Glassheart** — Central chamber at the atrium base: a crystalline heart that stores the floor’s dominant memory and powers the filaments. Disabling or bargaining with it opens the stair to Floor 6.
    

**Map idea:** concentric rings with radial walkways; mark fragile tiles and rope bridges; include elevation markers for vertical hazards and grappling opportunities.

### Rooms and Encounters

#### Entrance Spire

- **Feature:** A gate of woven glass sealed by a memory lock.
    
- **Challenge:** Offer three short memory prompts (player backstory hooks or minor secrets). Each correct offering reduces the gate’s lock DC by 2; wrong offerings trigger a filament snap (Dex save DC 13 or fall 10 ft and take 1d6 bludgeoning).
    

#### Filament Ring

- **Hazard:** Hanging filaments store whispers. Touching a filament requires a **DC 13 Wisdom save** or be compelled to speak a truth aloud (roleplay) and attract nearby guardians.
    
- **Encounter:** 2–3 **Glass Wights** (CR 2) that phase through filaments and attempt to sever walkways. They are fragile but can push players into the void.
    

#### Memory Spires (three)

- **Each spire contains:** one preserved memory orb and a minor guardian (e.g., Tapestry Weaver variant or Memory Phantom).
    
- **Mechanic:** Players may attempt to extract the memory (DC 14 Arcana or Sleight of Hand). Success yields a usable memory (Echo‑Glass Vial); failure releases a Memory Phantom and fragments the orb.
    

#### Looming Gallery

- **NPC:** A bound spirit called the **Archivist Wisp** who trades memories for information or favors. It offers one of: a clue to the Glassheart sequence, a temporary buff, or a minor item — in exchange for a memory or a promise.
    
- **Puzzle:** A refractive sequence puzzle where players must align glass panes to focus light on a sigil. Each misalignment awakens a guardian tapestry.
    

#### The Glassheart

- **Core encounter:** The Glassheart is a semi‑sentient crystalline organ that defends itself by animating filaments and summoning Memory Phantoms. It can be fought, bargained with, or reprogrammed.
    
- **Mechanics:**
    
    - **Absorb Memory:** The Glassheart can absorb one Echo‑Glass Vial per round to heal 2d8 HP.
        
    - **Shatter Pulse:** Every third round it emits a pulse (Con save DC 15 or take 3d8 psychic and be stunned 1 round).
        
    - **Alternate resolution:** Offer three coherent memories to the Glassheart (via ritual or the Threaded Codex) to pacify it and open the stair to Floor 6.
        

**Suggested encounter makeup:** Glassheart (boss, CR 6–8 depending on party level) + 2–4 Glass Wights or Memory Phantoms.

### Traps and Puzzles

- **Filament Snap:** Filaments act as tripwires. Triggering one causes a section of walkway to collapse (Dex save DC 14 to grab edge or fall 20 ft, 4d6 bludgeoning).
    
- **Memory Exchange Puzzle:** A locked door requires three memories that form a coherent narrative. Players must place Echo‑Glass Vials in pedestals in the correct order (Insight DC 14 to deduce order from clues in the Looming Gallery). Wrong order spawns a Memory Phantom.
    
- **Refractive Sequence:** Align three glass panes to focus light on a sigil. Each pane rotated incorrectly increases ambient noise, awakening nearby guardians.
    
- **Glassfloor Fragility:** Certain tiles are fragile (Perception DC 13 to spot). Stepping on them without care causes them to crack; weight threshold mechanics allow single‑file crossing or careful balancing.
    

### Loot and Rewards

- **Glassheart Shard** (rare) — a sliver of the Glassheart that can be used as a spellcasting focus; once per long rest it grants a +1 bonus to one spell’s save DC or attack roll. Requires attunement.
    
- **Echo‑Glass Vials** (common) — record and replay a memory or sound; useful for puzzles and distractions.
    
- **Memory Locket** (uncommon) — stores a single minor memory; when opened it grants advantage on one social check tied to that memory.
    
- **Time‑Touched Trinket** (uncommon) — reroll one d20 once per long rest.
    
- **Coins and Curios** — 100–200 gp in assorted ancient coinage, a few ornate buttons, and a small woven tapestry fragment worth 10–30 gp.
    
- **Optional unique reward:** **Archivist’s Favor** — if the party bargains with the Archivist Wisp and fulfills a task, it grants a one‑time ritual hint or a map fragment for a later floor.
