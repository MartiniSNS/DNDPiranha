Blacksmith contact (use in [[Greenhollow]])

- Name: [[1-Haldric Ironhand]]
- Shop: The Ironroot Forge — a stout little forge on the market lane, two doors down from the baker, bell over the door and a blackened anvil visible through the window.
- Hours: Dawn till early evening; will close early for urgent work by arrangement if you mention [[1-Maela Fern - Barmaid of The Whispering Oak]].

Haldric, quick notes for the DM

- Description: Gruff, broad‑shouldered former bridge‑guard with a scarred forearm and an easy laugh for friends. Loyal to Maela (owes her a favour). Knows local roads, tollkeepers and mercs.
- Personality: Practical, blunt, good at gossip if you trade work or ale. Prefers coin but accepts favours/odd jobs for discount.

[[Alderwyn - Piranha/02-Characters/NPCs/Haldric Ironhand/2-Goods and Services|2-Goods and Services]] (suggested, tweak as you like)

- Sharpen & quick tune (weapons, tools): 1 sp, 10–30 minutes.
- Minor repairs (buckle/strap/edge repair): 1–3 gp, same day.
- Major repair (broken blade, bent bow): 3–8 gp, 1–3 days.
- New mundane weapon/armor (commission): material cost (~half market price) + 2–5 gp labour, 3–14 days depending on complexity.
- Custom non‑magical modification (hidden compartment, weighted pommel, bayonet fitting): 5–25 gp + rare material or a side favour; 3–7 days.

Discount / special terms for the party

- “Maela’s folk” discount: 25% off labour or Haldric will accept a small local favor (fetch a crate from Brambleford, deliver a message) in lieu of full pay.
- If the PCs captured cult gear or odd materials, Haldric can identify workmanship (Investigation/Insight DC 12 to glean what he knows) and may offer to alter goods for discreet transport.

Story hooks & uses

- Haldric is a source of local intelligence: he hears about tollhouse deliveries and travellers who pass the forge. He may know a tollkeeper at Blackford (tie to your ledger lead).
- He can ask the party for a favour in return (escort a wagon, recover a lost shipment) — a neat side quest that ties into Brambleford / Blackford leads.
- If PCs cultivate him, he can refer them to a fence, a merc captain, or a retired watchman with knowledge of the bridge routes.

Optional one‑off mechanical house rule (use only if you want a small reward)

- Jury‑rig: 10 minutes + 1 gp (or 25% discount if Maela sent you) — Haldric can jury‑rig a weapon or piece of armor to grant a single short benefit in the next encounter (e.g., +1 to a single attack roll or +1 AC against one attack). Use sparingly.

How to present it at the table (DM line)

- Maela slides a folded scrap of paper across the table. “Haldric Ironhand, Ironroot Forge — market lane. Tell him I sent you and he’ll sort you out.” She winks. “And when you need someone to knock a head or two straight, say you’re friends of mine.”
