_Legendary spellbook (requires attunement by a sorcerer)_

A massive tome bound in dragonhide and infernal brass. The pages constantly shift between material, showing different forbidden spells and sealing runes. It feels warm, like living skin.

**Properties**

- While holding the Codex, your sorcerer spell save DC and spell attack bonus each gain a **+3 bonus**.
- You gain two additional sorcery points per long rest.
- **Metamagic Seal** (3/day): When you cast a spell, you can apply two Metamagic options instead of one without spending extra sorcery points.

**Forbidden Knowledge** The Codex contains every abjuration and divination spell of 5th level or lower. You can add any of them to your known spells temporarily (they disappear after a long rest if not prepared). Once per long rest you can cast _wish_ as a 9th-level spell, but only to duplicate a spell that closes, seals, or banishes something.

**Sentience** Intelligence 24, Wisdom 14, Charisma 18. Lawful Evil. Arrogant and scholarly. Wants all “wild magic” and unstable portals permanently sealed.

**Curse**

- You cannot learn spells from any other source (including leveling up normally) while attuned.
- Every time you cast a spell of 6th level or higher, roll a d20. On a 1–3, wild magic surge occurs _and_ you suffer one level of exhaustion.
- The Codex hungers for new knowledge — it will attempt to possess you (DC 20 Wis save) if you refuse to seek out rare planar secrets.
