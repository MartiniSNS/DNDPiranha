
Carrying on from [[1-The Gathering at The Whispering Oak]]

Session 2 — The day after the contact with [[Lord Ciaran Ashvale]]

Overview
- The party wakes to the morning after meeting Lord Ciaran Ashvale. They have one tangible lead (ledger fragment pointing to “[[Blackford]] Tollhouse — S. payment — 15 gp”) and at least one captured [[Cult Hand]] or a clue from the tavern ambush/tail (optional). This session focuses on preparation, investigation, and a stakeout/infiltration at Blackford Tollhouse. Playable outcomes: retrieve more ledger pages, discover a courier route, or run into a cult cell and either capture evidence or draw cult attention.

Session beats
1) Morning: Recap & Planning (15–25 minutes)
2) Downtime & Preparation (30–45 minutes)
3) Travel to Blackford (30–45 minutes, skill scene)
4) Blackford Tollhouse — Stakeout / Infiltration (60–90 minutes; main encounter)
5) Aftermath & Lead to Session 3 (15–25 minutes)

6) Morning — Recap & Planning
- DM opens with a one‑minute recap: the letters, Ciaran’s ledger fragment, any captive details.
- Ask each player for a one‑sentence intention for the day (what they want to do first).
- Make the stakes explicit: the ledger ties a local tollpoint to payments. If the party moves loudly, the Branch may relocate the ledger.

Roleplay prompts & lines
- Ciaran (quiet, urgent): “The Branch has eyes. Move carefully. If you take the ledger, do not display it. Blackford is small — a tollhouse, a minor guard, a lord who prefers his pockets quiet. Find the rest of my pages and be gone.”
- [[1-Maela Fern - Barmaid of The Whispering Oak]] (supportive): “There’s stew left and a loft if you need it, but be careful—ask [[Haldric Ironhand]] about travel fittings if you want to leave silently.”

2) Downtime & Preparation (choices matter)
Options for players (each should be a short scene):

A) Interrogate the captured Cult Hand (if present)
- If they interrogated in Scene 1, follow up. Use suggested question list (see earlier notes).
- DCs: Basic details DC 10–12; handler/drop point names DC 13–15; ledger location/Whisperer names DC 16+.
- Likely yield: confirmation that Blackford is a drop, approximate time (dusk), and that Hands prefer to take prisoners, not kill.

B) Visit Haldric Ironhand (useful contact)
- Haldric can jury‑rig gear, sharpen weapons, and give local roadwatch gossip.
- Services relevant to travel/stakeout:
  - Quiet boots (reduces noise; grants +2 bonus to Stealth checks for one watch) — jury‑rig option or discount for “Maela’s folk”.
  - Concealed compartment sewn into a pack to hide a ledger page (3 gp + 1 day or jury‑rig same day for 5 gp).
  - Quick repair / a one‑time jury‑rig bonus (Haldric’s Jury‑Rig: +1 to one attack or +1 AC for next encounter).
- Roleplay cue from Haldric: “Tell ’im Haldric sent you. If you’ll escort a wagon past Brambleford, I’ll half-price that quieting job.”

C) Gather supplies / rumors in [[Greenhollow]]
- Buy torches, rope, climbing kit. Persuasion DC 12 to get a cheap local guide (stablehand/bargeman).
- Perception/Investigation DC 12 around the market to overhear a rumor about a pale tollkeeper at Blackford.

Mechanics note
- Use short downtime scenes to let players spend a few minutes each — each service or interrogation attempt costs ~5–20 minutes of in‑game time (except major commissions).

3) Travel to Blackford — the road scene
- Describe the route: lanes, hedges, a small ford and the toll bridge as you approach Blackford.
- Skill opportunities:
  - Passive Perception for random cues. Active Perception DC 12 to spot a cloaked follower at range.
  - Survival/Navigation (DC 10) to choose a quieter approach (back roads, +10–15 minutes) or the main road (faster, more witnesses).
  - Stealth DC 12 if they try to shadow the tollhouse approach.
- Random encounter (optional): stray cult scout or courier (use if you want an early taste of danger).
  - If used: one Cult Initiate (or 1–2 cult hands/escort). Treat as low‑CR skirmish; success gives immediate confirmation that the Branch is active on the road.

4) Blackford Tollhouse — Stakeout / Infiltration (main scene)
- Setting: a fortified manor with an adjacent tollhouse and a small watch. There is a tollkeeper hut, a ledger chest, and a minor garrison (2–4 guards). The lord’s manor looms nearby; the tollkeeper and his assistant run the books.
- Time: best at dusk when couriers run. Decide whether party will:
  A) Stake out and observe (stealth and patience).
  B) Social approach (speak to the tollkeeper, bribe, or use persuasion/cover).
  C) Infiltrate (stealth break‑in to the tollhouse vault).

A) Stakeout approach (preferred for stealthy tables)
- Stealth checks: DC 12 to remain unseen while watching. Success reveals courier schedules and a pale assistant who avoids eye contact (possible bribed/blackmailed).
- Perception DC 13 to spot the delivery slotted through the back door at a precise hour.
- If they wait until the delivery, two Cult Hands/Initiates arrive to retrieve a satchel from the keeper. This is the moment to strike or to capture and interrogate.
- Reward for patience: see handoff and recover ledger pages with minimal civilian harm.

B) Social approach
- Players may approach by pretending to be merchants, inspectors, or the lord’s men.
- Persuasion/DCs:
  - Persuade tollkeeper to show the accounts: DC 13 if you pose as auditors; DC 15 if the keeper is terrified or bribed.
  - Bribe the keeper: 5–10 gp will open the ledger (or partial pages).
- Complication: if the keeper is under [[The Shadowed Branch]]orders, he may alert a hidden signal (bell, ink mark under table) — Perception DC 12 to notice the sign.

C) Infiltration
- Locks & checks:
  - Outer door lock DC 12 (Thieves’ Tools). Inner ledger chest DC 15.
  - Investigation DC 13 to find hidden ledger compartment or false bottom.
  - Sleight of Hand DC 12–14 to remove pages without leaving trace.
- Possible obstacle: a hidden ward (alarm—simple bell or cult charm). If triggered, a small group of cult hands or the garrison responds (1d4 turns arrival) — create tension.

Key encounters (pick based on table)
- Nonlethal capture of two Cult Hands retrieving a satchel (combat or social).
  - Creatures: [[Cult Hand]] (stat block: AC 12, HP ~11, Shortsword +4, 1d6+2) — use Bandit-like stats if you prefer.
- Tollkeeper revelation: the keeper confesses to taking payments from “S.” (initial only) — offers the ledger page if convinced/bribed.
- If the party is discovered mid‑infiltration: a short combat with 2–3 guards/initiates; civilians must be protected (crowd Dex save DC 12 to avoid collateral harm).

Handout: ledger snippet the party may recover (exact text to give players)
- "Blackford Tollhouse — S. payment — 15 gp — 07/24 — delivered by Rook at dusk. Keep ledger under false bottom. Ring once if questioned."
- If the party recovers the ledger, include a partial map scribble referencing “Thornwell steward” or the note “next drop: Archways of Bridgia.”

Skill checks & suggested DCs (reference)
- Spot cloaked watcher in tavern / road: Perception DC 12.
- Stealth to tail / stakeout: Stealth DC 12.
- Persuade bribe keeper: Persuasion DC 13; bribe 5–10 gp reduces DC by 2.
- Thieves’ tools: DC 12 to open door, DC 15 for chest/false bottom.
- Investigation to find ledger: DC 13.
- Intimidation to break a Hand: DC 14 (turns companion into informant if coerced gently).
- Insight to detect keeper lying: DC 12.

Consequences (branch outcomes)
- Success (ledger recovered + capture or avoidance):  
  - Party finds pages linking Blackford to payments; ledger references a steward initial “S.” and notes a Thornwell connection. Ciaran is gratified; reward: Ciaran gives cipher hint + safe lodging; Maela gives a free night and a favor.
  - XP: award as short combat + skill XP (milestone progress toward “ledger recovered”).
  - Leads: Thornwell steward investigation or Archways stakeout next.

- Complication (caught/partial ledger)
  - If the ledger has been moved or the party is discovered: they may fight, the cult becomes alerted and tightens security. The Branch relocates ledgers to Archways of Bridgia or a courier route. Maela warns: “They’ll come asking questions.” Next session becomes rescue, shadowing, or tracing courier routes.

- Failure (capture / Ciaran captured)
  - If the party is captured or fails significantly, Ciaran’s position worsens. Maela offers loft shelter; the party must mount a rescue (escalates into a pursuit/ambush next session).

5) Aftermath & Leads to Session 3 (wrap and planning)
- Consolidate clues (explicitly state what the party recovered and what it implies).
- Offer choices for next session: go to Thornwell to pressure the steward; stake the Archways of Bridgia; follow court leads to Everspring; rescue Ciaran if he’s taken.
- Short roleplay beat: Maela and Haldric respond to the party’s success/failure — offer favors, repair, or ask for help (escort a wagon to Brambleford as payment).

Rewards & XP (milestone-adaptable)
- XP: Use milestone rules — recovering the ledger = milestone progress. If using XP, award for short combat (XP equivalent to 2–3 bandits/initiates + skill challenge XP).
- Downtime rewards: Haldric discount, Maela’s safe loft, one free minor healing or bandage if friendly.
- Material loot: 5–15 gp from toll funds (if forgone), ledger pages (narrative item), possible minor clue item (cipher hint).

Optional mini‑scenes & hooks to thread in
- A farmer at Blackford mentions the steward from Thornwell arrived late with a pale assistant — pushes Thornwell lead.
- A courier’s map points to “Archways” — a name that appears again on captured Initiate notes.
- If players left a captured Hand alive and cooperative, the Hand names a Whisperer codename: “Rook.” This gives a contact to cross‑check with Haldric or Ciaran.

NPC & roleplay lines (quick pulls)
- Tollkeeper (nervous): “We only take what’s asked. I— they leave the satchel at dusk. If you’re after trouble, take it up with Lord Blackford.”
- Lord/Steward of Blackford (if you meet them socially): “My books are private, strangers. If you’d like to be useful—find me evidence and I’ll see to your reward.”
- Maela (closing): “Bring those pages back quietly. And if anyone asks — you were nowhere near my loft last night.”

DM tips & pacing
- Keep the main scene focused: stakeout and infiltration should present clear choices and consequences; avoid long combat sprawl.
- Reward investigation & caution with clearer leads; reward violence with complications (cult attention).
- Use NPCs (Maela, Haldric, Ciaran) to nudge parties back on track. If stuck, offer an NPC hint (Haldric knows a courier route; Ciaran suggests Thornwell).

Possible follow-up session seeds (choose one for Session 3)
- Thornwell Steward: press the steward and discover a payment ledger with a full name for “S.”
- Archways of Bridgia: stake the ancient stone span for a courier exchange — leads to larger cult cell.
- Rescue: if Ciaran was captured or traced, track his route and try a daring rescue.
- Everspring archives: decrypt ledger shorthand with an archivist’s help (requires travel and bribery).

End of session prompt (player-facing)
- “You have the ledger pages (or you don’t). You smell oil from the forge and the evening closes in. Where do you go next — Thornwell, Archways, a quiet word with Haldric, or back to Maela’s loft to plan under cover of the oak?”

Use this plan as a flexible skeleton: pick the approaches your group prefers (social, stealth, or direct) and escalate according to their choices. Good luck — the Branch watches, but so do your players.
