[[2-Scene 1]]

Lord Ciaran’s Reveal & Roleplay (20–30 min)

Purpose: Give the party a patron, mission seed, and an initial clue.

Beat

- A cloaked figure approaches the PCs after they’re seated, it is [[Lord Ciaran Ashvale]]
- He explains briefly: he’s hunted [[The Shadowed Branch]], has evidence but needs discrete help. He gives a ledger fragment (or a coded scrap) that ties a local official to payments.

Scripted lines for Ciaran

- “My name is Ciaran Ashvale. I once served at court. I have no time for long speeches. I need capable hands to follow a ledger I could not secure myself.”
- If asked why the letters: “I could not risk a public plea. The Branch watches who asks questions. That is why I sent you, anonymous, effective.”

Give Clue A — Ledger Fragment / Coded Scrap

- Ledger fragment text (player-handout):
    - “Blackford Tollhouse — S. payment — 15 gp — 07/24” and a partial wax seal impression of the twisted branch.
- OR a coded scrap with a repeated strange mark; allow Investigation/History check (DC 14) to realize it’s bookkeeping shorthand and probably a bribe record.

Skill opportunities

- History/Investigation DC 14 to place the ledger style as coming from a minor lord’s steward.
- Insight DC 13 to notice Ciaran’s fear and know he’s credible.

Optional social complications

- If PCs loudly question Ciaran in public, Maela may cut service and show caution (public spellcasting or overt questioning risks the town watch noticing).
- If PCs ask Maela about Ciaran, she’s helpful but asks a favor: “Take a favor off my tab, fetch a crate at Brambleford later,” which seeds a side thread.
