# **THE CRIMSON WASTES: TWO-DAY JOURNEY TO THE VAULT OF WHISPERS**

## **OVERVIEW: THE ENVIRONMENT**

### **General Conditions**

**Day 1:**

- Temperature: 110-130°F (43-54°C)
- Sky: Perpetual dull red, like looking through blood
- Visibility: Good in open areas, poor near geysers (sulfur clouds)
- Ground: Obsidian shards, cracked earth, occasional patches of black sand
- Sounds: Constant wind, distant screams, groaning earth

**Day 2:**

- Temperature: Rising (120-140°F as you approach the Spire's proximity)
- Sky: Darker red, occasional lightning (red, not white)
- Visibility: Decreasing (sulfur storms moving in)
- Ground: More treacherous; volcanic glass everywhere
- Sounds: Louder; the Vault's aura begins to affect perception

### **Survival Mechanics**

**Every 4 Hours of Travel:**

- Constitution save (DC 10) to avoid exhaustion
- DC increases by 1 for each save already failed
- Short rests are possible but uncomfortable (no real shelter)
- Water is non-existent; magical means required

**During Long Rests:**

- Constitution save (DC 12) to actually gain benefit from rest
- Failure means only half hit points restored
- Nightmares are guaranteed (Wisdom save DC 14 or lose 1d4 hit points to psychological damage)

## **ENCOUNTER 1: THE SULFUR STORM (Random - Early Journey)**

### **SETUP**

The party is about 6 hours into their journey when the sky darkens unnaturally. The wind picks up, carrying with it a toxic yellow-green cloud that smells of rotten eggs and burning flesh.

**A sulfur storm approaches from the northeast.**

### **THE ENCOUNTER: Environmental Hazard & Puzzle**

**Initial Perception:**

> _The wind suddenly shifts, carrying with it a putrid stench. The sky darkens from dull red to a sickly greenish-red. In the distance, a wall of yellow-green fog rolls toward you like a living thing._
> 
> _You have minutes to decide what to do._

**The Storm's Properties:**

- Moves at 30 feet per round (faster than a running human)
- Deals **2d6 poison damage** per round to anyone inside it
- **Heavily obscures** vision (invisible beyond 5 feet)
- Reduces movement speed by half
- Corrosive: non-magical clothing and wood take damage
- Lasts 1d6 hours

### **Skill Checks Available**

**Perception DC 12:** Spot shelter (ruins, a cave, a depression in the land)

**Survival DC 14:** Navigate safely to higher ground where the storm is less intense

**Arcana DC 16:** Recognize this as a "Poison Storm" (specific magical phenomenon, can be predicted and tracked)

**Nature DC 13:** Understand the wind patterns; predict the storm will pass north of your current position if you move south

### **Puzzle: Finding Shelter**

The party has three options:

**Option A: Seek Natural Shelter (Survival DC 14)**

- There's a cave system 1 mile south
- Getting there requires navigating around geysers and volcanic glass
- **Perception DC 13** to spot the cave entrance before the storm hits
- Inside the cave: relatively safe, but there's a **Tremor Hazard** (see below)

**Option B: Create Magical Protection (Arcana DC 15)**

- Cast spells like _Mage's Magnificent Mansion_, _Leomund's Tiny Hut_, or similar
- Costs spell slots but provides guaranteed safety
- The shelter lasts as long as the storm
- **Complication:** The magical aura attracts **1d4 Lesser Devils** who investigate the anomaly (see Combat below)

**Option C: Wait It Out (Constitution Saves)**

- The party tries to endure the storm by hunkering down
- Each person makes Constitution saves (DC 14) each round
- Failure: take 2d6 poison damage
- Success: take no damage but must repeat save next round
- **Very dangerous** but possible if party is high-level

**Option D: Push Through (Survival DC 16)**

- Navigate through the storm toward the cave
- Requires constant Navigation checks
- Mistakes lead to getting lost (adds 2d6 hours to journey)
- Failure could result in taking poison damage

### **Complication: Tremor Hazard**

If the party enters the cave, they immediately feel a tremor.

**Perception DC 12** to notice that the tremor is **rhythmic** and approaching.

A **Remorhaz** (or similar burrowing creature) has been sheltering in this cave from the storm. It smells the party and investigates—**not hostile, but territorial and hungry**.

**Options:**

- **Combat:** The creature is hungry; fight or flee
- **Negotiation:** Use magic to calm it or offer food
- **Stealth:** Sneak deeper into the cave where the creature won't follow
- **Mutual Truce:** Leave the cave, let the creature have it

## **ENCOUNTER 2: THE DEVIL PATROL (Combat/Negotiation - Day 1 Afternoon)**

### **SETUP**

As the storm clears (or while navigating around it), the party spots **dust on the horizon**. Coming toward them is a formation of **Zariel's devils on patrol**.

### **THE ENCOUNTER**

**What the Party Sees:**

> _Three figures crest a ridge of obsidian stone—tall, winged, armored in infernal steel. They move with military precision, clearly searching for something._
> 
> _Or someone._

**The Patrol:**

**Leader:** 1 **Erinyes** (use Captain Serath's stats as reference, but make this one less intelligent/powerful)

- **AC 17, HP 45**
- Armed with a **+1 longsword** and **whip**
- Can cast _Fireball_ (recharge 5-6)

**Subordinates:** 2 **Bearded Devils** (Barbazu)

- **AC 15, HP 33 each**
- Armed with **glaives**
- Can cast _Dominate Person_ (recharge 5-6)

**Their Purpose:**

- Hunting for **The Courtier** (they don't know he's in the Vault)
- They're supposed to capture him alive
- They're also checking for intruders in this region of Hell

### **Interaction Options**

**Option A: Combat**

This is a **difficult combat encounter**. The Erinyes is dangerous; the Bearded Devils are formidable.

**Tactics:**

- Erinyes uses ranged attacks and spellcasting from above (flying)
- Bearded Devils form a defensive line
- They try to **capture** party members rather than kill them (interrogation is useful)

**Treasure if defeated:**

- 300 gp in infernal gold
- 5 soul coins
- Erinyes drops a **+1 longsword**
- A letter (partially burned) mentioning The Courtier and the Vault

**Complications:**

- If combat is loud, **1d4 additional devils arrive in 2d6 rounds**
- If combat lasts more than 3 rounds, an **alarm is triggered** (all devils in the region are alerted)

**Option B: Deception/Negotiation**

**Possible lies/stories:**

- "We're working for Zariel, conducting our own search"
- "We're mercenaries hired to hunt souls"
- "We're cultists transporting a sacrifice"

**Required checks:**

- **Deception DC 16** to fool the Erinyes
- **Insight DC 14** to understand what story she'll believe
- **Charisma checks** to establish credibility

**If successful:**

- They let the party pass with warnings about the area
- They provide information about The Courtier's general location
- They might actually help (if the party impresses them)

**If unsuccessful:**

- Combat begins
- Or they take the party into custody for "questioning"

**Option C: Avoid Combat**

**Stealth DC 16** to hide before they're spotted

- Hide behind rock formations or underground
- Let them pass
- Saves time and resources
- **But:** They're actively searching the area; repeated checks needed

**Option D: Surrender & Turn the Tables**

- The party surrenders without fighting
- Uses magic/words to turn the devils against each other
- Convinces one devil to side with them
- Very risky but potentially rewarding

### **Complication: Serath's Shadow**

If this goes badly or the alarm is raised, **Captain Serath herself might arrive** (hours later):

> _A figure descends from the crimson sky, wings spread wide. It's the Erinyes from the Spire—Captain Serath. She's furious._
> 
> _"You again. And causing trouble in my territory? How delightful. We have unfinished business."_

This becomes a **critical encounter**—she's much more dangerous than the patrol.

## **ENCOUNTER 3: THE CULTIST CAMP (Stealth/Exploration - Day 1 Evening)**

### **SETUP**

As the party makes camp or continues through the evening, they spot **campfires in the distance**—a cultist staging ground.

### **THE CAMP**

**Layout:**

- 4 large tents arranged in a square
- A central fire with a cooking pot
- Perimeter guards (2 cultists with torches)
- Additional cultists resting inside tents
- An altar carved from bone at the camp's center

**Personnel:**

- **8 Cultists** (use **Cult Fanatic** stat block; CR 2)
- **1 Cult Leader** (use **Priest** stat block; CR 2)
- **2 Cultist Guards** (use **Bandit Captain** stat block; CR 1/8)

**Total:** 11 enemies, but most are sleeping

### **Options**

**Option A: Avoid the Camp**

**Stealth DC 14** to slip past undetected

- Takes an extra hour of travel
- Risky; guards might spot movement

**Option B: Reconnaissance**

**Perception DC 13** to spot details:

- What they're carrying
- What they're chanting about
- Whether The Courtier is here (he's not)
- Their level of organization

**From a distance, Investigation DC 15:**

- There's a map in the leader's tent showing the Vault's location
- Supplies and food caches
- Evidence they're searching for The Courtier
- Orders from the High Matron

**Option C: Infiltration & Theft**

**Stealth DC 15** to enter the camp unseen

- Can steal the map
- Can poison supplies
- Can sabotage weapons
- Takes time (10-15 minutes minimum)

**Option D: Combat**

Fighting in a camp:

- Most cultists are sleeping (can be killed before waking)
- Guards are alert (raise alarm if combat starts)
- Reinforcements arrive if alarm is sounded
- Tent fabric provides half-cover

**Treasure:**

- 200 gp in coin and gems
- Cult supplies and weapons
- **The map** (shows the Vault's location)
- Evidence of cult operations
- Personal letters (readable for clues)

### **The Map**

If the party obtains the map, they gain:

- **Shortcut to the Vault** (saves 4 hours of travel)
- Information about other cult sites
- Evidence of the High Matron's command structure

**But:** The map shows they've found this location, which means other cultists are close. The party might be followed or intercepted.

### **Complication: The Cult Leader's Journal**

In the leader's tent, a journal reveals:

> _"The Courtier has arrived at the Vault as promised. The High Matron's orders are clear: he must not be captured alive. If heretics come seeking him, we are to trigger the ritual circle and draw something... worse. Let them face damnation itself."_

This means:

- The party is expected
- The Vault might be deliberately trapped
- A ritual is prepared to summon something powerful if they fail

## **ENCOUNTER 4: THE GEYSERS OF THE DAMNED (Puzzle/Environmental Hazard)**

### **SETUP**

The party enters a region of geothermal activity. The ground is cracked and unstable, with **hot springs and geysers** erupting randomly.

### **THE PUZZLE: Navigating the Geyser Field**

**The Area:**

- 300 feet across
- 12+ geysers scattered throughout
- Ground is cracked (unstable footing)
- **Perception DC 14** reveals patterns in the eruptions
- **Investigation DC 16** reveals safe paths

### **The Geyser Mechanics**

Each geyser erupts on a cycle (different for each one):

**Geyser A:** Erupts every 1d6 rounds (2d6 fire damage, 30-foot radius, Dex save DC 14 for half)

**Geyser B:** Erupts every 2d6 rounds (3d6 fire damage, 20-foot radius)

**Geyser C-L:** Various cycles and damage amounts

### **Crossing the Field: Three Methods**

**Method 1: Brute Force (Direct Crossing)**

- Just walk across
- **Constitution saves (DC 12)** every round
- Failure: take 2d6 fire damage from a geyser
- Takes 3 rounds to cross
- High risk, fast

**Method 2: Careful Navigation (Survival/Perception)**

- Watch the patterns
- **Perception DC 14** to understand the eruption cycles
- **Survival DC 16** to navigate safely
- Takes 4 rounds but no damage if successful
- Failure: take 1d6 damage per failed check

**Method 3: Magical Solution**

- _Fly_ spell: bypass the hazard entirely
- _Water Walk_: walk on the hot springs safely
- _Teleportation_: skip the field
- Uses spell slots but guarantees safety

### **Environmental Complications**

**Unstable Ground:**

- Every round, make a **Dexterity save (DC 13)** or fall prone
- Falling into a hot spring deals **3d6 fire damage**

**Sulfurous Air:**

- Breathing difficulty
- **Constitution save (DC 12)** each round
- Failure: poisoned condition for 1 round

**Illusions & Mirages:**

- The heat creates false paths
- **Insight DC 15** to realize a path is a mirage
- Following a mirage adds 1d6 rounds to crossing time

### **Reward for Success**

If the party successfully navigates the field:

- They find a geothermal vent with **residual magical energy**
- **Arcana DC 14** reveals this is a **ley line intersection**
- They can **cast a ritual spell** here without components (once)
- Or they can gain **advantage on one saving throw in the next 24 hours**

### **Hidden Treasure**

**Perception DC 18** finds a **bone-carved chest** half-buried in ash:

- 500 gp in infernal gold
- 3 rare gems (worth 200 gp each)
- A scroll of _Protection from Fire_ (one-time use)
- A journal fragment (lore about the Vault)

## **ENCOUNTER 5: THE WANDERER (Roleplay/Information)**

### **SETUP**

As night falls on Day 1, the party encounters a **solitary figure** walking through the wastes—moving away from the direction of the Vault.

### **THE WANDERER**

**Appearance:**

- A humanoid figure, but it's hard to tell what race (shadow clings to them)
- Robes of a once-rich material, now tattered
- Carrying a staff of blackened wood
- Eyes that glow faintly with infernal light
- Limping slightly, as if recently injured

**Identity:** This is **Theron**, a former cultist who fled the Vault

### **Interaction**

**If approached peacefully:**

> _The figure stops, turns slowly. When it speaks, the voice is hollow, echoing._
> 
> _"Turn back. The Vault draws no visitors without cost. I paid mine in memories. In will. In flesh."_
> 
> _It lifts an arm—there are brands carved into the skin, infernal runes._

**What Theron Knows:**

- The Vault's layout (general directions)
- The Golem guardian's command word (but it's a **Deception DC 18** to get him to share it)
- The true purpose of the Ritual Chamber (it's not just for summoning)
- The Courtier's location (he's in the Sanctum, Level 4)
- A warning: **"The Vault remembers. It feeds on those who enter without faith."**

**Theron's Quest Hook:**

If convinced to help:

- He provides a map of the first two levels
- He warns about specific traps
- **But:** He's cursed; once the sun rises, he must return to the Vault against his will
- He begs the party to break his curse (possible side quest)

**Theron's True Nature:**

**Insight DC 16** reveals:

- Theron is bound to the Vault; he can't truly escape
- His curse forces him to wander, then return
- He's been doing this for **decades** (he's lost count)
- He's not entirely human anymore; he's partially infernal

**If the party helps him:**

- He becomes an ally
- Provides concrete assistance (combat advantage, intel)
- Or he becomes a recurring NPC (haunts them throughout the campaign)

### **Complication: Theron's Pursuers**

**Behind Theron, in the distance, shadows move.**

1d4 **Infernal Hounds** (use **Hell Hound** stat block) are tracking him—servants of the Vault, sent to recapture him.

**Options:**

- Help Theron fight
- Let him handle it (he's experienced)
- Negotiate with the hounds
- Take Theron and run

## **ENCOUNTER 6: THE RIFT'S SHADOW (Supernatural/Atmospheric)**

### **SETUP**

During a long rest on the night of Day 1, the party begins to feel **wrong**. The magic in the air is thick, oppressive.

### **THE EXPERIENCE**

**What They See:**

> _As you try to sleep, the ground beneath you becomes unstable. The stars above fade, replaced by a sickly purple light. The air tastes of copper and burning."_
> 
> _In the distance, something immense shifts. A tear in the world, barely visible, glowing with sickly light. The Rift. Not metaphorical. Real. Painfully real._
> 
> _Voices emanate from it—voices of the damned, calling, begging, promising._

**Mechanics:**

**Wisdom save (DC 14)** to resist the voices:

- Failure: Scared condition for 1 round, disadvantage on next save
- Success: Gain insight into the Rift's nature

**If a character fails 3 saves:**

- They're **charmed** and compelled to walk toward the Rift
- Other party members can prevent this with a DC 12 Arcana check
- Or a successful **Insight DC 16** to snap the person out of it

**What They Learn (if they make saves):**

The Rift is:

- **Ancient and real**, not just legend
- **Weakening**, or being deliberately opened
- **Connected to the Shadowed Branch's endgame**
- **Something is trying to get through from the other side**

**Magical Phenomenon:**

**Arcana DC 16** reveals:

- The party's magical items resonate with the Rift's frequency
- Magic-users feel their power amplified (advantage on spell saves)
- But also feel **drawn toward the Rift**

### **Short Rest Interrupted**

The party doesn't fully benefit from their long rest due to nightmares:

- Roll constitution saves as normal
- Treat failures as if they had only a short rest that night
- They're tired and anxious but not damaged

### **Complication: The Vision**

**If any character is a spellcaster:**

- They receive a **sending** from an unknown entity
- Message: _"Come. Complete the work. Open what was sealed. We wait beyond."_
- Source is unclear but feels old, powerful, malevolent

This seeds the idea that something beyond the Rift is aware of them.

## **ENCOUNTER 7: THE OBSIDIAN FIELD (Hazard/Puzzle)**

### **SETUP**

Day 2, morning. The party crosses into a field of **razor-sharp obsidian shards**, all pointing upward like a bed of broken glass.

### **THE HAZARD**

**Crossing the field:**

- 200 feet across
- Non-magical movement is treacherous
- Every foot of movement requires a **Dexterity save (DC 12)** or take 1d4 slashing damage
- Running increases damage to 1d6 per 5 feet moved
- Stealth impossible (noise of walking on glass)

### **Navigation Options**

**Option A: Slow, Careful Walk**

- Takes 6 rounds to cross
- Make one save per round
- Highest total saves = lowest damage taken
- Safe but slow

**Option B: Sprint Across**

- Takes 3 rounds to cross
- Make saves at disadvantage (running on glass)
- Faster but riskier

**Option C: Magical Solution**

- _Levitate_: float above the glass
- _Teleportation_: skip it entirely
- _Pass Without Trace_: move silently (but still takes damage)

**Option D: Create a Path**

- Use magic or strength to move the shards
- **Strength DC 15** to physically create a safe path (takes 20 minutes)
- **Arcana DC 16** to magically sweep the shards aside (takes 10 minutes)

### **Hidden Danger: The Obsidian Golem**

**Perception DC 16** reveals that some of the "shards" are **more structured than others**—they form a humanoid shape.

An **Obsidian Golem** (similar to Stone Golem but with razor edges) is dormant in this field, **activated if the party disturbs too many shards**.

**Combat:**

- AC 17, HP 52
- Attacks deal extra slashing damage (1d6 extra)
- Regenerates if it takes damage (2 HP per round)
- Can be destroyed by magic or hit repeatedly

**Or:** The party can sneak past without waking it (**Stealth DC 14**).

### **Treasure**

**If the Golem is defeated or bypassed:**

- 600 gp worth of magical obsidian shards (can be sold or crafted into weapons)
- A **Ring of Protection** (left by a previous traveler)
- A warning: _"The Vault watches. Turn back while you still can."_

## **ENCOUNTER 8: THE FINAL STRETCH (Atmospheric/Arrival)**

### **SETUP**

As Day 2 progresses, the landscape changes. The **Vault's influence** becomes palpable.

### **THE EXPERIENCE**

**What They Sense:**

> _The ground trembles with a rhythm that's almost like breathing. The sky darkens further—no longer red, but deep crimson, almost black. The air is thick, oppressive, and seems to watch you._
> 
> _Whispers emanate from everywhere and nowhere. Familiar voices call your names. Temptations, threats, promises._
> 
> _In the distance, black stone rises from the ground—the Vault, finally visible._

**Perception DC 13:** The party spots the hidden entrance (the illusion that appears as a cliff face)

**Arcana DC 16:** The party feels the wards protecting the Vault

**Insight DC 14:** The party understands they're being tested before they even arrive

### **Final Challenge: The Wards**

As the party approaches the Vault, they encounter **magical barriers**:

**Ward 1: Illusion**

- **Arcana DC 18** to perceive
- **Insight DC 16** to understand it's a test
- Passing through without understanding it: **Wisdom save DC 15** or become **frightened**

**Ward 2: Compulsion**

- **Charisma save (DC 15)** to resist
- Failure: compelled to speak your name and purpose
- Success: you can lie or stay silent

**Ward 3: Psychological**

- Shows the party visions of failure, death, damnation
- **Wisdom save (DC 16)** to proceed
- Failure: **exhaustion level 1** (fatigue and despair)

**Reward for Passing:**

- The party gains **advantage on the first combat encounter** in the Vault (mental fortitude)
- Or **+2 to saving throws** for the first hour inside the Vault

### **The Vault's Welcome**

If the party passes the wards:

> _The cliff face seems to... open. Not a door, but a recognition. The Vault acknowledges your passage._
> 
> _Cold air flows out, smelling of old stone, infernal magic, and secrets centuries old._
> 
> _You have arrived at the Vault of Whispers._
> 
> _Behind you, the sun begins to set. The wastes grow darker._
> 
> _There is no turning back now._

## **PACING & DIFFICULTY SCALING**

### **For a Quick Journey (6-8 hours real time)**

Include:

- Sulfur Storm (short encounter)
- Devil Patrol (combat or negotiation, brief)
- Obsidian Field (hazard, quick resolution)
- Arrival at Vault

**Skip:** The Wanderer, Cultist Camp, Rift Vision, Geysers

### **For a Detailed Journey (2-4 sessions)**

Include all encounters in order:

- Allows for roleplay and decision-making
- Builds atmosphere and tension
- Gives multiple chances to learn about the Vault
- Players feel the weight of the journey

### **For a Deadly Journey (High Difficulty)**

Increase all DC checks by +2 Add extra enemies to combat encounters Include **all complications** listed in each encounter Make environmental hazards more dangerous

## **FINAL NOTES**

### **Atmosphere is Key**

Make these encounters feel like a **descent into Hell**. Each encounter should reinforce:

- The danger of the location
- The inevitability of conflict
- The weight of what they're about to face
- The sense that they're being watched/tested

### **Allow Player Agency**

Every encounter should have multiple solutions:

- Combat, but also negotiation/stealth
- Direct routes, but also alternate paths
- Hazards that can be overcome multiple ways

### **Build Toward the Vault**

Each encounter should hint at:

- The Vault's existence and power
- The Courtier's location
- The Cult's activity
- The Rift's proximity

By the time they arrive, players should feel like they've been tested and are ready for what comes next.
