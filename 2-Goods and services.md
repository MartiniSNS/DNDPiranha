[[1-Maela Fern - Barmaid of The Whispering Oak]]
Practical goods & services

- Food & drink: ale, cider, stew, simple bread and cheese. (Cheap: ale/cider ~4 sp, a hearty stew ~5 sp.)
- Lodging: simple guest loft rooms for the night (standard room ~5 sp/night). A quieter, private loft room can be arranged for a higher price (1–2 gp) if you need privacy.
- Basic first aid: bandaging, setting simple breaks, stabilizing a dying creature. (She can stabilize without magic; serious healing requires a healer/cleric.) Fee or barter accepted.
- Small supplies: torches, rope (short lengths), and other common adventuring odds — not a full outfitter, but she can supply essentials or point you to [[Everspring]] for larger purchases.
