[[Scene 2]]
Deciphering the Secondary [[Ledger]]
**What it is:** A handwritten record kept separately from official tollhouse books. This is the cult's local operational ledger—names, dates, payments, and coded notes.

**Initial skim (no check needed):**
Allow players to read the ledger aloud and extract obvious facts:
- Six months of entries (dates ranging from ~Month 1 to current).
- Monthly payments to "S. Maldric — Thornwell steward — 15 gp/month."
- Notation: "For grain tithe surplus cover. Daughter's safety guaranteed."
- Other payments listed: "M. Blackford — 8 gp/month — manor account access." "A. Herdsman — 5 gp/month — north road watch."
- Courier name appears frequently: "Rook — dusk delivery — manifest secured."

**Investigation DC 12 (parsing the shorthand):**
Success reveals:
- The ledger uses abbreviations and initials, but the pattern is consistent. "S. Malric" is likely a steward's full name.
- "Tithe surplus cover" suggests blackmail tied to grain taxes—the steward falsifies records to hide stolen grain, and the Branch exploits this.
- The column headers suggest a payment schedule: "monthly payout," "leverage held," "status" (all entries show "secure" or "compliant").

**Investigation DC 14 (reading between the lines):**
Success reveals:
- A note on the final page: "If S. Malric fails, activate secondary plan: family moved to capital, girl placed in steward's rival household (scandal). Rook to handle."
- This suggests the Branch doesn't just blackmail—they have contingency plans to destroy lives if leverage fails.
- Another marginal note: "H. Matron approval for escalation pending. Watch for Rooted Fane alignment."
- "H. Matron" likely refers to the High Matron, the cult's leader.
- "Rooted Fane alignment" suggests a ritual date or a site of power.

**Investigation DC 16 (full decipherment):**
Success reveals:
- The ledger tracks not just payments but also cult members' "status" — some are marked "compromised," "loyal," or "disposable."
- Kael Dun (one of the dead cultists) is marked "loyal" with a note: "Witness removal if necessary."
- A final entry, dated three days ago: "New directive from H. Matron. Ledger security increased. If Ciaran's pages surface, move primary cache to Archways. Rook to confirm receipt."
- This means the cult knows Ciaran has pieces of the ledger and is preparing to move the evidence.


"As you turn the pages, the careful handwriting reveals a pattern—payments in and out, dates aligned with moonrise, and names. 'S. Malric.' 'M. Blackford.' 'A. Herdsman.' The amounts are small but consistent: 15 gp here, 8 there, 5 elsewhere. And in the margins, cryptic notes: 'Daughter secured.' 'Tithe falsified.' 'Loyalty confirmed.'"
