Second tier of [[The Shadowed Branch]]

**Whisperers** — The High Matron's personal agents, numbering perhaps a dozen across Alderwyn.

- **Role:** Handle blackmail schemes, correspondence networks, and recruitment of new Hands and Initiates.
- **Characteristics:** Educated, cunning, often posing as merchants, clerks, scholars, or court advisors.
- **Methods:** They forge documents, plant false evidence, and turn trusted allies into informants.
- **Geographic Assignment:** At least one Whisperer per major region (Verdant Fringe, Apletersia, Wathertia, etc.).

**Notable Whisperers (examples for your campaign):**

- _The Courtier_ — A false-face at Apletersia's Veiled Registry, managing ledgers and ensuring court secrets remain buried.
- _The Steward's Shadow_ — A travelling agent who blackmails minor officials like the Steward of Thornwell.
- _The Toll-keeper's Master_ — An overseer who communicates with the lord of Blackford through coded letters hidden in trade manifest.
