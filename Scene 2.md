# Session 3 — Scene 2: Evidence & Interrogation
## The Weight of Discovery
*The dust of combat still hangs in the air. Papers scatter across the ledger chest. Garrin, if alive, trembles in a corner or lies bandaged and conscious. The bodies of the cultists remain still. Outside, the evening deepens—perhaps five to ten minutes have passed since the last spell crackled. The toll clock in the distance marks the hour.*

*Now comes the work of understanding: sorting truth from lies, parsing the ledger's code, and deciding what to do with Garrin and any captured cultists. The evidence in your hands could unravel threads that reach all the way to Thornwell, the Archways of Bridgia, or deeper still.*

---

## Core Scene Sequence

### Opening (DM Narration — set the tone)
"You've secured the scene. The air tastes of ash and old paper. Around you:

- The secondary ledger lies open, its pages filled with careful handwriting and cryptic entries.
- Sealed letters from 'S.' are scattered on the floor—some still intact, some torn.
- Garrin is either pleading quietly, defiant, or unconscious depending on the fight's outcome.
- A small iron chest sits in the corner of the cellar alcove, still locked.
- The ritual scroll from the hidden nook waits for examination.

You have maybe **8–12 minutes** before someone notices the silence. What do you do first?"

## Mechanical Investigation Beats

### 1) Deciphering the Secondary Ledger
**What it is:** A handwritten record kept separately from official tollhouse books. This is the cult's local operational ledger—names, dates, payments, and coded notes.

**Initial skim (no check needed):**
Allow players to read the ledger aloud and extract obvious facts:
- Six months of entries (dates ranging from ~Month 1 to current).
- Monthly payments to "S. Malric — Thornwell steward — 15 gp/month."
- Notation: "For grain tithe surplus cover. Daughter's safety guaranteed."
- Other payments listed: "M. Blackford — 8 gp/month — manor account access." "A. Herdsman — 5 gp/month — north road watch."
- Courier name appears frequently: "Rook — dusk delivery — manifest secured."

**Investigation DC 12 (parsing the shorthand):**
Success reveals:
- The ledger uses abbreviations and initials, but the pattern is consistent. "S. Malric" is likely a steward's full name.
- "Tithe surplus cover" suggests blackmail tied to grain taxes—the steward falsifies records to hide stolen grain, and the Branch exploits this.
- The column headers suggest a payment schedule: "monthly payout," "leverage held," "status" (all entries show "secure" or "compliant").

**Investigation DC 14 (reading between the lines):**
Success reveals:
- A note on the final page: "If S. Malric fails, activate secondary plan: family moved to capital, girl placed in steward's rival household (scandal). Rook to handle."
- This suggests the Branch doesn't just blackmail—they have contingency plans to destroy lives if leverage fails.
- Another marginal note: "H. Matron approval for escalation pending. Watch for Rooted Fane alignment."
- "H. Matron" likely refers to the High Matron, the cult's leader.
- "Rooted Fane alignment" suggests a ritual date or a site of power.

**Investigation DC 16 (full decipherment):**
Success reveals:
- The ledger tracks not just payments but also cult members' "status" — some are marked "compromised," "loyal," or "disposable."
- Kael Dun (one of the dead cultists) is marked "loyal" with a note: "Witness removal if necessary."
- A final entry, dated three days ago: "New directive from H. Matron. Ledger security increased. If Ciaran's pages surface, move primary cache to Archways. Rook to confirm receipt."
- This means the cult knows Ciaran has pieces of the ledger and is preparing to move the evidence.

**DM guidance:** Give layers of discovery. Lower DC = basic facts (names, amounts, basic motives). Higher DC = contingencies, ritual references, and urgency (the cache is about to move).

### 2) Reading the Sealed Letters from "S."
**What they are:** Correspondence from "S. Malric" to "Rook" (the courier). Three intact letters remain.

[[Letter_1.png]] (oldest, ~four months ago):**
> "Rook,
> 
> The arrangement remains secure. S. Maldric provides monthly surplus, and no questions are asked. The toll records will match the accounts—my nephew ensures it. My daughter's safety is guaranteed so long as I comply. I have no other choice.
> 
> The next delivery should arrive before the harvest fair. I will ensure the ledger fragment remains in the false bottom.
> 
> — S.M."

**Investigation DC 11 (basic reading):**
This reveals:
- S. Maldric is cooperative but under duress (mentions daughter's safety).
- The ledger is intentionally split—fragments hidden in multiple locations.
- A harvest fair is coming (creates a time pressure or social event for future scenes).

**Investigation DC 13 (analyzing handwriting & tone):**
Success reveals:
- The handwriting is shaky in places—signs of stress or fear.
- The tone is defeatist—Malric has accepted his situation.
- The phrase "my nephew ensures it" suggests a second person in Thornwell is also compromised.

[[Letter_2.png]] (two months ago):**
> "S.M.,
> 
> The H. Matron is pleased with your silence and precision. Your daughter thrives in the capital. When she is of age, she will be offered a choice: leave with a purse, or join our ranks as she wishes. This is the mercy we offer to the obedient.
> 
> Continue the arrangement. A new contact will arrive at the next fair to confirm the Rooted Fane alignment. You will provide what is asked.
> 
> — Rook"

**Investigation DC 12 (reading implications):**
This reveals:
- The cult is long-term in its planning—they've moved Malric's daughter to the capital.
- There's a "Rooted Fane alignment"—a specific date or ritual connected to a place called the Rooted Fane.
- A new contact will arrive at the harvest fair.

**Investigation DC 15 (detecting subtext):**
Success reveals:
- The phrase "offered a choice" is sinister—the daughter may be groomed to join the cult.
- "H. Matron is pleased" suggests the cult hierarchy watches Malric's behavior closely.
- The Rooted Fane is treated as a significant location—not just a rumor, but a real site with ritual significance.

[[Letter_3.png]] (most recent, dated ~one week ago):**
> "S.M.,
> 
> Urgent. The alignment date is confirmed: three weeks from the writing of this letter. We require the full tithe records for the last year and a written oath from you, sworn in sap, binding you to the Branch's purpose.
> 
> Rook will collect both at the fair. If you refuse, we activate the secondary plan. Your daughter will know nothing of what happens to her. Comply, and she remains safe.
> 
> Time grows short. The rift weakens. We must act.
> 
> — Rook"

**Investigation DC 11 (basic reading):**
This reveals:
- A deadline: ~three weeks from the letter's date (roughly one week away in game time, depending on when you find it).
- The cult needs "tithe records" and a "sworn oath."
- The phrase "the rift weakens" is ominous—suggests the cult is trying to exploit or seal something.

**Investigation DC 14 (decoding the urgency):**
Success reveals:
- "Sap" as a binding agent suggests ritual magic—not just blackmail, but compulsion.
- "The rift weakens" likely refers to the ancient tear in the Weave that was sealed 200 years ago (per campaign lore).
- The cult is working toward a specific date (the Rooted Fane alignment) and needs Malric's cooperation to proceed.

**Investigation DC 16 (full implications):**
Success reveals:
- The letters suggest the cult is preparing for a major ritual tied to the Rooted Fane.
- Malric's oath, bound in sap, would tie him permanently to the Branch (per the ritual scroll reference).
- If Malric doesn't comply by the alignment date, the cult will destroy his daughter or use her as a ritual component.

**DM guidance:** These letters are plot gold. They directly reference the Rooted Fane, the High Matron, and a looming deadline. Players should feel the pressure—the cult is building toward something big, and Malric's cooperation is essential to their plan.

### 3) Examining the Ritual Scroll
**What it is:** A parchment detailing a dark rite. Found in the iron chest (if opened) or the cellar nook.

**Text (players can read all or part based on checks):**

> **"Rite of Binding — For Use When Leverage Fails"**
> 
> *When a subject resists the Branch's will, the sap of the Rooted Fane may be drawn and mixed with the subject's own blood. An oath sworn under this rite binds the subject's fate to the Branch itself. Breach of the oath, and the sap returns to the heart—death certain and swift.*
> 
> *Requirements:*
> *— Sap from the Rooted Fane (sacred; stored in glass vials, kept in cold dark).*
> *— Subject's blood (freely given or taken; ideally drawn at a place of power).*
> *— Witness (a Whisperer or High Priest/Matron).*
> *— Oath spoken aloud three times.*
> 
> *Effect: Subject cannot break the oath without death. The Branch knows of any attempt to flee or betray. Pain and sickness follow disobedience, escalating until the subject recants or dies.*
> 
> *Use sparingly. Not all subjects survive the initial binding.*

**Arcana / Religion check DC 12:**
Success reveals:
- This is old magic, tied to nature and life force—not standard necromancy or scholarly magic.
- The sap of the Rooted Fane is a key component—suggests the Rooted Fane is a real place with magical properties.
- The binding is permanent unless the oath is publicly renounced (which would expose the cult).

**Arcana / Religion check DC 15:**
Success reveals:
- The ritual is designed to create an unbreakable compulsion—torture or death if the subject tries to break free.
- The requirement for a witness means there's always someone who knows the oath was sworn.
- This is darker magic than simple blackmail—suggests the cult has access to ritual knowledge and possibly ritual sites.

**Arcana / Religion check DC 18:**
Success reveals:
- The sap's power is tied to the Rooted Fane itself—a place of significant magical potential.
- The binding may be tied to the old catastrophe 200 years ago (sap magic, ritual sites, rifts in the Weave).
- If the party has met a character familiar with the old lore (hermit, archivist, etc.), they might recognize this as a remnant of pre-Rift magic.

**DM guidance:** This scroll is evidence of the cult's capabilities and motives. It shows they're willing to use dark magic to ensure loyalty. It also points directly to the Rooted Fane as a place of power and suggests deeper lore about the Weave's weakness.

### 4) Interrogating [[Garrin Tollman]] (if alive & cooperative)
**Starting point:** Garrin is frightened, bleeding or bandaged, and pleading.

**Suggested opening lines (DM uses):**
> "I didn't know—I swear I didn't know they'd fight here! They just left the satchel at dusk, and I—I was paid to look away."

> "My wife. My wife is in town. Please, if you let me live, I'll tell you everything. Just—don't let them know I talked."

**Interrogation approach: Persuasion DC 12 (gentle)**
Use this if the party is kind, promising protection.

*Success reveals:*
- Garrin's name and that he's been taking bribes for ~six months.
- He was paid 2 gp per week to allow satchels to be left in the false bottom of the ledger chest.
- The courier, "Rook," is a pale man with a scar on his left thumb. He arrives at dusk most weeks and leaves quickly.
- Garrin was told never to open the satchels—just leave them for collection.
- "S." is mentioned in whispered conversation once; Garrin assumed it was a steward or official based on the tone of respect.

*Failure:*
- Garrin clams up or demands a guarantee before speaking. Offer him safety (Maela's loft, a guard, departure to another town) and he'll reconsider.

**Interrogation approach: Intimidation DC 14 (harsh)**
Use this if the party threatens or is aggressive.

*Success reveals:*
- All of the above, plus: Garrin admits he was shown a scrap of cloth from his wife's dress—a threat.
- He confesses that the cult told him "if anyone asks about the ledger, say it burned" and that he had an escape plan (a horse hidden, supplies for a day's ride).
- If pushed further (another Intimidation DC 15 success): Garrin admits he's seen couriers arrive other times at the nearby manor—suggests the Blackford lord is also involved.

*Failure or complication:*
- Garrin becomes hysterical or tries to flee. He may ring the bell again (Reaction, see stat block) or attempt to escape if not restrained.

**Interrogation approach: Insight DC 13 (reading the signs)**
Success reveals:
- Garrin's fear is genuine—he's not a true believer, just a desperate man under coercion.
- When he mentions his wife, his voice cracks—this is his leverage point. Offer safety for his family and he'll cooperate fully.
- He's concealing something about the manor—use a follow-up question: "What do you know about the lord?" Persuasion DC 12 to press.

**Interrogation follow-up: "Tell me about the Blackford lord."**
Persuasion DC 12 or Insight DC 13 to press.

*Success reveals:*
- The lord is nervous around the cult handlers.
- Garrin saw a courier enter the manor through a private door one night.
- There's a key that opens something in the lord's private study (Garrin may know about the iron key from the first cultist's satchel).
- The lord sometimes leaves sealed envelopes for collection by couriers.

**DM guidance:** Garrin is a sympathetic NPC if played well. His fear is real, and his wife's safety is his primary concern. A party that promises protection gains a long-term informant; a party that threatens him gets fast answers but loses future cooperation.

### 5) Interrogating a Captured Cultist (if any survive)
**Starting point:** If a cultist is unconscious, they wake after 1d4 minutes and are immediately disoriented.

**The cultist's identity:** Use the journal from the corpse if available. The name is **Kael Dun**, a mid-level operative.

**Kael's personality (DM choice, based on your table):**
- **Option A — True believer:** Defiant, refuses to speak, mutters about "the Branch's will" or "the H. Matron watches."
- **Option B — Mercenary:** Pragmatic, willing to deal if the price is right. "What's your offer? I'm listening."
- **Option C — Coerced:** Frightened, similar to Garrin. Will trade information for safety or mercy.

**Default (use if you haven't decided): Kael is a mixture—loyal but scared. He won't betray the high command, but he'll give field-level information under pressure.**

**Interrogation approach: Persuasion DC 13 (bargain)**
*Success reveals:*
- Kael's name and role: a field Hand, responsible for satchel collection and watch rotations.
- The Blackford operation is one of six active cells in the Verdant Fringe.
- The handler above Rook is called "the Whisperer" (title only; Kael doesn't know the name).
- Kael was told to protect the secondary ledger at all costs and to destroy it if capture seemed imminent.
- The next major operation is tied to the "Rooted Fane alignment" (Kael has overheard this phrase but doesn't know details).

*Failure:*
- Kael refuses to speak beyond his name and demands a lawyer/authority figure.

**Interrogation approach: Intimidation DC 15 (threats)**
*Success reveals:*
- All information from Persuasion, plus: Kael admits he's terrified of the Branch—they've killed people for talking.
- He reveals that Rook is higher in the hierarchy than a standard Hand; Rook answers directly to the Whisperer.
- He confesses that the cult uses compulsion rites (sap binding) on senior operatives—Rook may be bound.
- If pushed further: Kael admits the cult has a ritual site called the Rooted Fane and that something important happens there "when the stars align" (~three weeks away).

*Failure or complication:*
- Kael becomes aggressive or suicidal—he may attempt to bite through his tongue or beg to be killed rather than face recapture by the Branch.

**Interrogation approach: Insight DC 14 (reading emotions)**
Success reveals:
- Kael is deeply afraid of the Branch's leadership, more so than fear of the party.
- His mention of "sap binding" carries particular dread—he may himself be bound.
- He values his life but fears retribution more; this suggests a pathway: offer him a way to disappear (new name, travel funds, safe passage).

**Zone of Truth (if the party uses magic):**
If the party casts Zone of Truth and Kael fails his Charisma save, he cannot lie. He'll answer honestly, which yields all information above plus:
- The Whisperer's approximate location: "They move between Thornwell and Everspring, but I've only seen them at meetings."
- The identity of the High Matron: "I don't know the true name—only that they're called H.M. and they lead from a place called Rooted Fane."
- The date of the alignment: "Three weeks, on the first full moon. The branch must be strong by then."

**Warning for DM:** Using magic for interrogation may draw cult attention if they discover it was used (reputation cost in the Fringe; possibility of magical counter-measures).

## Optional: Opening the Iron Chest
**Location:** In the cellar nook where the ritual scroll was found.

**Lock DC 15** (Thieves' Tools) or **Strength DC 17** (smashing it).

**Contents:**
- Wax seals stamped with the twisted-branch sigil (unused, fresh).
- A vial of dark sap (appears to be tree resin; labeled in tiny script: "Rooted Fane — Handle carefully").
- A smaller ledger fragment: another piece of the operational record, listing "secondary cache location: Archways Deep Vaults" and a key sketch.
- A brass key (unmarked; likely fits a lock at the Archways or another cult site).

**Arcana check DC 13 (examining the sap):**
Success reveals:
- The sap is not ordinary tree resin—it has a faint aura of magic, though it's dormant.
- Handling it for too long causes a tingling sensation (harmless but unsettling).
- It's likely used in binding rituals and should be treated as dangerous.

**Investigation DC 14 (analyzing the key sketch):**
Success reveals:
- The sketch shows a vault door with specific lock configurations—likely at the Archways of Bridgia.
- The sketch includes measurements and angle marks, suggesting the party can attempt to open it if they reach the site.

## Time Pressure — The Reinforcement Timer Revisited

**If you haven't rolled yet:** Roll 1d6 now to determine when reinforcements arrive.
- **1–2:** **3 minutes** (very short window—party must make quick decisions).
- **3–4:** **8 minutes** (moderate time; enough to finish interrogation but not a full search).
- **5–6:** **15 minutes** (generous; party can be thorough).

**When reinforcements arrive:**
- **Low alert:** A town guard or two (unarmed or with shortswords). They investigate noise and demand explanation.
- **Medium alert:** 2–3 cult Hands with a mission to recover the ledger or retrieve Kael/Garrin.
- **High alert:** A Whisperer-level agent (senior cultist) with 1–2 escorts, arriving to negotiate or threaten.

## DM Scene Resolution Options

### Option A: Thorough Investigation (30–45 minutes)
- Party carefully searches bodies, reads ledger, opens iron chest, and interrogates Garrin/Kael.
- They piece together the full picture: S. Malric is a steward in Thornwell, the Rooted Fane alignment is coming, and a major ritual is planned.
- **Cost:** Reinforcements likely arrive near the end of the scene. Party must decide to fight, flee, or negotiate.

### Option B: Quick Search & Escape (15–25 minutes)
- Party grabs the most obvious evidence (secondary ledger, letters, satchel with gold) and escapes before reinforcements.
- They get partial information but must research or interrogate captured cultists later.
- **Cost:** Lesser detail but faster pacing; Thornwell connection is clear but ritual details are vague.

### Option C: Interrogation First (20–30 minutes)
- Party prioritizes questioning Garrin or Kael over artifact search.
- They learn locations, names, and motives through roleplay.
- The ledger remains available for later study but they miss the iron chest or sealed letters.

## Narrative Beats to Play

### Beat 1: The First Discovery
*Once the party finds and opens the secondary ledger:*

> "As you turn the pages, the careful handwriting reveals a pattern—payments in and out, dates aligned with moonrise, and names. 'S. Malric.' 'M. Blackford.' 'A. Herdsman.' The amounts are small but consistent: 15 gp here, 8 there, 5 elsewhere. And in the margins, cryptic notes: 'Daughter secured.' 'Tithe falsified.' 'Loyalty confirmed.'"

**Pause and ask:** "What do you do? Do you keep reading, or do you check for other hidden documents?"

### Beat 2: The Realization
*When they reach the letter dated one week ago:*

> "Your eyes catch on the final line: 'Time grows short. The rift weakens. We must act.' The word 'rift' sits heavy—you've heard stories, old warnings about a tear in the world that was sealed long ago. Is the cult trying to tear it open again? Or seal it further? You can't be certain, but the implication is clear: something major is coming, and the steward of Thornwell is a crucial piece."

### Beat 3: The Ritual Scroll
*When they encounter the text:*

> "You hold a ritual scroll, its edges yellowed and brittle. The words describe a binding rite—one that uses sap and blood to create an unbreakable oath. 'Breach of the oath, and the sap returns to the heart—death certain and swift.' It's clinical, methodical, and horrifying. This isn't blackmail anymore. This is compulsion. This is slavery."

### Beat 4: Garrin's Plea (if he's conscious)
*Use this line for emotional weight:*

> "Garrin's voice breaks as he speaks: 'They showed me a scrap of my wife's dress. Just a scrap. And they said, "She's safe, as long as you do as we say." I didn't want this. I was desperate. Please—I have a family.'"

**Pause for player reaction.** Let them decide whether to trust him, use him, or discard him.

### Beat 5: The Countdown
*As the party finishes searching:*

> "Outside, the light is fading fast. You've been in this tollhouse for perhaps ten minutes now. Longer than you'd hoped. Longer than is safe. In the distance, you hear the sound of a horse—or is that just the wind? The cult knows operatives are missing. How long before they send someone to check?"

## Roleplay Lines & NPC Reactions

**Garrin (cooperative):**
> "The pale man—Rook—he always arrives just before dusk. He has a scar on his thumb, shaped like a crescent. He's polite, almost gentle, but there's something cold in his eyes. He hands me the satchel and leaves with whatever was in the chest. I never saw his face clearly."

**Garrin (defiant if tortured or angered):**
> "You think you're heroes? The Branch is bigger than you know. They have people everywhere—in the courts, in the guilds, in the watch itself. You take me, they'll find me. You kill me, they'll kill ten others to send a message."

**Kael (true believer, if he resists):**
> "The H. Matron's will is absolute. The rift calls to us. We are the instruments of restoration. You can kill me, but the Branch will endure. The alignment comes, and none of you can stop it."

**Kael (mercenary, if he bargains):**
> "Look—I was paid to stand watch and move cargo. I don't care about rituals or politics. You let me walk, and I disappear into the Fringe. I'll never speak your names. Deal?"

**Kael (coerced, if he's scared):**
> "Please—I didn't choose this. They have my sister. They said if I run, she dies. If I talk, she dies. Either way, she's dead. Unless—unless you can help her. Where is she? Can you protect her?"

## Consequences & Fallout Summary

### If the party secures all evidence:
- They have a complete picture: Thornwell steward, Blackford lord, operational structure, ritual date, and Rooted Fane location.
- **Next session:** They can plan a proactive strike—raid Thornwell, pressure Malric, or investigate the Rooted Fane directly.

### If they get partial evidence:
- They have names and locations but miss ritual details or the urgency of the timeline.
- **Next session:** They may think they have more time than they do; cult catches them off-guard.

### If they're interrupted by reinforcements:
- They must flee, taking what they can carry.
- The cult recovers the scene and may move evidence to secondary locations (Archways of Bridgia).
- **Next session:** Becomes a manhunt or a race to secondary sites.

### If Garrin/Kael escape or die:
- Loss of an informant; they must find information elsewhere (Thornwell archives, captured couriers, or Everspring research).
- Cult knows the party is investigating and escalates countermeasures.
