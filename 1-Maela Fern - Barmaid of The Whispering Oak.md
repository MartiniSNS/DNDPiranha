## Role
Barmaid and unofficial "heart" of [[The Whispering Oak]]

## Description
Maela is a young woman with auburn curls tied back in a messy bun, always sporting a warm smile and twinkling green eyes. Her hands are quick, her laughter infectious, and she’s known for her uncanny ability to remember every patron’s favorite drink. She moves gracefully between tables, her boots barely making a sound on the old wooden floor, and she has a knack for defusing tense moments with a gentle touch or a well-timed joke.

## Personality & Quirks
- Cheerful, welcoming, and a bit nosy—Maela loves a good story and is always eager to hear the latest gossip.
- She has a soft spot for newcomers and goes out of her way to make them feel at home.
- Sometimes hums old folk tunes while working.
- Can tell when someone’s hiding something, but rarely pushes for details unless she senses danger.
--
## Motivation
Maela dreams of one day owning the tavern herself and is fiercely loyal to its staff and regulars. She keeps a watchful eye out for trouble, especially since rumors of the cult have begun to swirl in Greenhollow.

## Connections
- Trusted confidant of the tavernkeeper.
- Friendly with most locals, and often the first to notice newcomers.
- May overhear or witness clues related to the cult’s activities.

Mechanics & suggested DCs (use as a DM guideline)

- Hear general rumour by eavesdropping: passive Perception/Investigation or a DC 10 check.
- Buy a useful, specific lead from Maela: Persuasion DC 12 (or pay ~1–2 gp).
- Earn a dangerous secret tied to the cult (coded letters, names): Persuasion DC 15 or pay ~3–5 gp / perform a meaningful favour.
- She’ll stabilize or bandage for free if you’re friendly; charging applies if you’re rough or hostile.

In play: Maela’s tone

- Warm, practical, watchful. She laughs easily, but her eyes miss little. She respects those who respect her house and rewards kindness with tips, names, and safe shelter; cross her and the Whispering Oak’s roots close around you faster than you can say “last call.”
