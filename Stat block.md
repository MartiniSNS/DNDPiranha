[[Lord Ciaran Ashvale]] 

Armor Class 13 (studded leather / parry) 
Hit Points 27 (6d8) 
Speed 30 ft. 
Medium humanoid (human) — Neutral Good (wary)

STR 11 (+0) DEX 13 (+1) CON 12 (+1) INT 14 (+2) WIS 12 (+1) CHA 16 (+3)

Skills: Persuasion +5, History +4, Insight +3, Deception +5 Passive Perception 11 Languages: Common (+courtly tongues) Challenge 1 (200 XP)

Traits

- Coded Knowledge. Advantage on Investigation and History checks involving ledgers, seals, court records, or coded correspondence.
- Courtly Presence (1/short rest). When making a Persuasion check to secure aid, shelter, or passage, Ciaran can add +2 to the roll (represents old rank and polished manner).

Actions

- Rapier. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d8 + 3 piercing.
- Dagger. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 1d4 + 3 piercing.

Reaction

- Parry. Add +2 to AC against one melee attack that would hit. Must use this reaction before the DM states the attack’s result.

Roleplay & Use at Table

- Ciaran is a fragile but capable patron. He prefers to avoid direct fights, using persuasion, bargaining, and careful parrying to escape danger.
- He keeps evidence (ledger fragments, cipher keys) hidden on his person (boot, lining, signet ring).
- If captured, he bargains, trades information, or stalls rather than dying heroically. If rescued, he rewards the party with leads, introductions, or a favour.
- Use Coded Knowledge to hand players clues and to shine in archive/ledger scenes.

Tactical Notes

- Social engine: use Ciaran to open doors in towns, negotiate with officials, and interpret courtly records.
- Combat: competent but not stalwart; if outnumbered or wounded, he will try to flee or hand off evidence to the party.
