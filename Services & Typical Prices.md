[[Thalia Mireweave]]

Services & Typical Prices (Everspring market; adjust by DM)
- Minor repairs (patch, resew): 1–3 gp, 1–3 hours.  
- Recut & resizing (o/s armour cloth, cloaks): 5–12 gp, 4–8 hours.  
- New common outfit (suitable for travel): 8–25 gp, 1–3 days.  
- Fine garment (noble-quality coat/dress): 50–150 gp, 3–10 days.  
- Disguise kit (tailored outfit + makeup & hairwork): 25 gp + 1 day — grants a one‑time advantage on Deception checks to pass as a named social role if used correctly.  
- Concealed compartment sewn into clothes or pack: 3–8 gp + 2–6 hours — Sleight of Hand DC 12 to hide/retrieve without notice.  
- Quieting work (softened soles, muffled hems): 10 gp + 6–12 hours — grants +2 to Stealth checks for one watch (8 hours) when worn; degrades after 3 uses.
- Emergency jury‑stitch (field repair): 2 gp + materials, instant/one-action field fix — restores garment function for one encounter.
- Sap‑cleaning / stain neutralizing (special): 5–20 gp depending on severity. For Rooted Fane sap or ritual residues, Thalia requires an Arcana check or an alchemical additive (see below).
