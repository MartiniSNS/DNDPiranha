**A Twisted Moment:** The harpy, pinned and gasping, lets out a sound between a shriek and a laugh. If anyone speaks Sylvan or Common, she rasps:

- _"You've **delayed** nothing. The stones drink deep tonight. The **barrow** opens. Your friends chase shadows while the **real work** happens beneath..."_
- She claws at the air: _"Tell them the **Whisperer sends her blessing**. Tell them to **run**."_
- Then she either dies, or one of her wounds proves mortal before interrogation can continue.

**The Implication:** A **Whisperer**—a senior cult agent—is orchestrating events. This is no random harpy; it was a _deliberate guardian_.

As your party collects themselves, they search the harpy's lair more thoroughly.

**What they find:**

- Beneath loose stone, a shallow cache containing:
    - **The silver ring** (engraved "To my heartbeat—R.L.") — a keepsake from the kidnapped parents
    - **A child's wooden hobby-horse**, worn smooth by small hands
    - **Scraps of cloth** stained with what appears to be ritual ash or sap
    - **The wax-sealed charm** (as noted before)—when examined closely, it bears the **twisted branch symbol** of the Shadowed Branch

Before they can fully recover, a figure emerges through the front door, looking down at the defeated harpy.

**Who it is:**

- A **young shepherd**, wild-eyed and limping, bearing a fresh wound on his shoulder
- He's been tracking the harpy (or fleeing from something worse)

**What he tells them (Insight DC 11 reveals truth):**

- _"The bird—it wasn't alone. There were **people** in the woods. Masked. Moving toward the **old stones** at [[Oakbarrow]]."_
- He's part-delirious: "They took my friend. Took him _singing_. He walked right into the dark, like the stones were _calling_ him."
- If pressed: "There's a child ([[Elin Amsley]])) she was crying by their family home The masked ones ignored her. They only wanted the _shepherd_."

- **Main room (where they fought):** Furniture, debris, scattered bones and feathers.
- **Adjacent parlour:** A fireplace (long cold), a bookshelf with water-damaged tomes, a writing desk.
- **Back chamber:** An old bed, clothing scattered, a small lockbox

As you catch your breath atop the crumbling outcrop, you notice something you hadn't before: the remnants of a structure—half-collapsed stone walls, a sagging timber frame, and what might once have been a door, now hanging crooked on rusted hinges.

The air around it is _stale_. Thick with age.

_Make a Perception check, both of you._

**DC 10:** You notice dust motes dancing in the fading light that filters through gaps in the walls. This place has not seen living breath in years—perhaps decades.

**DC 12:** Beyond the dust, you spot shapes within—furniture long since warped by time, and... something pale, half-buried beneath a fallen beam and decades of grime.

**DC 14+:** There's a _hat_. A top hat, to be precise, sitting askew on what might once have been a shelf or table. It's so caked in dust that at first glance it looks like just another pile of rubble.

---

## **The Discovery**

_Assuming a successful check:_

As you move cautiously through the threshold of this forgotten place, your boots stirring up clouds of dust that dance like ghosts in the last light of day, you spot it: **a top hat**, perched on the remnants of what might have been furniture.

The hat is _magnificent_—or would be, if not for the layer of dust so thick it obscures nearly all colour. Fine felt, once black or deep burgundy, now the colour of old bone. A silk band, faded and frayed, circles its base. A single feather—also dust-grey—droops from the band like a wilted thing.

_What do you do?_

---

## **The Moment of Discovery**

Once a character reaches for it:

---

### **Option A: Rubbing It Off**

One of you reaches out and, with careful fingers, begins to brush away the dust.

_Make a Dexterity check, DC 10._

**Success:** You manage to clear away much of the grime. The hat's true colour emerges—a rich _deep burgundy_, almost wine-dark, with silver embroidery along the band depicting... _twisted branches?_ The silk lining, when you can see it, bears a maker's mark: ornate lettering that reads _"Ashvale Haberdashery - Apletersia."_

The feather, once cleaned, reveals itself to be _silver-white_, caught with a faint, iridescent shimmer—not natural feather-shimmer, but something _magical_, perhaps?

**Failure:** As your fingers brush the hat, the dust comes away in a choking cloud. You cough violently, your eyes watering. The hat tumbles to the ground, and in the chaos of dust, you catch only a glimpse of burgundy and silver before the cloud obscures your vision.

---

### **Option B: Blowing It Off**

You draw a deep breath and blow hard at the hat.

_Make a Constitution check, DC 11._

**Success:** Your breath is strong and steady. The dust erupts from the hat in a great, billowing cloud that catches the last rays of sunlight, turning momentarily golden. As it settles, the true nature of the hat is revealed—_deep burgundy felt, silver-threaded embroidery, and a magnificent silver-white feather._

The lining gleams with a maker's mark: _"Ashvale Haberdashery - Apletersia."_

**Failure:** You blow too hard. Not only does the dust explode outward in a choking, blinding cloud, but the hat itself gets caught in the wind of your breath and tumbles across the floor, landing hard against a piece of broken masonry. You're doubled over, coughing, eyes streaming, as dust settles around you like a second snow.

When your vision clears, the hat lies a few feet away, still dust-covered, the feather slightly bent.
