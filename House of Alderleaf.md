Region claimed: [[The Verdant Fringe]]
Ruler: King Rowan Alderleaf III “the Thorned,”. 
Capital: Thornhall – a fortified town built inside and around three ancient, hollowed-out world trees. Bridges of woven living branches connect the canopy courts; the throne room is literally inside the largest tree’s heartwood.

Flavor: Sylvandar is a young, aggressive frontier monarchy that only declared itself a kingdom thirty years ago. They style themselves as protectors of the “true green” against both wild overgrowth and creeping civilization. Magic is heavily regulated—any arcane casting without royal license is treated as treason. Public spellcasters are rare and usually wear iron collars that suppress their power. The realm’s soldiers are known as Thornwardens: lightly armored, highly mobile scouts and investigators who answer directly to the king

Why they control [[The Verdant Fringe]]: [[Greenhollow]] is officially a royal market town of Sylvandar. Taxes flow to Thornhall, and the king’s banner (a green oak leaf pierced by a silver thorn) flies over the toll gate. Most locals accept it because Sylvandar keeps banditry and monster incursions down… for now.

**Shadowed Branch hooks**:

- The Thornwarden PC’s first contact in Greenhollow (perhaps the steward from your Session 0 notes) was found dead with a black-rooted branch carved into his chest—right after he was going to hand over his report.
- A rival noble from Apletersia or Wathertia is already in Greenhollow trying to prove Sylvandar is too weak to control its own territory… and the cult is happily feeding both sides information to spark a border war.
