Sacris is at the manor, a cart arrives with "Hell and back - Delivery Service"

**DC for Perception/Insight/Arcana: 16**

This is deliberately _difficult_—the demon is disguised well. Here's why:

- **Perception DC 16:** The "delivery person" has almost perfect mannerisms—they walk like a tired human, their clothes are dust-worn and authentic, they even _smell_ faintly of road and sweat. But a keen eye catches: their shadow moves _wrong_ (too fluid, slightly out of sync), or their fingers are _just_ too long beneath work gloves, or when they blink, you see a flicker of amber fire where pupils should be.
    
- **Insight DC 16:** The demon's forced politeness is _too_ practiced—like recited lines from a handbook. There's no tiredness in their eyes, no actual weariness. They smile with teeth just slightly too sharp when Sacris answers a question.
    
- **Arcana DC 16:** A faint aura of brimstone and sulfur clings to them if you're attuned to magical presences. Their form flickers _minutely_ in candlelight—not quite solid.
    

**If Sacris rolls lower:** The demon passes as a tired hauler. Maybe slightly odd. Probably just a long journey. Proceed with the trap.


_The "delivery person" stands at the threshold, clipboard in hand (or whatever suits your tone)._

**"Mornin'. Got a delivery for..." _checks clipboard_ "...one Sacris? Just got three crates here. Where'd you want 'em? Inside? The large one's heavy—my boys'll handle it, but we'll need a clear path to where you want it."**

The two figures behind lumber in carrying boxes with exaggerated effort (they're _showing_, not straining). They're crude demons—barely humanoid, disguises thin as parchment. But if Sacris isn't looking closely, they're just "strong haulers."

**The 7-foot box is the trap.** It radiates an aura—warm, almost welcoming. If Sacris touches it or gets near:

**Constitution save, DC 13** to resist the pull of the magic. On a fail, they're drawn toward it. When they're close enough, the box _opens_ (the seams weren't really sealed), and a **portal of writhing red flame** unfolds inside.

**Strength save, DC 15** to resist being sucked in. On a fail, Sacris is yanked through in a rush of heat and the smell of brimstone. The box snaps shut behind them.

If Sacris _resists_, the demons drop pretense and attempt to force them in (combat ensues, or Sacris flees—up to your table's preference).


## **THE ARRIVAL IN AVERNUS**

_Sacris tumbles through the portal in a cascade of hellfire and lands—hard—in the red dust of Avernus, gasping and disoriented._

They're at the **Pit of Eternal Agony**, right at the central arena's edge.

---

## **THE PIT CAPTAIN'S ANNOUNCEMENT**

_The crowd roars. Bets fly. Soul coins change hands. The barbarian enters the arena, chest heaving, ready for glory against the best fighter the Bleeding Tankard has to offer._

**The Pit Captain**, a scarred half-orc with a voice like grinding stone, steps forward. His eyes are hungry.

**"CHAMPIONS! TODAY WE HONOR THE BLOOD WAR WITH A SPECTACLE! OUR RETURNING WARRIOR FACES... OUR _UNDEFEATED_ CHAMPION!"**

The crowd erupts. The gates on the opposite side of the arena open.

_Out walks Sacris. Confused. Singed. Blonde hair smoking slightly. Robes torn at one shoulder, still in the swaying daze of planar transit._

**The crowd goes SILENT.**

Then—chaos. Laughter. Roaring. Soul coins flip frantically as odds shift. Drunk devils hoot. A quasit squeaks from the rafters.

**The Pit Captain grins, showing teeth.**

**"Let the match BEGIN!"**


## **CHECKS TO STOP / NEGOTIATE THE FIGHT**

The party has a window—before swords clash—to intervene.

### **Option 1: Appeal to the Pit Captain's Sense of "Honor"**

**Persuasion check, DC 16** (Hard — he's bloodthirsty and loves a spectacle, even a bad one)

_"This fighter isn't ready. This isn't a fair match. Where's the honor in that?"_

**On Success:** The Pit Captain strokes his chin. _"Fair point, soft-skins. But if the _small one_ wants to forfeit..."_ He gestures to Sacris, leaving an out.

**On Failure:** _"Fair? FAIR?! This is the Pit, not a nursery rhyme! If your friend can't stand, they shouldn't enter. _FIGHT!_"_

---

### **Option 2: Bribe / Bet Against the Match**

**Persuasion + bribe, DC 14** (Medium — devils love coin and advantage)

_"Captain, what if we paid you to... find your 'champion' suddenly indisposed? Pick another fighter instead?"_

**Soul Coins Required:** 10–20 soul coins (adjust based on party wealth) or an equivalent infernal favor.

**On Success:** The Pit Captain smirks. _"Now that's a business proposition. Very well. Champion's got a sudden... cramp. We'll send in a_ different _warrior instead. One more your speed, maybe."_ (He picks a Medium-difficulty opponent instead of whatever your "undefeated champion" was.)

**On Failure:** _"Not enough, and now you've insulted my integrity. FIGHT, or forfeit your souls!"_

---

### **Option 3: Challenge the Captain's Authority**

**Intimidation or Deception, DC 17** (Very Hard — risky, defiant move)

_"The Pit Captain's judgment is _clouded_. A real warrior wouldn't hide behind a _confused sorcerer_. Where's the glory in that?"_

**On Success (Intimidation):** The captain's eyes flash with anger—then respect. _"You've got spine, I'll grant that. Fine. NEW MATCH: Your _warrior_ against my second. Prove your worth, and I'll remember it."_ (This triggers a mid-tier opponent, and if the party wins, they earn the Pit Captain's _grudging_ respect—useful for future visits.)

**On Success (Deception):** _"Hrmph. Point taken. I wasn't thinking clearly. Pit dust, perhaps. Send in the _Bloodfist Mercenary_ instead—someone who's _actually_ worthy."_ (A predetermined, fair opponent.)

**On Failure:** _"ENOUGH TALK! Either your champion fights, or you ALL fight. Choose."_

---

### **Option 4: The Desperate Gambit — Offer to Fight Instead**

**Strength check, DC 15** (Dangerous — risky, but bold)

One of the party members steps forward. _"Send me instead. I'll face your champion."_

**On Success:** The Pit Captain laughs—a booming, satisfied sound. _"NOW that's spirit! Very well, soft-skin. You go first. Show me what you've got."_ (Combat begins with a single party member vs. the "undefeated champion"—or a scaled version, depending on difficulty.)

**On Failure:** _"Ha! Brave, but foolish. You AND the little one. Make it a tag match. _FIGHT!_"_ (Two-on-one combat, stacked odds.)

---

### **Option 5: Sacris Takes Control (If He's Awake)**

**Arcana or Performance check, DC 13** (Medium — Sacris realizes the absurdity and acts)

If Sacris has enough presence of mind, he can use magic or sheer personality:

_Sacris casts **Prestidigitation** or shouts something defiant in Infernal, or does something so absurd the crowd's attention snaps to HIM rather than the fight._

**On Success:** The crowd is _delighted_. The Pit Captain is intrigued. _"The little one's got fire! I like it. Tell you what—you face our _second_ fighter, not our champion. Earn a victory, and I'll buy you a drink."_

**On Failure:** The crowd boos. The Pit Captain is unimpressed. _"Enough showboating. FIGHT or FORFEIT!"_


## **THE BLEEDING TANKARD SOCIAL CONSEQUENCE**

After the dust settles, regardless of outcome:

- **If the party intervened successfully:** Graz'ztak notices. He raises an eyebrow from behind the bar. _"Interesting. The soft-skins have manners. I'll remember that."_ (They gain a measure of respect; future services may cost less or come with better information.)
    
- **If they failed and fought:** The crowd respects whoever won (or came close). Reputation rises among the rabble—but the Pit Captain expects them again soon.
    
- **If Sacris nearly died:** Graz'ztak ensures he's patched up (for a price, of course). _"Your friend's lucky we needed a show. Next time, be more careful with my guests."_
    

---

**One final touch:** Once things calm, have Sacris realize he has **no idea how he got here**—the last thing he remembers is "furniture delivery people." The party must piece together that he's been _transported_ to Avernus and that whoever pulled him here _had a reason._
