_Legendary greataxe (requires attunement by a barbarian)_

A massive, black-iron greataxe with a blade forged from the petrified heart of a dead world. Glowing red runes pulse along the edge like bleeding wounds. The haft is wrapped in chains made from the manacles of the first Heretic bound by the circle.

**Properties**

- You gain a **+3 bonus** to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
- While raging, your melee weapon attacks with the Worldreaver score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.
- **Planar Cleave** (3/day): As a bonus action while raging, your next attack this turn deals an extra 6d10 force damage. If the target is a creature native to another plane or using teleportation/portals, it must succeed on a DC 20 Charisma saving throw or be banished to its home plane for 1 minute.

**Rage of the Sealer** While attuned and raging, you gain resistance to damage from spells and advantage on saving throws against planar magic (e.g. _gate_, _plane shift_).

**Sentience** Intelligence 16, Wisdom 20, Charisma 14. Lawful Neutral. Speaks telepathically in a deep, grinding voice. Desires to close all unauthorized planar travel and “reave the worlds that should not touch.”

**Curse**

- You cannot attune to any other weapon.
- Each time you score a critical hit, you must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or fly into an uncontrollable rage for 1 extra minute (attacking the nearest creature, even allies).
- The axe slowly turns your skin into living stone (cosmetic at first, then -5 ft speed after 30 days).
