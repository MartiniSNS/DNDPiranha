Fourth tier of [[The Shadowed Branch]]

**Initiates** — New converts or those bound by debt, desperation, or dark promise.

- **Role:** Perform tasks assigned by Hands (deliver messages, keep watch, gather intelligence).
- **Characteristics:** Often ordinary folk—desperate nobles, indebted merchants, blackmailed commoners—who serve out of fear or the promise of wealth.
- **Loyalty:** Weakest link; may be turned or broken under pressure.
- **Knowledge:** Know only their immediate handler; unaware of the true scope of the cult.

**Example Initiates:**

- A miller's son who was threatened into carrying coded letters.
- A town clerk who was blackmailed and now reports to the Whisperer in his region.
- A desperate merchant who agreed to smuggle relics in exchange for settling a debt.
