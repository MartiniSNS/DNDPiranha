### **Traits**

**Ritual Compulsion.** Aldwyn is under the effect of a powerful enchantment from the barrow's ritual. He moves toward [[Oakbarrow]] at a steady pace and will not deviate from the path unless forcibly restrained or the compulsion is broken (see "Breaking the Compulsion" below).

Any attempt to turn Aldwyn around or prevent his movement requires:

- A successful **grapple check (DC 11)** to physically restrain him, or
- A spell or ability that breaks enchantment (e.g., _Dispel Magic_ DC 14, _Remove Curse_, _Greater Restoration_).

If grappled but not magically freed, Aldwyn struggles passively—he does not fight back but will slip free and resume walking toward the barrow if restraint is released.

**Pale Eyes.** Aldwyn's eyes have turned milky-white from the ritual's touch. He is **blind** to anything more than 5 feet away, but he can sense the direction of [[Oakbarrow]] as if by supernatural instinct. He navigates toward it unerringly, even in darkness.

**Slow Reflexes.** Aldwyn's movements are dreamlike and sluggish. He has disadvantage on all Dexterity saving throws and Initiative rolls.
