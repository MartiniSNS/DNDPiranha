# 💀 **Stat Block (CR 3 Mini‑Boss)**

Medium humanoid (human), chaotic evil

**AC:** 16 (reinforced leather + spirit ward) **HP:** 62 (8d8+24) **Speed:** 30 ft

**STR 16 (+3)** **DEX 12 (+1)** **CON 16 (+3)** **INT 10 (+0)** **WIS 12 (+1)** **CHA 12 (+1)**

**Saving Throws:** Str +5, Con +5, Wis +3 **Skills:** Intimidation +3, Athletics +5, Perception +3 **Damage Resistances:** necrotic **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 13 **Languages:** Common **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP)

# 🪓 **Actions**

### **Spirit‑Charged Hammer** _(Melee Weapon Attack)_

+5 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target. **Hit:** 11 (1d10+3) bludgeoning + **4 (1d8) necrotic**. Target must succeed on a **DC 13 Strength save** or be **knocked prone**.

### **Reckless Spirit Surge (Recharge 5–6)**

Jarn channels the spirit inside him.

- Gains **advantage** on all attacks this turn
    
- Deals an extra **1d8 necrotic** per hit
    
- Emits a 10‑ft aura of flickering green light
    
- All creatures in the aura must make a **DC 12 Wis save** or be **frightened** until the end of their next turn
    

All attacks against him have advantage until his next turn.

### **Shadow Lash (Bonus Action)**

Jarn lashes out with a tendril of shadow.

- **Range:** 15 ft
    
- **Attack:** +5 to hit
    
- **Hit:** 7 (1d6+3) necrotic
    
- Target’s speed is reduced by 10 ft until the end of its next turn
    

### **Wolf Command (Bonus Action)**

If a wolf is within 20 ft:

- Wolf moves up to 15 ft
    
- Wolf makes a bite attack as a reaction
    
- Wolf gains **advantage** on that attack
    

# 🩸 **Traits**

### **Spirit Ward**

When Jarn is reduced to 0 HP, he instead drops to 1 HP once per day. A burst of green light erupts (no damage), and he screams:

> “The Collector isn’t done with me!”

### **Branch Ritual Mark**

Jarn’s shadow moves independently for a moment when he’s angry. Once per round, he can impose **disadvantage** on one attack against him.

### **Pack Tactics (with Wolves)**

Jarn gains advantage on attack rolls if a wolf is within 5 ft of the target.
