[[3-Scene 2]]

Ambush / Tension Beat (combat or chase) (20–45 min) Purpose: Show the cult is active and willing to act quickly. Give a short, manageable combat/chase that stresses consequences for failure.

Trigger options (pick one based on table tone) 

A) Ambush in the tavern (brash)

- Two [[Cult Hand]] (use cult hand statblocks below; equivalent to two bandits/cultists) burst in, attempt to grab Ciaran and silence him.
- Combat starts; fighting in crowd risks civilian harm (Maela and patrons try to duck; use simple rules: crowd DC 12 Dex saves to avoid being shoved).

B) Street pursuit (subtle)

- A cloaked figure exits after Ciaran leaves. If PCs follow stealthily (Stealth check DC 12), they can tail to an alley and force a brief chase/combat with one or two Initiates (cultists).
- If they fail to follow, Ciaran is followed and threatened later (future consequence).

Combat tactics

- Hands try nonlethal capture if possible (to interrogate Ciaran). If PCs resist, Hands escalate.
- Use simple environment hazards: overturned tables, a swinging lantern (Dex DC 12 to avoid splash of hot oil), low rafters (half cover).

Consequences

- If PCs protect Ciaran: he’s grateful, reveals a second clue (a cipher key or a map scribble pointing to Blackford).
- If Ciaran is captured or escapes injured: the party must decide to pursue immediately (risks) or regroup (longer arc; Maela provides a safe loft and a contact).
