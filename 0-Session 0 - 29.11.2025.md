# Session 0 Template — Whispers of the Shadowed Branch
(DM voice: Gather round. This session will bind our story together before the road opens.)

Purpose: Align players and DM on tone, rules, safety, character creation, and initial plot seeds so play starts smoothly.

---

ESSENTIALS TO COVER / SCRIPT SUGGESTIONS

1) Campaign Pitch
- "Whispers of the Shadowed Branch" — You begin in Greenhollow, a market town on the Verdant Fringe. Small mysteries become threads to a secretive cult, the Shadowed Branch, which manipulates nobles with blackmail. Expect investigation, social intrigue, and rituals that may threaten the Weave. We play light-heartedly, but choices carry cost.

2) Safety Tools
- X‑Card: Any player taps it and the scene is gently reframed/removed. No questions asked.
- Lines (hard limits) & Veils (soft limits). Examples to ask players:
  - Lines: sexual violence, child abuse, real‑world political extremism? (Yes/No)
  - Veils: graphic death, rituals described in detail?
- Pause/Time‑out: Anyone can request a short break.

1) Magic & Historical Note
- Two centuries ago, a necromancer cabal tore a rift, nearly destroying the world; the rift was bound at great cost. Magic is far less common now; arcane wells are hidden. Spellcasters face admiration in some places and distrust in others. Public spellcasting can draw danger.

1) House Rules
- Milestone levelling only.
- Reputation by region: Accepted / Tolerated / Feared — affects NPC reactions and markets.
- Homebrew: Allowed with DM approval. Submit concepts 48 hours before session.

5) Character Creation Rules
- Starting level: 1
- Ability generation: Standard Array / Point Buy / Rolling (pick one).
- Allowed sources: PHB + X (specify). No class/option X without DM ok.
- Backgrounds: Encourage ties to region or NPCs (Maela, steward, lord).
- Alignment: Optional; flavour only.
- Magic users: Note social consequences and suggested in‑character explanations for flashy effects.

6) Player Deliverables (to DM before Session 1)
- Full character sheet (digital or scanned).
- Short backstory (3–6 sentences).
- Two personal goals (short-term and long-term).
- Three bonds/contacts:
  - Bond A: local contact in Verdant Fringe (e.g., Maela Fern).
  - Bond B: one potential patron or rival in Everspring or Wathertia.
  - Bond C: a secret (something character fears being revealed).
- Consent to table safety rules.

7) Party Bonds Template (fill at Session 0)
- "I owe [Name] a favour because ___." (who/why)
- "I distrust [Name/Institution] because ___."
- "If the Shadowed Branch threatens my home, I will ___."
(Write 3–5 shared bonds. DM keeps a printed copy.)

8) Hooks DM Assigns (examples)
- Maela Fern slips you a coded charm hidden in a stew bowl.
- A steward begs for secrecy after a rider passed with a sealed envelope.
- A ledger page washes up with the tide — it names a local noble and a rune.

9) Logistics & Expectations
- Session length & cadence (e.g., weekly, 3–4 hours).
- Attendance & notice policy (24 hours notice for absence).
- Tools: Virtual Tabletop (if any), dice, character sheet platform.
- Communication: Where to post absences, schedule swaps (Discord/Group DM).
- Food & breaks (if in-person).

10) Pregens & Backup Characters
- Provide 1–2 pregenerated characters or ask players to bring a backup (level 1).
- Keep pregens on hand for probable early‑death or if a player cannot attend.

11) Downtime & Player Agency
- Describe downtime options: research in Everspring, crafting wards, repaying favors (Maela).
- Encourage roleplay and investigation; reward creative use of resources.

12) Recording & Content Sharing
- Ask players whether sessions will be recorded or notes shared.
- If recording: require consent.

CHECKLIST — FOR PLAYERS (bring these at Session 1)
- Character sheet (filled).
- Backstory (3–6 sentences).
- Chosen bonds and contacts.
- Any custom homebrew request sent to DM.
- Ability to roll or digital dice, depending on platform.

CHECKLIST — FOR DM (before Session 1)
- Prepare 3 encounter seeds for Greenhollow.
- Pregens for 1–2 classes.
- NPC statblocks (Maela, steward, tollkeeper).
- Map of Greenhollow & Whispering Oak (handout).
- Print Session 0 bonds and at least one party goal.
- Safety items (X‑Card on table or digital equivalent).

QUESTIONS TO ASK PLAYERS??
- Where did your character grow up? Why did they leave/stay?
- How do you feel about magic after hearing the story of the rift 200 years ago?
- Who in Greenhollow would you trust with your life? Why?
- What is one secret you would rather keep at all costs?
- What would make your character consider doing something morally grey?

SESSION 0 SIGN‑OFF
- Reiterate safety tools and return to any unsettled questions.
- Each player reads their short backstory and one bond aloud.
- Players sign (verbally or via chat) to confirm understanding of house rules and safety limits.

Play well — the roots run deep, and curious hands find both coin and knives.
