## Potion Creation Rules (for your campaign)

[](https://github.com/copilot/c/96e58881-9358-4685-bbb4-ff28b662517f#potion-creation-rules-for-your-campaign)

**Crafting Time:**

- Common (Tier 1): 1 day per potion
- Uncommon (Tier 2): 3 days per potion
- Rare (Tier 3): 1 week per potion
- Very Rare+ (Tier 4): 1 month per potion

**Crafting DC:**

- Tier 1: DC 10 (Alchemist tools)
- Tier 2: DC 13
- Tier 3: DC 15
- Tier 4: DC 18+

**Cost Reduction:** Gathering your own ingredients from the wilderness (Alchemist's Kit + Herbalism Kit checks) can reduce material costs by up to 50%. Dangerous ingredients (from monsters, rare plants) may require expedition or negotiation.

**Shelf Life:**

- Tier 1: 1 month
- Tier 2: 3 months
- Tier 3: 1 year
- Tier 4: 10 years or indefinite
