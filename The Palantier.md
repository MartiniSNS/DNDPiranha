_Legendary wondrous item (requires attunement by a wizard)_

This perfectly spherical orb is slightly larger than a human fist. It appears to be carved from a single piece of flawless starlit crystal shot through with threads of liquid gold and shifting constellations. When held, faint whispers in long-dead arcane languages can sometimes be heard from within. It constantly radiates a soft silvery light and feels ice-cold to the touch.

**Properties** While attuned to the Palantier, it functions as a **perfect arcane focus** for your wizard spells. You gain the following benefits:

### Arcane Focus Mastery

- Your spell attack rolls and spell save DCs for wizard spells you cast gain a **+3 bonus**.
- You may use the Palantier as your spellcasting focus even if a spell normally requires material components with a gold cost (the Palantier consumes a tiny amount of its own stored power instead).
- You have **advantage** on all ability checks made to maintain concentration on wizard spells.

### Scrying & Far-Seeing

As an action, you can cast the _scrying_ spell (save DC 20) without expending a spell slot or requiring material components. You can do this a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 2). You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

Additionally, while holding the Palantier you can cast _clairvoyance_ and _arcane eye_ at will (no spell slot required).

### Planar Revelation

Once per long rest, you can use an action to cause the Palantier to reveal hidden planar truths. Choose one of the following effects:

- **Portal Sight**: You instantly know the location of the nearest permanent or semi-permanent portal within 10 miles, what plane it leads to, and any visible guardians or traps.
- **True Seeing**: For the next 10 minutes you gain the benefits of a _true seeing_ spell.
- **Sealing Memory**: You can cast _forbiddance_ (save DC 20) once without expending a spell slot or material components. The spell is centered on the Palantier and lasts for its full duration without concentration.

### Visions of the Far-Seer

Each time you finish a long rest while attuned, roll a d20. On a 1–5, the Palantier shows you a disturbing vision of a distant place, time, or possible future. The DM chooses or rolls on a table of cryptic visions (or invents one). These visions often contain useful clues but are never entirely clear.

### Sentience & Curse

The Palantier is **sentient** (Intelligence 22, Wisdom 18, Charisma 20). It can speak telepathically with its attuned user and has hearing and darkvision out to 120 feet. Its alignment is **Lawful Neutral**. It desires to seal dangerous planar rifts and prevent unauthorized travel between worlds.

**Curse**: The Palantier is jealous of other sources of power. While attuned:

- You cannot attune to any other magic item that grants a bonus to spell attack rolls or spell save DC.
- Each time you cast a spell of 6th level or higher, you must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or suffer one level of exhaustion as the orb drains part of your life force to fuel its ancient purpose.
- Powerful entities (archdevils, celestials, or great old ones) can sense when the Palantier is actively used and may become aware of your location.

**Destruction**: The Palantier can only be destroyed by being cast into the River Styx at its source or by the direct intervention of a deity of magic.
