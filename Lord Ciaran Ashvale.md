## Role
Disgraced noble, fugitive from [[The Shadowed Branch]] cult

## Description
Lord Ciaran Ashvale is a once-prominent noble from the capital, now living in secret under an assumed name. He is in his early forties, with sharp features and haunted blue eyes that hint at sleepless nights. His dark hair is streaked with premature grey, and his clothes, though finely made, are worn and travel-stained.

## Background
Ciaran was a trusted advisor at court until he uncovered the cult’s blackmail schemes targeting the royal family and several noble houses. After refusing to cooperate and narrowly escaping an assassination attempt, he fled to Greenhollow, seeking allies who might help expose the cult and restore justice.

## Personality & Quirks
- Wary and cautious, always scanning for threats.
- Intelligent and persuasive, but slow to trust.
- Keeps a journal full of coded notes and evidence against the cult.
- Often fidgets with a signet ring, the last token of his former status.

## Motivation
Ciaran is desperate to protect himself and the few loyal friends he has left. He hopes to recruit capable adventurers to investigate cult activities, uncover more evidence, and ultimately confront the cult’s leaders.

## Connections
- Secretly communicates with a handful of old allies in the capital.
- Knows several cult members by name and face but fears their reach.
- Has ties to Greenhollow’s officials, who may or may not know his true identity.

## Role in the Campaign
- Sent the mysterious letters to the party, summoning them to The Whispering Oak.
- Can provide the first clues about the cult’s blackmail and the danger facing the kingdom.
- May act as a patron, quest-giver, or even a reluctant mentor to the group.
