[[1-The Gathering at The Whispering Oak]]
Tavern Arrival & Spotlight (20–40 min)

Purpose: Set mood, spotlight each PC, introduce Maela and Ciaran, hand out the letter handout.

Setup

- PCs arrive separately. Encourage one-line entries and an immediate observable (what they order or what they notice).
- NPCs present:[[1-Maela Fern - Barmaid of The Whispering Oak]] , 2–4 locals, a traveling merchant in the corner, and [[Lord Ciaran Ashvale]] (later).

Player spotlight prompts

- Ask each player: “What drew you to [[The Whispering Oak]] tonight?” (rich roleplay lead).
- Allow short skill uses: Perception (Spot a lurking watcher), Insight (judge other’s intent), Investigation (notice coded marks).

Mechanics & DCs (guidelines)

- Passive Perception to notice oddities: typical 10. Active Perception DC 12 to notice a cloaked figure at the edge of the tavern.
- Persuasion DC 12 to coax Maela into discreet conversation (or pay 1–2 sp).
- Insight DC 13 to realize the letter’s handwriting shows haste or a disguised seal.

Maela (in-play flavour)

- Use these lines when PCs interact:
    - Warm: “Welcome, strangers, stew’s been on the fire all day. Sit. If you need a quiet corner, I can make one.”
    - Wary: “If you’re trouble, I ask you to leave it at the door. Roots here run deep, and I protect my regulars.”

Handout A — The Letter (give to each player)

> “Opportunity awaits you at the Whispering Oak in Greenhollow. Meet at dusk. Tell no one. C.A.”

If a player examines the stationary or envelope (Investigation DC 12): find small smear of sap and a faint mark that resembles [[Cult Symbol]] (Faded)
