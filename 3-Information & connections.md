
[[1-Maela Fern - Barmaid of The Whispering Oak]]

- Rumors and gossip: free if you listen; sharper, useful leads cost coin or a favour (suggest 1–2 sp for a local rumour, 1–2 gp for a specific lead). Maela knows who’s been asking about coded letters and who’s moved oddly at night.
- Local contacts: she can introduce you to regulars (stablehands, a travelling merchant, the miller) who owe her small favours — useful for tracking a witness or tailing a rider.
- Discreet meetings: reserve a tree‑alcove or a private corner in the loft for a confidential talk. For total privacy (kitchen storeroom after hours), negotiate a higher fee or return a favour.
- Archive leads: she can point you toward people in [[Everspring]] or the minor lord at [[Blackford]] who might hold records or influence.
