_Legendary longbow (requires attunement by a ranger)_

A graceful recurved bow made of silvered yew and demonbone, strung with a single thread of pure Styx water that never breaks. Arrows nocked in it shimmer with starlight.

**Properties**

- **+3 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
- Your ranged attacks ignore cover and deal an extra 2d8 radiant + 2d8 force damage to creatures from other planes.
- **Pierce the Veil** (3/day): As an action, fire an arrow that creates a temporary 10-ft wide, 60-ft long tunnel through any barrier, wall, or even a _forcecage_ for 1 minute. Creatures inside the tunnel are exposed to the destination plane’s environment.

**Hunter’s Sight** You can cast _hunter’s mark_, _locate creature_, and _locate object_ at will without expending slots. You always know the direction and distance to the nearest active planar portal within 100 miles.

**Sentience** Intelligence 18, Wisdom 22, Charisma 16. Neutral. Calm, precise, and coldly logical. Wants portals sealed to preserve “the natural order of the hunt.”

**Curse**

- You cannot benefit from any other magic bow or ranged weapon.
- Each time you use Piercing the Veil, you must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or gain a level of exhaustion as the bow drinks your life force to stabilize the tunnel.
- Animals and fey instinctively fear you and may attack on sight.
