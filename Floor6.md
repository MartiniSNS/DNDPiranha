**Theme:** The Sixth Floor is the **Chamber of the Whisperer**, the largest and most imposing level of the Vault so far. It is a sprawling, multi‑zone floor built around a colossal central arena where the players finally confront **The Whisperer** — the ancient intelligence that has been shaping memories, illusions, and guardians throughout the Vault.

### Layout

A vast circular floor with multiple outer sectors feeding into a massive central arena. The Whisperer’s influence permeates everything: whispers echo unnaturally, shadows move against the light, and memories flicker at the edges of vision.

- **A. Outer Ring Corridors** — Wide stone passages lined with whispering runes.
    
- **B. Memory Vaults** — Four large chambers containing unstable memory constructs.
    
- **C. Echo Spires** — Tall pillars that amplify sound and distort perception.
    
- **D. The Whisperer’s Arena** — A colossal, circular chamber with a raised dais, shifting platforms, and a swirling vortex of threads and echoes at its centre.
    
- **E. The Descent Gate** — A sealed exit that only opens once the Whisperer is defeated or pacified.
    

### Outer Ring Corridors

These corridors form a full loop around the arena, giving players multiple paths inward.

- **Hazards:**
    
    - Whisper‑runes that trigger psychic pulses if stepped on.
        
    - Echo illusions that mimic party members’ voices.
        
- **Encounters:**
    
    - **Echo Shades** — shadowy constructs that mimic a player’s last spoken sentence before attacking.
        
    - **Memory Wraiths** — remnants of failed custodians.
        

### Memory Vaults

Four large chambers spaced evenly around the ring. Each vault contains a different type of memory construct.

- **Vault of Joy** — Illusions of happy memories that turn hostile when touched.
    
- **Vault of Fear** — Phantoms that force Wisdom saves to avoid panic.
    
- **Vault of Regret** — Animated regrets that drain Strength or Charisma.
    
- **Vault of Resolve** — A trial chamber where players must stand firm against psychic pressure.
    

**Purpose:** Each vault weakens one of the Whisperer’s defences. Clearing all four grants advantage on certain checks during the final confrontation.

### Echo Spires

Three towering stone pillars surround the arena’s outer edge.

- **Effect:**
    
    - They amplify sound, making whispers audible across the floor.
        
    - They distort perception, causing disadvantage on Perception checks within 20 ft.
        
- **Interaction:**
    
    - Destroying a spire reduces the Whisperer’s lair actions.
        
    - Climbing a spire (Athletics DC 15) reveals hidden runes that offer clues to the Whisperer’s origin.
        

### The Whisperer’s Arena

A massive circular chamber with a raised central dais and shifting stone platforms that move unpredictably.

- **Atmosphere:**
    
    - The air vibrates with whispers.
        
    - Threads of silver and shadow swirl around the dais.
        
    - Lanterns flicker as if reacting to unseen breaths.
        
- **Arena Features:**
    
    - **Shifting Platforms:** Move every round; falling risks 2d6 bludgeoning damage.
        
    - **Memory Vortex:** A swirling column of threads that the Whisperer uses to manipulate memories.
        
    - **Echo Pools:** Shallow pools that replay fragments of past events when stepped in.
        

### The Whisperer

A towering, semi‑ethereal entity woven from threads of memory, shadow, and echo. Its form constantly shifts, showing glimpses of faces, places, and moments from the party’s past.

- **Nature:**
    
    - Not fully physical.
        
    - Speaks in layered whispers.
        
    - Seeks to “curate” the party’s memories.
        
- **Goals:**
    
    - To test the party’s resolve.
        
    - To claim one memory from each player.
        
    - To determine whether they are worthy to descend further.
        
- **Encounter Options:**
    
    - **Combat:** A multi‑phase boss fight with lair actions tied to Echo Spires.
        
    - **Negotiation:** Players may bargain memories for safe passage.
        
    - **Trial:** A skill‑based challenge where players confront illusions of their past.
        

### Lair Actions

The Whisperer acts on initiative count 20.

- **Echo Pulse:** All creatures must succeed on a Wisdom save or take psychic damage.
    
- **Memory Twist:** One player sees an illusion of a past event and must pass an Insight check to resist.
    
- **Thread Snare:** Threads lash out, restraining a creature until they pass a Strength save.
    

Destroying Echo Spires reduces the number of lair actions.![[Floor6-map.png]]
