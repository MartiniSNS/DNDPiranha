## Symbol Description
The symbol of [[The Shadowed Branch]] cult is a **twisted black branch**, stylized to resemble both a tree limb and a grasping hand. The branch curves into a rough circle, its tips ending in thorn-like points. Hidden among the gnarled twists is a subtle, almost imperceptible eye—suggesting the cult’s watchful gaze and secret knowledge.

- **Appearance:**  
  Often drawn in charcoal or ink, carved into wood, or sewn discreetly into clothing and banners.
- **Colour Palette:**  
  Deep black, faded Gray, and hints of dark green.

## Context in the Adventure
- Players may find the symbol etched onto tavern tables, marked in the dirt outside noble houses, or hidden in coded letters.
- The symbol can serve as a warning, a calling card, or a secret sign to cult members.
