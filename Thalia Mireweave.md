Master Tailor of [[Everspring]]

Owner of [[The Needle & Quill - Tailors]]

Overview
Thalia Mireweave is a respected tailor and discreet fixer for Everspring’s merchants and minor gentry. She makes fine garments, repairs travel-worn cloaks, and—if you know how to ask—sews secrets into hems. She’s witty, exacting, and loves a well-cut suit as much as a well-kept secret.

Appearance & Personality
- Early 50s, silver-streaked dark hair in a tight bun, needle-marks on her fingers. Always wears a patched but immaculate waistcoat.
- Precise, patient, and quietly amused. Good listener; remembers faces and fashions.
- Fiercely professional: she values her reputation and privacy. She dislikes brash behavior in her shop.

Shop Layout (small hand-map)
- Front: small display of ready garments, curtained fitting alcove.
- Workroom (back): large table, sewing machines (foot-powered), bolts of cloth, dyes, boxes of threads and hidden trinkets.
- Loft: one small rental room for discreet visitors.
- Wall safe: behind a tapestry—used for rare materials or clients who want secrecy.
