[[1-Maela Fern - Barmaid of The Whispering Oak]] (true form: changeling)
Medium humanoid (changeling), Neutral Good
Armor Class 12 (leather)
Hit Points 22 (5d8)
Speed 30 ft.

STR 10 (+0)  DEX 14 (+2)  CON 12 (+1)  INT 12 (+1)  WIS 14 (+2)  CHA 16 (+3)

Saving Throws — Wis +4
Skills Insight +5, Perception +4, Persuasion +5, Deception +5, Medicine +4
Senses passive Perception 14
Languages Common
Challenge 1/8 (25 XP)

Traits
• Changeling Shapechanger. As an action, Maela can change her appearance and voice. She can appear as any humanoid of her size class; the change lasts until she uses this trait again or dismisses it. Physical traits such as hair, eye colour, facial features, height (within small bounds), and apparent minor scars can be altered. The change is not magical in the sense of spell effects, but it is uncanny; creatures that touch her may notice thin seams or oddly malleable skin — treat touch as a clue (see DM: detection below).

• Secretive by Habit. Maela is practiced in concealment for social safety. While in a chosen disguise she gains advantage on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Persuasion) checks intended to pass as the persona she’s assumed.

• Warm Hospitality. Maela can use an action to stabilise a dying creature without a Medicine check. If the party has a friendly relationship with her, she will do this for free.

• Local Networks (1/day). Maela can call in a small favour from a local contact (stablehand, ferryman, town guard contact, etc.). The contact appears within a short time and grants the party advantage on one social or info-gathering check related to the Fringe (DM decides exact use).

• Disguise Kit Proficiency. Maela is proficient with disguise kit tools; she routinely augments her shapechanging with clothes, wigs, and props.

Actions
• Dagger. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 1d4 + 2 piercing damage.
• Help (Bonus Action). Maela can use the Help action as a bonus action to grant an ally advantage on the next attack roll or ability check (within 5 ft).

Reactions
• Fleeing Grace. If a hostile combatant focuses on her and she is outnumbered, Maela can use her reaction to Disengage and move up to half her speed without provoking opportunity attacks (roleplay of slipping between patrons).

Roleplay / DM Notes
- Maela hides being a changeling deliberately. In Greenhollow she usually presents as a warm, ordinary young woman with auburn hair. She keeps the changeling truth to a single trusted contact (or none — up to the DM).
- She uses her shapechanging mostly for safety, gathering gossip incognito, or when the tavern needs a different face (e.g., pretending to be a travelling merchant to hear coins talk).
- Maela avoids overt magic: she will not cast or display spellcasting in public. If pressed, she downplays it and offers mundane explanations (a good seamstress, a knack for mimicry).

Detecting Maela’s Nature (DM guidance)
- Passive discovery is unlikely. If an NPC or player actively investigates, use the following DCs:
  - DC 15 Insight or Investigation to suspect she is not who she claims to be (not definitive).
  - DC 15 Investigation or DC 13 Perception to notice small physical oddities (seams at wrists/neck, slightly wrong fingerprints).
  - A successful DC 15 Insight check against Maela’s Deception (she has +5) or a direct contested Deception vs. Wisdom (Insight) check reveals her changeling nature.
- Touch or forced close examination (medical exam, forensic) grants advantage on the unmasking check; prolonged scrutiny (10+ minutes) reduces the DC by 2.

Consequences of Discovery
- If revealed knowingly by Maela (she admits it): townsfolk reactions depend on rapport. If she’s trusted, many forgive or accept her as “Maela” who’s kept them safe; if suspicious, some NPCs react with fear or superstition (Persuasion to placate).
- If exposed publicly (someone reveals it in the tavern): a Persuasion/Performance DC 12 may calm the crowd; failure could draw the watch or jeering and loss of some favors (Maela’s Local Networks trait might be temporarily limited).
- [[The Shadowed Branch]] would view a changeling who can hide identity as valuable for recruitment or blackmail—Maela’s secrecy increases her danger if cult operatives suspect her.
