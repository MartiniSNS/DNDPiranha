

**What Your Party Sees:**

A strikingly beautiful figure emerges from the shadows—tall, regal, with skin the color of burnt amber and eyes like molten gold. Black wings fold against her back. She wears form-fitting armor of infernal steel, adorned with chains that move of their own accord.

Her name is **Captain Serath**.

---

### **ERINYES — "Captain Serath"**

**Appearance:**

- 6'2", lithe, devastatingly attractive (Charisma 18)
- Black hair braided with silver chains
- A rapier at her side that seems to drink in the light
- Scars along her arms—marks from celestial combat

**Personality:**

- Intelligent, cruel, seductive
- Uses charm as much as force
- Enjoys psychological games and manipulation
- Actually keeps her word, which makes her dangerous

**Her Role:** Captain of the Internal Guard—she hunts traitors, escapees, and those who don't belong in Nessus Hold.

**Demeanor on Approach:**

_She circles your party slowly, wings unfurled slightly, a smile playing at her lips._

> _"How delightful. Newcomers. Or fugitives? Let me guess—you're not entirely sure which one you are anymore, are you?"_
> 
> _She extends a hand, and the chains around her wrist jingle softly._
> 
> _"Come now. Tell me true, and perhaps I'll let you walk away."_

**What She Wants:**

- The truth (she can sense lies with magical perception)
- Amusement (she enjoys clever deception attempts)
- Information (what brings outsiders to the city?)
- A reason not to drag you to Zariel's dungeons

**Potential Interactions:**

**If they're honest:**

- She's genuinely impressed. _"How refreshing. Honesty in Hell. You're either very brave or very stupid."_
- She might let them pass, but not before extracting a promise: _"A favor, then. I'll collect it when the time is right."_
- Now they're indebted to an Erinyes—which is worse than being enslaved

**If they try to deceive her:**

- She knows. Immediately.
- _"Oh, that's adorable. You think I can't taste a lie? Here's the truth: you're going to come with me, or I'm going to hurt you. Both options end with you where I want you."_
- **Insight DC 18** to read her and understand she might actually respect a bold bluff
- Combat becomes likely unless they offer something valuable

**If they flirt/seduce:**

- She laughs—a genuinely delighted sound.
- _"Brave. I like it. But no—I don't do attachments. However, if you amuse me enough..."_
- She might spare them, test them, or invite them to her quarters (where the real interrogation happens)

**If they mention a name or faction:**

- Her expression hardens. _"Ah. So you're involved in that business, are you? How much do you know? And more importantly—how loyal are you?"_
- Depending on the faction, she's either interested in recruiting them or executing them

**Combat Stats (if it comes to that):**

- AC 17, HP 66
- Attacks: Longsword +7 (1d8+4 slashing) or Whip +7 (1d4+4 slashing, can grapple)
- Spellcasting: _Fireball_, _Polymorph_, _Invisible_ (at will)
- Reactions: Can take an action after each creature's turn
- **Special:** If reduced below 25 HP, she tries to escape rather than fight to the death
