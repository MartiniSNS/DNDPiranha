### Tapestry Revelation

The tapestry peels back a corner of its woven scene and a soft voice, like cloth rubbing on cloth, speaks.

> **“You came back. You always come back.”**

The woven image plays a memory: a rain‑slick street, a shouted name, a hand slipping away. The scene lasts 30 seconds and is visible to everyone in the room. The tapestry’s voice is neither cruel nor kind — it simply records.

### Tapestry Offer

After the memory finishes, the tapestry tightens its weave and offers a choice in the same soft voice.

> **“I keep what is given. I can hold this for you, mend it, or trade it. What will you give in return?”**

**Options the tapestry proposes**

- **Hold** — The tapestry stores the memory in a glass orb for later retrieval.
    
    - _Mechanic:_ Player makes a **DC 14 Wisdom save**. On success the memory is stored safely in an **Echo‑Glass Vial**; on failure the memory is fragmented and a **Memory Phantom** forms (see encounter).
        
- **Mend** — The tapestry will alter the memory to soothe the pain, at a cost.
    
    - _Mechanic:_ Player makes a **DC 15 Charisma (Performance) or Wisdom (Insight) check**. On success the memory becomes less traumatic; the player gains **advantage on one social check** related to that memory for 24 hours. On failure the memory is altered in a way that creates doubt (disadvantage on one related check for 24 hours).
        
- **Trade** — The tapestry will give a clue or boon in exchange for a different memory.
    
    - _Mechanic:_ Player chooses a minor memory to relinquish (player‑chosen or roll on a table). The tapestry grants a **useful clue** about the Reliquary Loom or a **Time‑Touched Trinket**. The lost memory is gone for 24 hours.
        

### Player Choices and Roleplay Prompts

- **Reluctant Sacrifice** — The PC hesitates, fingers brushing the threads. Describe what the memory smells like and why it matters.
    
- **Bargainer** — The PC tries to haggle: what will the tapestry accept instead? Offer a personal item, a secret, or a promise.
    
- **Defiant** — The PC refuses. The tapestry tightens; the woven face frowns. The room chills.
    

### Mechanical Resolution

- **If the player succeeds** on the chosen check/save: describe the memory’s final form (sealed vial, softened recollection, or exchanged memory) and grant the mechanical benefit.
    
- **If the player fails:** immediately spawn a **Memory Phantom** (use the stat block provided earlier). The phantom reenacts the memory and attacks or haunts the PC for 1–3 rounds. The tapestry keeps a fragment of the memory and murmurs a taunt as it reseals.
    
- **If the player bargains and offers a secret:** require a **DC 13 Insight** from the tapestry (GM roll in secret). If the tapestry accepts, it gives a specific clue tied to the Reliquary Loom’s sequence or the location of the Threaded Codex.
    

### Aftermath Hooks

- **Saved Memory:** The Echo‑Glass Vial can be replayed later to solve a puzzle or to comfort an NPC.
    
- **Mended Memory:** The PC gains a roleplay beat — a quieter moment of closure or a new doubt to explore.
    
- **Traded Memory:** The party receives a tangible clue or item that advances the floor’s puzzles.
    
- **Phantom Spawned:** The encounter may reveal deeper ties between the Vault and the PC’s past; the tapestry may later call for the memory’s return.
