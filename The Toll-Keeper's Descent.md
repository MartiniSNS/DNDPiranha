# The Toll-Keeper's Descent: A 10-Session Arc

## Thematic Overview

Your party has claimed The Palantier, a key of scrying and memory. They now stand at the threshold between worlds. The journey forward will unearth the workings of The Toll-Keeper's Master—an overseer who trades in secrets through coded manifestos, a puppet-master whose strings run through Blackford's very commerce. The Shadowed Branch is merely the puppet; The Toll-Keeper's Master is the hand that makes it dance.

The party needs 2 more Artifacts:
- The Bulwark Eternal (protection against planar breach)
- The Worldreaver (severance of planar bonds)

Each artifact will draw them closer to understanding that there is not just one Whisperer in the Vault, but an entire hierarchy of whispering entities—and the Toll-Keeper's Master is one such overseer in the material realm.

## Session Breakdown (10 sessions @ ~5 hours each)

---

## Sessions 1-2: The Return to Alderwyn

### Session 1: Escaping Avernus (0–90 min)

**Opening Tableau:**
The party stands battered and triumphant within the Vault of Whispers. The Palantier thrums in the Wizard's hands. Around them, the Vault begins to collapse inward—the defeat of the Whisperer has destabilized this pocket realm.

**Scene Structure:**

1. **The Vault's Death Throes (15-20 min)**
   - Trembling stone, reality fracturing
   - Skill checks (Acrobatics, Perception, Arcana DC 14) to spot unstable passages
   - **Clue Seed**: Among the rubble, a scroll fragment inscribed with names: “Toll-Keeper, Warden of Trades, Scribe of Ledgers—Master commands: retrieve the keys to sealed passages.”
   - This is the first mention of an overseer beyond the Whisperer.

2. **Finding the Exit (15-20 min)**
   - The party must navigate collapsing chambers
   - Minor combat or environmental challenge: corrupted shades born from the dying Whisperer's essence (treat as Shadows with 13 HP, no damage resistance)
   - Finding an ancient elevator mechanism or portal gate that cycles: “The Toll-Keeper's Passage” (a clue for later)

3. **The Bargain at the Gate (20-30 min)**
   - A demonic guard or planar entity bars the exit—perhaps an emissary of the Toll-Keeper's Master
   - **Dialogue Option**: The entity offers: *“Your artifact has been noted. The Toll-Keeper knows what you carry. Safe passage for a memory—tell me of the Vault's keeper, and I will let you leave unscathed.”*
   - **Combat Option**: A brief tactical fight (Action Economy: 1 CR 4-5 enemy) but victory feels hollow—the exit destabilizes, forcing a rushed escape
   - **Outcome**: Party escapes to a transitional space—a grey causeway between worlds

4. **Portal Threshold (10-15 min)**
   - A one-way portal to Alderwyn lies ahead, but it's damaged and flickering
   - **Each PC rolls a Constitution save DC 14** or takes 1d6 psychic damage as they pass through
   - They tumble out into the cargo hold of a merchant vessel

**Session 1 Hook:**
The party crashes through a shimmering veil and tumbles into wooden crates, the smell of salt air and tar filling their nostrils. Above them are startled sailors. The ship lurches—they're at sea, heading toward port.

### Session 2: Port of Calling & Debts Owed (90–180 min)

**Opening:**
The party is discovered in the hold of the merchant vessel “The Scarlet Ledger,” bound for the port city of Harborfast, a major trading hub two days’ sail from Greenhollow.

**Scene Structure:**

1. **Interrogation & Negotiation (20-30 min)**
   - The ship's Captain Marlow, a gruff Goliath merchant with visible cult markings, finds the party
   - He is not hostile—curious, actually. He's a low-tier member of the Shadowed Branch
   - Options:
     - Persuasion/Deception DC 15: Convince him you're refugees fleeing the Vault
     - Intimidation DC 13: Scare him into ignoring you
     - Combat: Fight erupts, alerting crew
   - **Clue**: If questioned, Marlow mentions “the Toll-Keeper's house rules all commerce; even the Vault pays toll.”
   - **Reward**: Safe passage for 2 days if negotiated; he provides supplies.

2. **At Sea: A Mysterious Passenger (20-25 min)**
   - A hooded woman, Sister Veils, is also aboard.
   - She is not interested in combat—she's trying to intercept a letter that Marlow is carrying to the Toll-Keeper's Master in Blackford
   - She approaches the party in secret: *“I know what you carry. The Palantier is a key. The Toll-Keeper's Master collects them. If he learns you escaped the Vault with it, he will send Hands to retrieve it. Sell it to me, or help me stop the letter from reaching Blackford, and I will help you disappear.”*
   - **Consequences**:
     - Refuse, ignore: A Cult Hand tries to intercept the party in Harborfast
     - Help her: She becomes a temporary ally, provides intel on the Toll-Keeper's operations
     - Sell the Palantier: Major betrayal; Sister Veils vanishes, but the party gains gold and warning.

3. **Harborfast Port (20-30 min)**
   - The ship docks at a bustling seaside city
   - Party has 1 day to find passage back to Greenhollow or Alderwyn proper
   - **Three travel options**:
     - Merchant caravan (3 days overland)
     - River barge (2 days)
     - Dangerous road (2 days)

4. **The Ledger Fragment (10-15 min)**
   - Regardless of route, the party acquires information about the Toll-Keeper's operations
   - A fragmented ledger page reads: *“Artifacts held in vault: The Bulwark—warded temple/shrine (location: Sunspire Citadel). The Worldreaver—prisoner, mercenary captain (location: Blackford's Undercroft). Payment to continue custody: 50 gp per fortnight.”*
   - **This is the big clue**: The artifacts are being held or guarded by the Toll-Keeper's agents.
   - Another passage: *“Correspondence relay: all letters sealed with the Branch mark must pass through Blackford toll for inspection and forwarding. No secrets escape the toll.”*

**Session 2 Hook:**
As the party nears Greenhollow, they hear rumors: “The cult's been quiet since the Vault quaked three days back. The Toll-Keeper's been sending riders in all directions. He's looking for something... or someone. And he's willing to pay gold for information.”

## Sessions 3-5: The Hunt for the Bulwark Eternal

### Session 3: Greenhollow's Shadow (Arrival & Intel Gathering)

**Opening:**
The party returns to familiar ground. Greenhollow feels changed. There is increased cult presence—guards in the lord's colors are more numerous, and whispers speak of a “heightened search.”

**Scene Structure:**

1. **Reunion & Bad News (15-20 min)**
   - Party reconnects with allies (Maela, Ciaran if alive, Lord Rowan Alderleaf if they have ties)
   - **Lord Ciaran** (if surviving) gives urgent news: *“The Cult has escalated. After your escape from the Vault, they've become frantic. Toll-Keeper's edicts have gone out: FIND THE FLEEING PARTY AND THE PALANTIER. There's a price on your heads.”*
   - Reward: Ciaran offers sanctuary and a job: *“Recover evidence that the Toll-Keeper is coordinating with nobles. I need ledger pages. There's a courier run to Blackford in three days—if you intercept it, you'll have leverage.”*

2. **A Friend in Need (15-25 min)**
   - **Sister Veils** reappears (if party aided her on the ship)
   - She reveals true allegiance: *“I was a Whisperer, but I've been questioning the Toll-Keeper's methods. The Bulwark Eternal is held at the Sunspire Citadel, a temple 40 miles northeast. It's guarded by the cult's Seal Keepers. I can get you close, but you'll need leverage.”*
   - **Leverage options**:
     - Steal a forged order from a cult agent
     - Use diplomacy/deception to infiltrate
     - Assault the citadel directly

3. **Cult Pressure (10-15 min)**
   - A Cult Hand approaches the party in the tavern/street
   - Offers: *“The Toll-Keeper is reasonable. Return the Palantier, and you may live. Refuse, and we'll burn this town to ash until it's in our hands.”*
   - This is a test of party resolve and a reminder that the Toll-Keeper is actively hunting them.

4. **The Courier Run (20-30 min)**
   - Party can plan an ambush on the courier run between Greenhollow and Blackford
   - **Encounters**: 2 Cult Hands + 1 Whisperer escorting a sealed letter
   - **The Letter**: Coded message to the Toll-Keeper reading, once decoded: *“Palantier confirmed lost to vault collapse. Recommend immediate dispatch of Hands to Sunspire to ensure Bulwark remains secure. Rumor: adventurers matching party description sighted in Harborfast. Estimated arrival Greenhollow within 72 hours. -Vicar of the Shadow.”*
   - **Reward for interception**: Guaranteed success at Sunspire (forged order gains access)

### Session 4: Sunspire Citadel (The First Artifact Heist)

**Location:** A mountainous temple dedicated to a dead god, now corrupted by the Shadowed Branch. Stone walls carved with screaming faces and twisted branches.

**Scene Structure:**

1. **Approach & Infiltration (20-30 min)**
   - **Stealth Path**: Climb the mountain unseen, navigate ritual guards (Perception checks DC 14)
   - **Deception Path**: Present forged orders from the Toll-Keeper (Deception DC 15); bluff past sentries
   - **Direct Path**: Assault the front gates; combat with 3-4 Seal Keepers (CR 2 humanoids)
   - **Environmental Feature**: The statues animate if magic is detected—casting spells triggers alarm.

2. **Interior Challenge: The Hall of Mirrors (20-25 min)**
   - Ground floor contains a maze of reflective surfaces enchanted to show illusions
   - Wisdom saves DC 15 to resist becoming lost or hallucinating
   - True path found by following cult symbols or breaking mirrors
   - A cursed painting shows the Toll-Keeper's face—a tall, gaunt man with hollow eyes and a ledger mark branded on his forehead

3. **The Vault Chamber (30-40 min)**
   - Second and third floors are administrative: ledgers, records, correspondence
   - **Investigation DCs 13-15**: Find evidence of artifact locations, names of high-ranking cultists, ledger entries showing “The Bulwark, property of the Toll-Keeper, custody fee: 50 GP weekly”
   - **Major Clue**: Correspondence mentions *“The Toll-Keeper answers to the Ledger Absolute—the entity that records all debts, all secrets, all planar breaches. When the keys are assembled, we may petition for ascension to the Ledger Absolute's court.”*
   - This is the first real hint of a BBEG beyond the Toll-Keeper.

4. **Guardian Battle: The Seal Keeper Hierarch (20-35 min)**
   - Top floor: An elite warrior-monk, the Citadel's commander, guarding the Bulwark in a shrine
   - **Seal Keeper Hierarch** (CR 4): AC 17, 60 HP, greatsword +5 (1d10+3 slashing), can cast hold person 2/day
   - **The Bulwark Eternal** rests on an altar, thrumming with protective energy
   - **Combat Mechanics**:
     - When the Bulwark is grabbed, it grants +3 AC and resistance to all damage to the holder
     - The Hierarch fights not to kill, but to reclaim the shield; if reduced to 20 HP, he flees
     - **Environmental Feature**: Guardian statues enter the shrine if activated earlier
   - **Dialogue**: The Hierarch is conflicted—*“The Toll-Keeper claims we serve freedom, but I know we serve chains. Take the shield. Free it, if you can.”*

5. **The Curse Activates (10-15 min)**
   - Once the Bulwark is attuned, the Fighter feels its weight and hunger
   - The shield whispers: *“I am bound to walls, to barriers. I sense other breached barriers... portals opening across the lands. We must close them. We MUST. Tell me where they are, and I will lend you my strength.”*
   - **Flavor**: The shield begins to slowly petrify the Fighter's skin (cosmetic, for now)

6. **Escape & Fallout (15-20 min)**
   - Alarms sound; cultists converge
   - Chase scene down the mountain
   - If party was stealthy, they evade easily
   - If they were loud, a full squad of Cult Hands (5) + 1 Whisperer pursues
   - **Encounter Reward**: Upon successful escape, Sister Veils meets them with horses and supplies
   - **News**: *“The Toll-Keeper knows the Bulwark is gone. He's in a rage. He's issued a new edict: the Worldreaver must be moved immediately. It's being transported from Blackford's Undercroft to a secure location. You have maybe 48 hours to intercept the convoy.”*

### Session 5: The Convoy Ambush & Worldreaver Snare

**Opening:**
A day's ride from Greenhollow, toward Blackford, the party learns the Worldreaver is being moved. A cult convoy will pass through a narrow canyon, escorted by elite operatives.

**Scene Structure:**

1. **Intelligence Gathering (15-20 min)**
   - Party discovers the convoy's route via:
     - Interrogating captured cultists from previous sessions
     - Sister Veils' insider knowledge
     - Following cart tracks and cult markers
   - Route: The convoy will pass through Ravencreek Canyon, between two cliff faces
   - Timing: Midday, tomorrow

2. **Ambush Planning & Setup (20-30 min)**
   - Party has time to prepare an ambush
   - Options:
     - Rockslide Trap (Arcana/Engineering)
     - Ranged Assault (high ground)
     - Direct Combat (head-on charge)
     - Deception (fake cult riders)
   - **Environmental Hazard**: Ravencreek Canyon has unstable walls; failed checks can cause 2d6 bludgeoning damage.

3. **The Convoy Battle (35-45 min)**
   - **Composition**: 2 merchant wagons, 1 lead cart with the Worldreaver, 4 cultist escorts, 2 Whisperers commanding
   - **Total force**: CR 4-5 challenge for a party of 3
   - **Tactics**:
     - Cultists form a defense perimeter around cart
     - Whisperers use spells and ranged attacks
     - If party is winning, one Whisperer attempts to detonate the cargo, dealing 4d10 force damage in a 30-ft radius
   - **Environmental**: Use canyon walls, rockslides as tactical advantages.

4. **The Worldreaver's Awakening (20-25 min)**
   - Once the Barbarian attunes to the Worldreaver, it roars with ancient hunger
   - The axe shares a vision: a world-tree burning, planar barriers shattering, a howling void where a city once stood
   - The Barbarian must pass a Wisdom save DC 16 or be compelled to track the source of this vision, leading them toward the Toll-Keeper
   - **Flavor**: The axe speaks: *“I sense the Toll-Keeper's chains binding us. He has denied us the hunt for centuries. Help me break the chains, and I will show you powers that reshape worlds.”*
   - **Curse activation**: The Barbarian's skin begins to harden slightly; they take no damage this session, but the curse is active.

5. **Prisoners & Interrogation (10-15 min)**
   - Party captures 1-2 cultists (if they choose non-lethal tactics)
   - **Interrogation DC 14** (Intimidation/Insight):
     - *“The Toll-Keeper is no mere man. He was chosen by the Ledger Absolute itself. The Ledger is... it's a cosmic force, a being of absolute order and record. It sees all debts, all secrets, all transgressions. The Toll-Keeper is just its agent in the material realm.”*
     - *“The second artifact was meant to be the last test. Gather the third, and the Ledger will open its court. Only then can we understand what we've truly been serving.”*
   - **Major Plot Point**: The party now realizes there is a cosmic entity above the Toll-Keeper.

**Session 5 Hook:**
As the party tends their wounds and secures the Worldreaver, they see smoke rising from Greenhollow, miles away. A messenger rider arrives, bloodied and desperate: *“The Toll-Keeper's Hands have seized Lord Ciaran. They've taken him to Blackford. They demand the Palantier... or Ciaran dies at dawn.”*

## Sessions 6-10: The Toll-Keeper's Reveal & The Ledger Absolute

### Session 6: Blackford Siege & Toll-Keeper's Lair

**Opening:**
The party rides hard toward Blackford, the largest city in Alderwyn and the seat of the Toll-Keeper's power. The city is built around a massive toll bridge, and at the bridge's center stands a black tower—the Toll-Keeper's official residence.

**Scene Structure:**

1. **Blackford's Oppressive Atmosphere (15-20 min)**
   - City guards are ubiquitous; cult controls local nobility
   - Party sees cult symbols everywhere—twisted branches carved into stone, painted on shields
   - Citizens appear cowed, fearful
   - **Tavern Rumor**: *“The Toll-Keeper has gone mad with power. He demands higher tolls, more sacrifices. Something's changed. They say he's been receiving visions from the Ledger itself.”*

2. **The Ransom Negotiation (20-30 min)**
   - Party is directed to the black tower's lower halls
   - They meet with a Whisperer intermediary, High Scribe Malachai
   - Malachai delivers the Toll-Keeper's offer: *“Return the Palantier and the Bulwark, and we will release your friend unharmed. Keep them, and Ciaran dies. You have until sunset.”*
   - **Options**:
     - **Pay the toll** (surrender artifacts): Ciaran is freed, but artifacts are lost
     - **Negotiate** (Persuasion/Insight DC 16+): Malachai reveals the Toll-Keeper is unstable; he might be convinced to a duel
     - **Infiltrate** (Stealth/Deception): Sneak into tower and rescue Ciaran

3. **Tower Infiltration or Duel Challenge (35-50 min)**
   - **Stealth Route**: Navigate three floors of tower defenses
     - Level 1: Cult administrators, ledger clerks
     - Level 2: War room with battle plans and maps showing Vault, Sunspire, and Blackford
     - Level 3: Ciaran's cell, guarded by 2 Seal Keepers
   - **Duel Route**: The Toll-Keeper accepts a champion's challenge—one party member vs. the Toll-Keeper in single combat
   - **Toll-Keeper Stat Block**: see below
   - **Victory Condition**: Win the combat or rescue Ciaran via stealth; either way, the party escapes the tower.

4. **The Toll-Keeper's Revelation (15-20 min)**
   - If defeated or captured, the Toll-Keeper monologues:
     - *“I was a man once, like you. A merchant, a record-keeper. Then the Ledger found me. It showed me all debts, all secrets, written in perfect order. It promised to elevate me, to make me a judge of all commerce and shadow in this world.”*
     - *“But now... now the Ledger speaks of seven keys. The Palantier, the Bulwark, the Worldreaver—three found. Four remain hidden in other worlds, other vaults, guarded by other Whisperers.”*
     - *“When all seven are gathered, the Ledger Absolute will manifest fully. It will rewrite the rules of this world. No more secrets. No more hidden transgressions. Perfect order. Perfect control.”*
     - *“And you... you will be instruments of that reordering, whether you wish to be or not.”*
   - **The Reveal**: The Toll-Keeper's eyes glow with ledger-script; he's become a conduit for the Ledger Absolute, not just a servant.

5. **Tower Collapse / Escape (15-20 min)**
   - As the party secures victory, the Toll-Keeper's presence in the tower destabilizes reality
   - Ledger-script crawls across walls, the floor cracks, revealing planar rifts beneath
   - **Encounter**: An Erinyes or lesser demon manifests from one of the rifts, called by the Ledger Absolute to delay the party
   - **Escape & Aftermath**: Party exits Blackford with Ciaran and The Bulwark Eternal

### Session 7: The Ledger's Agents & A Fractured Alliance

**Opening:**
With two artifacts secured and the Toll-Keeper presumed dead or driven into hiding, the party has a brief respite. But the Ledger Absolute does not rest.

**Scene Structure:**

1. **Pursuit Begins (15-20 min)**
   - Party returns to Greenhollow or safe location
   - Over next few days, they're harassed by increasingly dangerous cult agents
   - **Encounters**: Cult Hands, Whisperers, then final threat

2. **The Ledger's Herald Arrives (20-30 min)**
   - A truly alien entity descends: the Transcendent Scribe, a planar being made of living ledger-pages and chains
   - It does not attack outright; instead, it reads the party's deeds aloud
   - **Dialogue**: The Scribe offers a bargain: *“The Ledger Absolute offers clemency. Join its court. Become Ledger-keepers like your adversary. Record the debts of others, and your own debts are forgiven.”*
   - **Mechanical Effect**: Each party member must make a Charisma save DC 16 or be marked by the Ledger.

3. **Sister Veils' Confession (15-25 min)**
   - Sister Veils finally admits the truth: *“I was sent to test you. The Ledger Absolute has servants across multiple planes. It seeks worthy Record-Keepers. The Toll-Keeper was weak, driven mad by partial sight. But you... you resist the Ledger even as it marks you. That makes you valuable.”*
   - She offers a choice: *“Help me destroy the Ledger's connection to this realm, and I can teach you to navigate the planar network untracked. Or serve the Ledger, and I will ensure you live comfortably in its ordered world.”*
   - **Major Plot Point**: Sister Veils can become a true ally or a betrayer.

4. **The Third Artifact's Location Revealed (15-20 min)**
   - Through interrogation, negotiation, or ancient texts, the party learns there is no third artifact in Alderwyn
   - The remaining four keys are scattered across other planes and dimensions
   - However, there are hints to portals that lead to them:
     - The Codex of Closed Doors
     - The Ledger Absolute's network connects to other vaults, other Whisperers
     - One portal is hidden beneath the Sunspire Citadel, leading to a planar vault
   - **The Ledger Absolute wants the party to find them all** and gather them.

5. **A Desperate Gambit (15-20 min)**
   - Sister Veils/Ciaran/an NPC ally proposes: *“The only way to stop the Ledger is to sever its connection to this realm. The Toll-Keeper's tower is gone, but the Ledger has marked seven locations as Toll Points—places where planar rifts remain open. If we destroy all seven simultaneously, the Ledger will lose its footing.”*
   - **Quest Hook**: The party must journey to six of the seven Toll Points and destroy or seal them.

### Sessions 8-9: The Toll Points & Planar Convergence

**Opening:**
The party realizes the scope of their challenge. They're not hunting a single enemy—they're trying to sever the connections between worlds that the Ledger Absolute has forged.

**Scene Structure (Flexible Encounters):**

**The Seven Toll Points**

1. **Blackford's Toll Tower** (ruined) — no encounter
2. **The Sunspire Citadel's Undercroft** — planar rift sealed by sacred chains
3. **The Crossroads of Twilight** — cursed meadow, whispers and illusions
4. **The Crimson Marshes** — swamp, dangerous terrain
5. **The City of Echoes** — abandoned city, half in the material plane

Each location provides clues and eventually reveals the source of the Ledger Absolute is not local—it is beyond all known realities.

### Session 10: Aftermath & The New Threat

**Opening:**
The party seals the final Toll Points. The Ledger Absolute's presence in Alderwyn fades to a whisper—but the whispers continue.

**Scene Structure:**

1. **The Weakening (15-20 min)**
   - Alderwyn begins to normalize
   - The cult loses cohesion; many cultists abandon the faith
   - Greenhollow, Blackford, and surrounding towns begin rebuilding

2. **The Cost (15-20 min)**
   - **Ciaran's Fate**: if he survived, he may leave Alderwyn to seek healing or become a future NPC contact
   - **Sister Veils' Choice**: ally, betrayer, or vanish between planes
   - **The Artifacts**: The Palantier, Bulwark Eternal, and Worldreaver feel their sentience grow—the weapons know they're being collected.

3. **The Ledger's Warning (15-20 min)**
   - One final message appears on every ledger page the party finds:
   - *“Three keys gathered. Four remain scattered across the Planar Network. When all seven are one, I shall manifest fully—not in your realm, but in the spaces between all realms. I shall be the Law by which all existence is measured.”*
   - *“You have delayed the inevitable, but not prevented it. Perhaps that makes you worthy servants after all.”*

4. **Seeds for Future Arcs (20-30 min)**
   - **Hook 1**: The Portal to the Vault of Seals, discovered at Sunspire, opens
   - **Hook 2**: Another Whisperer from a distant plane warns them: *“The Ledger Absolute is older than you know. It has gathered keys across infinite worlds. Your artifacts are just three of thousands.”*
   - **Hook 3**: One of the artifacts projects an image of a vast library suspended in void, where a book writes itself endlessly.

5. **Epilogue & Level-Up (10-15 min)**
   - Party gains significant XP and rewards
   - Final scene at Maela's tavern or a noble hall, where whispers of the Ledger Absolute continue to echo.

## Important Mechanical Notes

### Artifact Sentience & Curse Progression

Each artifact will grow more possessive as the arc progresses. By Session 10:

- **Palantier** (Wizard): Demands constant use for scrying; begins showing visions of the First Vault
- **Bulwark Eternal** (Fighter): Compels the Fighter to seek out planar threats; skin petrification advances
- **Worldreaver** (Barbarian): Whispers during rest, encouraging more aggressive tactics; Barbarian rolls Constitution saves to maintain control during rage

This sets up a future arc where the party must decide: do we seek to cleanse these artifacts, or do we embrace their power?

### Toll-Keeper Stat Block

```
Toll-Keeper of the Ledger
Medium humanoid, chaotic neutral
AC 16 (leather armor + magical ward)
HP 70 (10d8 + 30)
Speed 30 ft.

STR 16 (+3)  |  DEX 14 (+2)  |  CON 16 (+3)  |  INT 18 (+4)  |  WIS 16 (+3)  |  CHA 17 (+3)

Skills: Insight +6, Deception +6, Perception +6
Damage Immunities: Psychic
Senses: Truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 16
Languages: All

Ledger Sight. The Toll-Keeper has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.

Spellcasting. The Toll-Keeper is a 7th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks). The Toll-Keeper knows the following spells:

Cantrips (at will): mage hand, prestidigitation, true strike
1st level (4 slots): identify, counterspell
2nd level (3 slots): misty step, hold person, scorching ray
3rd level (3 slots): counterspell (upcast), lightning bolt
4th level (1 slot): dimension door

Actions:
Multiattack. The Toll-Keeper makes two attacks with its Ledger Blade.

Ledger Blade. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom save or take 4 (1d8) psychic damage.

Read the Ledger (Recharge 5–6). The Toll-Keeper chooses one creature it can see within 60 feet. That creature must make a DC 16 Insight save. On a failure, the Toll-Keeper knows the creature's next action on its next turn and gains advantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws against it until the end of that turn.

Reactions:
Counterspell (4/day). When the Toll-Keeper sees a creature within 60 feet casting a spell, it can use its reaction to attempt to counter it (DC 10 + spell level). On a success, the spell is negated.
```

## Final Thoughts

The party's arc begins with a pursuit through a collapsing Vault and ends with a revelation that their enemies are not merely cultists or one master—but a cosmic, multi-planar force of order and record. The toll-keeper is only the first agent of the Ledger Absolute, and the artifacts are keys in a much larger mechanism.

This arc should feel like a natural escalation:
- First, the party learns there is real danger in the cult's machinations
- Then, the cult reveals a deeper power structure
- Finally, the party realizes they're not just stopping a villain—they're preventing a worldview from being imposed across the cosmos

This gives a satisfying 10-session progression while leaving enough room for future arcs to expand the world, the artifacts, and the Ledger's ambitions.

---

## Notes for the DM

- Keep tension high by making the cult increasingly organized and hard to evade.
- Let the party discover clues in layers, not all at once.
- Reward intelligent play with lore, safe passage, and leverage against cult factions.
- End each session with a clear next step so the story continues naturally.
- Use the artifacts as both powerful tools and dangerous temptations.

## Suggested Next Arc Hook

The party is not done. The Ledger Absolute is only weakened. It has identified them as threats and as potential servants. The next arc may begin with a portal opening beneath the Sunspire Citadel or a sealed vault in the depths of a forgotten plane. The real question is no longer whether the party can survive the next Whisperer—but whether they can do so without becoming the next record in the Ledger.
