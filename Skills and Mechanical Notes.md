[[Thalia Mireweave]]

Skill & Mechanical Notes (for DM)
- Tools: Sewing tools / Weaver’s kit / Disguise kit (proficient).  
- Suggested NPC stats (friendly utility NPC):  
  - Cha 12 (+1), Int 13 (+1), Wis 12 (+1).  
  - Persuasion +4, Insight +4, Perception +3, Sleight of Hand +5 (sewing), Investigation +3 (spotting hidden seams).  
- Special Abilities:
  - Quick Stitch (reaction/1/day): Thalia can make a rapid repair that gives a creature +1 AC or +1 to an attack roll (player’s choice) for 1 round.
  - Tailor’s Eye: advantage on checks to spot hidden compartments or altered garments (Investigation / Sleight of Hand).
- Social DCs (guideline):  
  - Gain Thalia’s discreet cooperation: Persuasion DC 12 (or a gift/favor).  
  - Convince her to do illicit work (forgery, very risky concealment): Persuasion/Deception DC 15 + a favor or 50+ gp. She demands guarantees and a bond (hostage item or favour).  
  - Detect if she’s under duress/lying: Insight DC 13.
