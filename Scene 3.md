# Session 3 — Scene 3: Time Pressure, Reinforcements & Decisions
DM voice: The tollhouse is still ringing with the echoes of the fight. You've pulled a ledger, read threats, and found a ritual scroll—now the world pushes back. Someone will come for what’s missing. How you respond in the next few minutes decides whether you leave with proof or run for your lives with nothing but questions.

---

## Scene Premise
- Time window: you have a limited number of minutes (use the Reinforcement Timer rolled in Scene 1 / 2). If not rolled, default: 8 minutes.
- Stakes: secure the ledger + evidence, decide Garrin’s fate, and either hold the tollhouse or leave with the goods.
- The cult will attempt to recover the ledger, silence witnesses, or capture Garrin. The town watch may show up if the bell is rung.

---

## Reinforcement Timer (Run this now)
Roll 1d6 (or use the previously rolled result). Interpret results:
- 1–2: Reinforcements in 3 minutes — immediate pressure.
- 3–4: Reinforcements in 8 minutes — modest window.
- 5–6: Reinforcements in 15 minutes — generous window (but cult will still act).

Announce the timer clearly at the table and remind players when half the time passes.

---

## Reinforcement Types & Typical Tactics
Pick appropriate level based on table tone and party level (starting level 1). Use one of the following sets.

Low Alert (small response; subtle):
- 1 Town Guard (AC 12, HP 9; spear/shortsword) — investigating noise; likely to demand explanation first.
- 1 Cult Hand (AC 12, HP 11; shortsword) — sent to retrieve a satchel quietly.
Tactics: approach cautiously, try to talk first; cult hand skulks for the ledger or bell.

Medium Alert (field response):
- 2 Cult Hands (as above) + 1 Cult Enforcer (AC 13, HP 22; club/shortspear; +1 to allies’ morale).
Tactics: cultists try to flank, knock unconscious, drag the ledger out. Enforcer focuses on breaking defenses and grabbing Garrin if complicit.

High Alert (senior response):
- Whisperer arrives with 1–2 Hands (Whisperer: AC 13, HP 35; uses social pressure and minor ritual trinkets; may carry a sap vial or signal device).
Tactics: parley first. Whisperer attempts to negotiate return of ledger; is willing to bribe or threaten. If talks fail, they call for more reinforcements or use a nonlethal control tactic (smoke, sap vial, or signal bell).

(Use these quick stat sketches if you need numbers — tweak to your table.)

Cult Hand (sketch)
- AC 12 | HP 10–14 | Attack: Shortsword +4 (1d6+2)
- Tactics: Try to disarm, subdue, or escape with evidence. Avoid lasting fights when possible.

Cult Enforcer (sketch)
- AC 13 | HP 20–26 | Attack: Heavy Club +5 (1d8+3)
- Trait: Rally — once per encounter, grants allied Hands +1 to attack rolls for 1 round.
- Tactics: Break defenses, take the hits to let Hands retrieve treasure.

Whisperer (sketch)
- AC 13 | HP 30–40 | Attack: Dagger +4 (1d4+2)
- Social power: Persuasion/Deception +6; can call for a parley and delay violence.
- Tools: carries sap vial (minor ritual item), two wax seals, and a small, whispering charm (used to signal nearby allies).
- Tactics: Negotiate or intimidate; recover ledger with minimal blood. If forced, attempt to flee with evidence.

## Tactical Setups & Map Suggestions
- Map A — Interior (toll counter + ledger chest, bar counter, cellar hatch)
  - Ledger chest: center of attention. Table cover/barricade options.
  - Cellar hatch (hidden): alternate escape/storage.
  - Back door: leads to lane → forest edge (escape path).
  - Bell (on counter): can be rung as a Reaction by Garrin or panic NPCs.

- Map B — Exterior (toll bridge approach + manor sightlines)
  - Use if reinforcements arrive mounted or as reinforcements use approaches from the manor or bridge.

Tactical notes:
- Give players cover rules (tables = half cover; counters = three-quarter).
- Barricade task: Strength check (Athletics DC 12) to drag furniture; 1 minute per heavy piece.
- Moving ledger: Sleight of Hand DC 13 to pocket; Investigation DC 12 to hide in false bottom or bag.

---

## Player Options & Mechanics (What they can try now)
1. Defend the tollhouse (hold & fight)
   - Barricade door, set ambush, place PC as sentry: Athletics DC 12 to drag heavy items; Perception DC 12 to spot entry.
   - If fighting, cult hands will attempt to stun and escape with evidence.

2. Hide / Secure the ledger (steal & smuggle)
   - Hide ledger in false bottom or pack: Sleight of Hand DC 13 to pocket; Investigation DC 13 to find a better hiding spot (false bottom, hollowed beam).
   - Hide the ledger on Garrin, in cellar, or in a pack strapped to a donkey/wagon outside (use NPC or animal checks).

3. Make Garrin the bargaining chip (use him as bait or witness)
   - Offer Garrin protection to testify: Persuasion DC 12 to convince Garrin.
   - Use Garrin to parley: Garrin may be willing to negotiate with a Whisperer to save his family—this risks exposure.

4. Parley or bluff with incoming cultists
   - Deception/Persuasion DC 14 to trade a decoy, bluff the ledger is destroyed, or bribe the cultists (5–25 gp depending on cult level).
   - If the party uses magic for intimidation, remember the public-casting consequence in a Fearful region.

5. Burn the ledger (destruction)
   - Quick burn: 1 round to ignite; Investigation DC 13 to read long enough to copy key lines before it’s consumed.
   - Consequence: evidence lost; cult thinks you destroyed it; political fallout and you lose lead.

6. Escape with evidence (run for the forest)
   - Stealth DC 12 to slip out to the lane; Survival/Navigation DC 10 for quiet route to avoid pursuers.
   - If chased: handle as a skill challenge (see below).

---

## Skill-Challenge: Smuggle & Escape (optional)
Use this 3-successes-before-3-failures skill challenge when players try to leave with the ledger and avoid pursuit.

Possible tasks (one per player per stage):
- Stealth (DC 12) — slip past an opening guard.
- Sleight of Hand (DC 13) — pocket or swap ledger for a decoy.
- Athletics (DC 12) — boost a party member over a low wall or carry Garrin.
- Perception (DC 11) — spot patrol routes or bell ropes.
- Deception/Persuasion (DC 14) — distract a guard/townsperson.

Success: party escapes to a safe staging ground with ledger intact and no immediate pursuit.
Fail: reinforcements catch up—combat or chase begins with disadvantage to the escaping party (+cult advantage).

## Parley & Negotiation Scripts
If a Whisperer arrives and offers to parley, use these lines and options:

Whisperer opening:
> "I come with courtesy. Return what you took, and we forget the trouble. Keep what you like and the Branch will keep what it likes—your friends, your memories, the tollhouse. Make the wise choice."

Party options:
- Offer a bribe (5–25 gp): Whisperer accepts if amount equals perceived ledger value. Reduces long-term score with Branch? Maybe yes.
- Offer Garrin (sacrifice): Whisperer may accept, returns ledger, and spares house—moral cost and likely vengeance.
- Bluff (claim the ledger destroyed): Deception DC 15; if caught, full combat.
- Demand safe passage (Insist they leave quietly): Persuasion DC 16; risky.

Whisperer’s bluff/response:
- If they suspect a lie: "We will know. You are small fish. But you have spirit. Remove yourselves before we tear the roots from this place."

## Reinforcement Arrival Events (Set Scenes)
Use one of these to change pacing mid-scene.

1. Bell Toll (if Garrin rings): Town guard arrives in 2d4 minutes. Guards will either arrest the party or demand they explain; use Persuasion DC 15 to avoid being taken in.

2. Cult Retrieval Team (fast): Two Hands burst in and attempt to take ledger. They will prioritize grabbing it and retreating; Enforcer tries to break legs if blocked.

3. Whisperer Arrival (cold): Whisperer arrives elegantly, offers parley. Tense social scene follows—Whisperer may reveal more information if the party plays along (or threatens them with the priesthood).

4. Surprise Reinforcements (dramatic): A mounted rider from the lord’s manor arrives—this may be the Blackford lord, an ally, or a trap. Use to upend assumptions.

---

## Sample Combat/Round Flow (if reinforcements attack)
- Round 0 (Arrival): Cultists attempt to enter quietly — make Stealth checks to approach.
- Round 1: Cult Hands target the person closest to the ledger; Enforcer targets barricade or Garrin.
- Round 2: Party either fights or tries to use environment (kick barrels, pull chandelier, throw sand; DM adjudicates effects).
- Round 3+: Cultists attempt to grab ledger and run; if they succeed, chase sequences begin.

Tactical tip: Cultists try to secure the ledger and escape — their goal is not to beat PCs but to retrieve the item and flee. Use hit-and-run tactics.

---

## Consequence Table (immediate)
If reinforcements succeed in any of these, apply consequences:

- Cult recovers ledger: Lose the main lead. They may have moved it to Archways or Thornwell—information lost; must track the courier instead.
- Garrin taken or killed: Lose an informant; town becomes more dangerous for the party. Maela is furious and will not help easily.
- Town guard arrives and arrests party: Party must clear their name (protections, bribes, legal fight) or escape.
- Whisperer negotiates and leaves with partial ledger: You learn partial info but the Branch is alerted to your efforts and tightens security.

If party successfully defends:
- They keep ledger and gain time to act. Reward: XP milestone + local allies (Maela quietly helps later if you protect her house).
- The cult will escalate—expect more professional responses soon (Whisperer, or moving the ritual date sooner).

## Drama Options & Twists
Add one to raise stakes:

- The ledger contains a hidden name: a Guard Captain, a local magistrate, or the Blackford lord himself. If revealed publicly, consequences are severe.
- A child runs into the tollhouse during the fray — heroes must protect innocents or suffer morale losses. Rescue yields strong local favor.
- Creek near the tollhouse contains a submerged scrap of paper — evidence the cult has been moving things at night via river.

---

## Aftermath & Scene Resolution (Set outcomes)
Based on what the party does, choose one of these outcomes and prepare follow-up beats.

1. Clean Success — Ledger secured, Garrin alive & cooperative, cult thwarted.
   - Next step: Plan a Thornwell confrontation or stakeout at Archways.
   - Reward: Milestone progress; Maela offers a favor and free lodging.

2. Partial Success — Ledger partially recovered or partial ledger taken.
   - Next step: Interrogate captured cultists for missing cache info, tail Rook at dusk, or stakeout Archways.
   - Complication: Branch increases patrols; mails are rerouted; you have less time.

3. Failure — Cult recovers ledger or Garrin taken; party forced to retreat.
   - Next step: Rescue mission, trace courier routes, or perform covert operation to steal a copy.
   - Consequence: Branch escalates—Whisperers become more active; the Rooted Fane timeline may accelerate.

## XP / Milestone Guidance
- Milestone if ledger remains in party control and core evidence uncovered → story progression (count as “exposed a local cell”).
- Combat XP: award per defeated cultist/enforcer as normal if using XP rather than milestone.
- Roleplay reward: extra favor with Maela or a contact in Blackford if they handled Garrin with empathy.
