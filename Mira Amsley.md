Mother of [[Elin Amsley]] and wife to [[Renwick “Ren” Amsley]]
Mira Amsley
- Role: Mother; seamstress and part‑time laundry worker at market stalls.
- Age/Look: Early 40s. Worn hands, quick eyes, hair pulled back into a ragged bun; usually has a smear of dye on one wrist.
- Personality: Kind, anxious, quick to tears. She is better at subtlety—she hears gossip at the market and reads people from their clothes.
- Voice/snippets:
  - “Elin, my love—oh thank you… please—tell me you didn’t hurt ‘er. Who gave this to her? Who’s sending notes to children?”
  - “I washed a cloak last week with odd sap on the hem—thought it was tar. I threw it out after the men came asking.” (Possible lead.)
- What she knows / can provide:
  - Market gossip: she saw a dyed red fabric sold to a cloaked man at Lowwater Lane last week (Persuasion DC 12 or pay 1 gp for the name or time).
  - She can examine the note closely and point out a seam or printer’s mark if anyone made it from cloth (Investigation DC 12).
  - She will offer local context: which stalls trade in dyes, who got odd coin recently, which patrons ask for “quiet mending” (Insight DC 12 reveals depth).
- Interrogation / skill checks:
  - Gentle coaxing: Persuasion DC 11 to get her to calm and speak freely.
  - Trust to reveal a secret (she was coerced once to mend a dark cloak for a Hands’ contact): Persuasion DC 14 + a promise of protection for her family; she’ll name the dyer or the cellar.
  - If threatened: she shrinks and clams up; call the watch or flee to neighbors.
- Hooks & complications:
  - Mira once found sap on a cloak she mended and, frightened, burned the scraps. She kept a burnt flake in a sewing box—Arcana/Investigation DC 14 to identify residue as possible Rooted Fane sap.
  - She worries their rent is late; a Branch agent paid their rent one month in coin and asked they “look after” the child’s presence near certain booths—potential coercion lead.
