1Pit Fighters for [[The Pit of Eternal Agony]]

### 1. **The Unbroken Kargoth** – CR 9

_Medium undead (orc), chaotic evil_

**Armor Class** 18 (plate) **Hit Points** 135 (18d8 + 54) **Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 20 (+5) | **DEX** 14 (+2) | **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 10 (+0) | **WIS** 12 (+1) | **CHA** 14 (+2)

**Saving Throws** Str +9, Con +7, Wis +5 **Damage Resistances** necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks **Damage Immunities** poison **Condition Immunities** exhaustion, poisoned **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 15 **Languages** Common, Orc **Challenge** 9 (5,000 XP)

_**Undead Fortitude.**_ If damage reduces Kargoth to 0 hit points, he makes a Constitution saving throw with DC 5 + the damage taken. On a success, he drops to 1 hit point instead.

_**Reckless.**_ At the start of his turn, Kargoth can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against him have advantage until the start of his next turn.

**Actions** **Multiattack.** Kargoth makes three attacks with his greataxe. **Greataxe.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 19 (2d12 + 6) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. **Hellfire Javelin (2/Day).** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (2d6 + 7) piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) fire damage.

**Reactions** **Parry.** Kargoth adds 3 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him. To do so, he must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

### 2. **Vespera the Serpent** – CR 6

_Medium monstrosity (yuan-ti), neutral evil_

**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor + shield) **Hit Points** 97 (13d8 + 39) **Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 16 (+3) | **DEX** 18 (+4) | **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 14 (+2) | **WIS** 13 (+1) | **CHA** 17 (+3)

**Saving Throws** Dex +7, Cha +6 **Skills** Deception +6, Stealth +7 **Damage Immunities** poison **Condition Immunities** poisoned **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14 **Languages** Abyssal, Common, Draconic **Challenge** 6 (2,300 XP)

_**Shapechanger.**_ Vespera can use her action to polymorph into a Medium snake or back into her yuan-ti form.

_**Magic Resistance.**_ Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Actions** **Multiattack.** Vespera makes one scimitar attack and two serpent whip attacks, or one bite. **Scimitar.** _Melee:_ +7 to hit, 7 (1d6 + 4) slashing + 10 (3d6) poison. **Serpent Whip.** _Melee:_ +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., 8 (1d6 + 5) piercing + 7 (2d6) poison, and target must succeed on DC 15 Con save or be poisoned for 1 minute. **Bite (Snake Form Only).** _Melee:_ +7 to hit, 9 (2d4 + 4) piercing + 14 (4d6) poison damage. DC 15 Con save or poisoned and paralyzed 1 minute.

**Reactions** **Uncanny Dodge.** Halves damage from an attack she can see.

### 3. **The Twins of Torment** – CR 5 (treated as one creature)

_Small fiend (demon, imp), chaotic evil_ (two imps fused by hellfire)

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor) **Hit Points** 66 (12d6 + 24) **Speed** 20 ft., fly 40 ft.

**STR** 8 (−1) | **DEX** 18 (+4) | **CON** 14 (+2) **INT** 11 (+0) | **WIS** 12 (+1) | **CHA** 14 (+2)

**Damage Resistances** cold; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical **Damage Immunities** fire, poison **Condition Immunities** poisoned **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 13 **Languages** Infernal, Common **Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

_**Two Minds, One Body.**_ The Twins act on the same initiative and can take two reactions per round. They can concentrate on two spells at once.

_**Devil’s Sight.**_ Magical darkness doesn’t impede their darkvision.

**Actions** **Multiattack.** Four sting attacks or two stings + two infernal spark. **Sting.** _Melee:_ +7 to hit, 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing + 10 (3d6) poison. **Infernal Spark (Recharge 5–6).** _Ranged:_ +7 to hit, range 60 ft., two targets. 11 (2d10) fire damage each.

**Innate Spellcasting.** At will: _produce flame, thaumaturgy_ 3/day each: _heat metal, suggestion_

### 4. **Generic Pit Gladiator** (use for quick minions or fodder) – CR 3

_Medium humanoid (any race), any alignment_

**Armor Class** 16 (studded leather + shield) **Hit Points** 75 (10d8 + 30) **Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 17 (+3) | **DEX** 14 (+2) | **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 10 (+0) | **WIS** 12 (+1) | **CHA** 13 (+1)

**Saving Throws** Str +5, Con +5 **Skills** Athletics +5, Intimidation +3 **Senses** passive Perception 11 **Languages** Common, Infernal **Challenge** 3 (700 XP)

_**Brutal Surge (Recharge 5–6).**_ After hitting with a melee attack, the gladiator can deal an extra 7 (2d6) damage.

**Actions** **Multiattack.** Two melee attacks. **Battleaxe.** _Melee:_ +5 to hit, 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing. **Javelin.** _Ranged:_ +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing.

### 5. **Bonus: Captured Nalfeshnee Demon** (Main Event Boss) – CR 13

Use the official _Monster Manual_ nalfeshnee stat block with these modifications:

- It is **chained** at the start of combat (DC 18 Strength check to break free as an action).
- Once per round it can use _Horror Nimbus_ as a bonus action.
- If reduced to 0 HP in the pit, its soul is trapped in a soul coin that appears on Graz’ztak’s belt.
