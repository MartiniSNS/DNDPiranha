[[-Session 4]]

_A small child ([[Elin Amsley]])—no more than 8 or 9 years old—sits on a doorstep, huddled against the weathered wood. Their clothes are thin, patched, and filthy. They are crying quietly, with the exhausted, hopeless quality of a child who has cried for hours._

_Upon noticing the adventurers, the child looks up with red-rimmed eyes. For a moment, fear flashes across their face—they tense to run. But something in the party's bearing makes them pause._

#### **Option A: Help the Child**

If the adventurers choose to help Pip:

1. **Immediate Care:** A kind word, food, shelter. The child calms slightly (Animal Handling or Persuasion DC 10).
    
2. **Shelter Search:** Where can they take Pip safely?
    
    - **The Whispering Oak** (if they're returning to Greenhollow): Maela will shelter the child without question, though she'll ask for the full story.
    - **An inn in Everspring**: Available, but risking the child's visibility to the cult.
    - **A sympathetic NPC**: If the party has cultivated allies in Everspring, now is the time to cash in.
3. **Lead Generation:** Pip knows their home address. If the party investigates:
    
    - **Perception (DC 12):** The house is recently ransacked—drawers pulled open, a ledger fragment torn to shreds, signs of struggle.
    - **Investigation (DC 13):** A half-burned letter in the fireplace mentions "Blackford" and "the toll must be paid."
    - **Insight (DC 11):** The father clearly tried to hide something—and failed.
4. **Immediate Quest Hook:** The child begs (wordlessly at first, then aloud if pressed) for the party to find their parents. This becomes a **Personal Quest** for these adventurers—rescue Pip's parents from the cult's vaults.
    

---

#### **Option B: Ignore the Child**

If the adventurers choose to continue tracking the red-hooded figure:

1. **The Child's Fate:** As the party moves on, Pip watches them go. They do not call out but sink back into their doorstep. _You leave them there, alone, as the sun begins to set._
    
2. **Consequence (Immediate):** The party's Insight or Wisdom (Perception) should clue them in that they've abandoned a child to the streets. Allow a brief roleplay moment for moral reckoning.
    
3. **Consequence (Long-term):** This choice will haunt them. At a future point, you may introduce:
    
    - News that [[Elin Amsley]] was "taken in" by the cult (twisted into a recruit out of desperation).
    - A rival or enemy who Pip recognizes and now serves.
    - A moment where Pip—now changed—confronts them.
4. **Pursuit:** The party continues tracking the red-hooded cultist. This should yield:
    
    - **Perception (DC 13):** The figure enters a building and does not emerge (giving the party a location to investigate later).
    - **Sleight of Hand/Stealth (DC 14):** The party can follow undetected and observe a brief meeting with another cultist (gathering intel on cult activities).
