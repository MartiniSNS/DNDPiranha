## **QUEST OVERVIEW**

A disgraced noble from Alderwyn—**The Courtier**—has fled to Avernus seeking refuge in an abandoned stronghold of the Shadowed Branch cult. This hidden base, dormant for centuries, holds secrets that could reshape both the Material Plane and the Hells themselves. Your players must infiltrate, locate The Courtier, and decide his fate—capture him for interrogation, execute him to prevent his knowledge from reaching Zariel, or negotiate with him for information.

**Quest Difficulty:** Hard to Deadly (adjust based on party level)  
**Recommended Party Level:** 8-12  
**Duration:** 2-4 sessions

---

## **THE SETUP: WHY DOES THIS MATTER?**

### **Who Is The Courtier?**

**Background:**

- A high-ranking official in Alderwyn's royal court
- Served as an advisor to the crown for decades
- Was secretly a ranking member of the Shadowed Branch
- Recently exposed (by the party or through Ciaran's network)
- Fled through a forbidden ritual before arrest could be made

**What He Knows:**

- The true identity of the Shadowed Branch's High Matron
- The location of major cult cells across the kingdom
- Ritual knowledge that could open the Rift permanently
- Names of corrupted nobles, clergy, and military officers
- The cult's ultimate endgame (something darker than mere blackmail)

**Why He Fled to Avernus:**

- The Material Plane is no longer safe
- Zariel's forces are actively recruiting powerful schemers
- He made a pact: exchange his knowledge for infernal protection
- He believes he can build a new power base in Hell itself

**Current Status:**

- Paranoid, desperate, but cunning
- Hiding in an old Shadowed Branch fortress that few remember exists
- Communicating with both remaining cult cells AND Zariel's agents
- Playing both sides, trying to maximize his power

### **Why Do the Players Care?**

**Personal Stakes:**

- If The Courtier reaches Zariel with his knowledge, the Material Plane is compromised
- Zariel gains leverage over the Shadowed Branch (and thus control of kingdoms)
- The party's allies and families could be in danger
- If cult operations are exposed, innocents might be executed as collateral

**Strategic Stakes:**

- The Courtier holds the key to dismantling the cult permanently
- His knowledge could prevent the Rift from being opened
- Capturing him alive gives the party immense political power
- His death might be the cleanest solution, but does it sit right with them?

**Moral Stakes:**

- How do you treat a prisoner in Hell itself?
- Can you trust his testimony?
- If you spare him, what then?
- What are you willing to do to stop a greater threat?

## **THE LOCATION: THE VAULT OF WHISPERS**

### **General Description**

**The Vault of Whispers** is an ancient stronghold of the Shadowed Branch, built centuries ago when the cult first attempted to establish a foothold in Hell itself. It's a fortress designed for one purpose: **secrecy and survival**.

**Location:** Deep in the **Crimson Wastes**, 5 days' hard travel from Nessus Hold

**Appearance:**

- Hidden beneath an outcropping of black stone
- Entrance is **warded** with illusion magic (appears as a solid cliff face)
- Built in infernal architecture—angles that hurt to look at, doors that don't quite line up with space
- The fortress is partially phased into the Shadowfell, making it difficult to perceive

**Atmosphere:**

- Perpetual gloom, even in the deeper chambers
- The air tastes of copper and old secrets
- Whispers echo from nowhere (magical, not supernatural)
- A sense of being watched constantly
- The ground feels unstable beneath your feet

---

### **THE LAYOUT: MULTI-LEVEL FORTRESS**

#### **LEVEL 1: THE OUTER WARD (Entrance & Surface)**

**The Illusion Gate:**

- Appears as solid cliff face
- **Arcana DC 18** to perceive the illusion
- **Insight DC 16** to understand something is hidden here
- Passing through: No damage, but disorienting (Wisdom save DC 14 or disadvantage on first roll in this section)

**The Guardian Chamber:**

- A single large room beyond the gate
- Contains a **Golem of Infernal Iron** (treat as Stone Golem, but immune to fire)
- Animated by a simple command word (The Courtier knows it; party doesn't, unless they find it)
- **Inscribed on the golem's chest:** "Shadow speaks, stone listens" (hint to the riddle)

**Exits:**

- A staircase descending down (heavily guarded)
- Side passages leading to barracks (empty, but contain weapons and supplies)
- A sealed door (requires **Strength DC 16** to break, or a key—findable in the barracks)

**Encounters Here:**

- Combat with the Golem
- Trapped doorways (dart traps, pressure plates)
- A frightened cultist hiding in the barracks (can be interrogated for information)

---

#### **LEVEL 2: THE WHISPERING HALLS (Administration & Records)**

**Atmosphere:**

- Endless corridors lined with doors
- Each door is identical; navigation is confusing
- Whispers emanate from the walls (magical echo of old rituals)
- Cold draft coming from deeper levels

**Notable Chambers:**

**The Archive of Secrets (Room A):**

- Walls lined with ledgers, scrolls, and dark tomes
- Contains records of cult operations spanning 200+ years
- **Key Information Found Here:**
    - A ledger naming the High Matron (written in cipher; Arcana DC 16 to decode)
    - Maps of cult cells across Alderwyn and other kingdoms
    - Ritual components and instructions for opening the Rift
    - A personal journal belonging to a former High Matron (reveals internal conflict within cult leadership)

**Defenses:**

- **Magical ward:** Any creature that enters must make an **Intelligence save DC 15** or be **frightened** until it leaves
- **Trapped shelves:** Pulling a specific sequence of books opens a secret passage; wrong sequence triggers a **fireball** trap (8d6 damage, DC 15 Dex save for half)
- **A bound spirit** inhabits the archive (appears as a ghostly scribe at a desk, ignores the party unless they damage the books)

**The Scribe's Chamber (Room B):**

- Where The Shadowed Branch kept its correspondence
- A writing desk with thousands of letters in various stages of composition
- **Key Information Found Here:**
    - Recent letters to/from The Courtier
    - Plans for summoning Zariel's agents
    - A letter offering The Courtier amnesty in exchange for his knowledge
    - Coded messages between High Priestesses

**The Vault Proper (Room C):**

- A circular chamber with a 15-foot-diameter circular door at its center
- The door is sealed with **5 different magical locks** (each requires a specific component or knowledge to open)
- Contains: The Courtier's personal treasury, ritual components, and an artifact of the Shadowed Branch

**Locks:**

1. **Lock of Names:** Requires speaking the true name of the cult's founder (Arcana DC 18 or history check)
2. **Lock of Sacrifice:** Requires blood of a willing sacrifice or the magic-user's own blood (1 HP permanently lost)
3. **Lock of Riddles:** A sphinx-like riddle must be solved (DC 16 Intelligence check, hint available from the archive)
4. **Lock of Shadows:** Requires a shadow being or shadow magic cast near it
5. **Lock of Binding:** Requires an infernal pact or a devil's consent

**The Council Chamber (Room D):**

- A large table where the cult's leadership once convened
- Spectral images can be seen here (non-hostile; they're recordings of past meetings)
- **Listening to the spectral meetings reveals:**
    - The cult's true goal (beyond blackmail)
    - The identity of Zariel's contact within the cult
    - A schism developing between traditionalists and radicals
    - The Courtier's recent betrayal of his co-conspirators

---

#### **LEVEL 3: THE RITUAL CHAMBERS (Deep Sanctuary)**

**Atmosphere:**

- Noticeably warmer (magical heat from infernal wards)
- The air thrums with power; spellcasters feel it in their bones
- A faint smell of sulfur and burnt flesh
- The walls are carved with infernal runes (constantly glowing faintly)

**The Grand Ritual Circle (Main Chamber):**

- A massive circle carved into the floor, 50 feet in diameter
- Inscribed with names of demons, devils, and elder entities
- **Purpose:** Designed to summon a devil of considerable power (or open a gateway)
- **Current Status:** Dormant, but recently activated (scorch marks, fresh blood stains)

**The Altar of Binding (Side Chamber A):**

- Where pacts are made and sealed
- Contains a ledger of all pacts ever made by the cult
- **A devil avatar is bound here** (not hostile unless the party tries to break the binding; if released, it becomes combat encounter)
- The avatar is **bound to serve the Shadowed Branch**, making it a potential ally if the party can convince it

**The Chamber of Echoes (Side Chamber B):**

- A resonance chamber designed to amplify magic
- Ritual components hang from the ceiling (some still glowing with residual magic)
- **If magic is cast here, it's empowered:** Double range, double damage dice (but also draws attention)

**The Summoning Platform (Lower Chamber):**

- A raised dais accessible by a narrow causeway
- Leads to a chasm of absolute darkness below
- A chain extends into the darkness (its purpose unknown; disturbing it causes tremors)
- **Carved inscription:** "Here dwells the Dreaming Dark, where the Rift was first wounded"

**Encounters Here:**

- The devil avatar (negotiable or combat)
- A ritual goes wrong, summoning something unexpected
- The Courtier's guards (if he's alerted to intruders)
- Environmental hazards: magical traps, unstable ritual circles

---

#### **LEVEL 4: THE COURTIER'S SANCTUM (Hidden Deep)**

**Access:**

- A secret passage from the Summoning Platform, concealed by magic
- **Perception DC 18** to find, or the party must solve a puzzle in the Ritual Chambers
- A winding tunnel descends into complete darkness (magical darkness; light spells don't fully penetrate)

**The Inner Sanctum:**

A luxurious chamber carved into the obsidian stone itself. Despite being in Hell, it's appointed like a noble's study:

- A four-poster bed with infernal silk sheets
- Mahogany desk with scattered papers (recent correspondence)
- A fireplace crackling with green flames
- Shelves containing rare books and artifacts
- A mirror (enchanted; allows scrying)
- A safe hidden behind a painting

**The Courtier's Presence:**

- He's here, but not alone
- He has **2-3 infernal servants** attending him (devils or bound demons)
- He's preparing for something (a ritual? An escape? A summoning?)
- He may or may not be aware of the party's presence (depending on how stealthy they've been)

**What's On His Desk (if the party searches):**

- A letter from Zariel's agents offering protection in exchange for service
- A map showing proposed locations for opening the Rift
- A personal journal detailing his crimes and his reasoning
- A ritual scroll (incomplete, but powerful; Arcana DC 18 to understand its purpose)
- A contact list for remaining cult members

**The Safe Contains:**

- Soul coins (200+ worth)
- A signet ring bearing the cult's symbol
- A small infernal artifact (grants advantage on saves against magic, but slowly corrupts the user)
- Documents proving the High Matron's identity
- A key to a secondary hideout (elsewhere in the world)

## **THE COURTIER: STAT BLOCK & TACTICS**

### **THE COURTIER**

**AC:** 15 (infernal robes + protective ward)  
**HP:** 66  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

**Ability Scores:**

- STR 10 (+0)
- DEX 14 (+2)
- CON 12 (+1)
- INT 17 (+3)
- WIS 15 (+2)
- CHA 16 (+3)

**Saving Throws:** INT +6, WIS +5  
**Skills:** Arcana +6, Deception +6, Insight +5, Persuasion +6  
**Damage Resistances:** Psychic  
**Senses:** Passive Perception 12

**Languages:** Common, Infernal, Celestial, Abyssal

---

### **ABILITIES**

**Infernal Pact (Recharge 5-6):**

- The Courtier can call upon a devil patron for aid
- Uses **Counterspell** (automatically succeeds on spell level 3 or lower, DC 16 for higher levels)
- Or casts **Suggestion** (DC 16 Charisma save)

**Ritual Knowledge:**

- Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects
- Can cast ritual spells without using spell slots (1/day each)
    - _Scrying_ (to check on allies/enemies)
    - _Sending_ (to contact distant allies)

**Diplomatic Immunity (Psychological):**

- The Courtier speaks as someone used to being obeyed
- Uses **Persuasion** and **Deception** as offensively as any warrior uses weapons
- **Insight DC 16** to see through his lies

---

### **ACTIONS**

**Multiattack:**

- One spell or one Dagger attack

**Dagger** (+4 to hit, 1d4+2 slashing)

- Not his weapon of choice; he'll avoid combat if possible

**Spellcasting:**

- The Courtier casts spells as a 9th-level spellcaster
- **Cantrips:** _Mage Hand_, _Prestidigitation_
- **1st Level:** _Magic Missile_, _Shield_, _Charm Person_, _Feather Fall_
- **2nd Level:** _Detect Thoughts_, _Scorching Ray_, _Hold Person_
- **3rd Level:** _Counterspell_, _Dispel Magic_, _Fireball_
- **4th Level:** _Polymorph_, _Dimension Door_, _Greater Invisibility_

**Infernal Servants:**

- 2-3 devils (use **Bearded Devil** or **Spined Devil** stats) fight on his behalf
- He directs them tactically

---

### **COMBAT TACTICS**

**Phase 1: Awareness**

- If alerted to intruders, he immediately casts **Greater Invisibility** and moves to high ground
- His servants engage the party while he assesses threats

**Phase 2: Control**

- Uses **Hold Person** or **Suggestion** on the most dangerous party members
- Casts **Fireball** if enemies cluster together
- **Counterspells** any powerful spells cast against him

**Phase 3: Escape**

- If clearly losing, he **casts Dimension Door** and escapes the sanctum
- Flees deeper into the fortress or attempts to reach the ritual chamber for a summoning
- He's intelligent; he knows when to abandon a fight

**Phase 4: Negotiation**

- If trapped or surrounded, he switches tactics entirely
- Offers information, deals, alliances
- **He prefers to talk his way out** rather than fight

---

## **THE COURTIER'S PERSONALITY & DIALOGUE**

### **How He Speaks**

_Cultured, persuasive, confident. He's a politician caught in a terrible situation, but he refuses to act like it._

**When First Encountering the Party:**

> _"Ah. How resourceful of you to find me. I assume Ciaran sent you? Or perhaps it was the crown? No matter."_
> 
> _He straightens his robes, displaying no fear despite being outnumbered._
> 
> _"I suspect you're here to kill me. Or capture me. Or perhaps you're hoping I'll surrender peacefully and tell you all my secrets."_
> 
> _A slight smile._
> 
> _"None of those options appeal to me. But I'm willing to hear what you have to say before this becomes... unpleasant."_

### **His True Nature**

Beneath the politeness is a man who:

- Believes the Shadowed Branch's work was _necessary_
- Sees himself as a pragmatist, not a villain
- Genuinely thinks he's serving a greater good
- Is terrified of dying, despite his composure
- Wants power and control above all else

### **His Motivations**

**What He Wants:**

- To live (primary objective)
- To maintain power and influence
- To advance the cult's goals (or rebuild the cult under his leadership)
- To avoid punishment from the crown or from Zariel

**What He'll Trade:**

- Information about cult operations
- Names of high-ranking members
- Ritual knowledge (but incomplete, to maintain leverage)
- Protection for himself or his allies

**What He Won't Do:**

- Betray the High Matron (he fears her more than he fears the party)
- Completely dismantle the cult (it's his power base)
- Make a deal he can't keep (his word is his currency)

## **QUEST OBJECTIVES & STAGES**

### **PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: CAPTURE OR ELIMINATE THE COURTIER**

**Success Conditions:**

- The Courtier is captured alive for interrogation
- OR The Courtier is killed (preventing his knowledge from reaching Zariel)
- OR The Courtier escapes BUT the party gains his knowledge/documents

**Failure Conditions:**

- The Courtier reaches Zariel's forces with his knowledge intact
- The Ritual Chamber is activated (summoning something catastrophic)
- The fortress is destroyed (taking valuable intelligence with it)

---

### **STAGE 1: THE APPROACH (Days 1-5)**

**Objective:** Travel to the Vault of Whispers without being detected

**Challenges:**

- Navigation in the Crimson Wastes (hostile environment)
- Zariel's patrols (random encounters with devils searching for The Courtier)
- Cultist scouts (the Shadowed Branch is also hunting him)
- Environmental hazards: geysers, sulfur storms, unstable terrain

**Encounters:**

- A band of 4-6 **Bearded Devils** on patrol (can be avoided or fought)
- Cultist spy ring (3 cultists trying to track The Courtier)
- Environmental hazard: sulfur storm (Dexterity saves, half movement)

**Discovery:**

- The party finds signs of recent cult activity
- Tracks leading to the fortress
- Abandoned camp with supplies and letters

---

### **STAGE 2: INFILTRATION (Level 1-2)**

**Objective:** Enter the fortress and avoid/overcome the initial guardians

**Key Encounters:**

- **The Golem** (Guardian Chamber)
- **The Frightened Cultist** (can provide information if convinced)
- **Trapped doorways** and illusions
- **Optional:** A cultist ritual in progress (can be disrupted or observed)

**Discovery:**

- Information about The Courtier's location
- Clues to the deeper levels
- Evidence of recent infernal pacts

**Skill Checks:**

- **Arcana DC 16:** Perceive magical wards
- **Perception DC 14:** Find hidden passages
- **Investigation DC 15:** Understand the fortress's layout

---

### **STAGE 3: EXPLORATION & COLLECTION (Level 2)**

**Objective:** Gather information before confronting The Courtier

**Key Encounters:**

- **The Archive** (puzzle/combat encounter with spirit guardian)
- **The Vault Proper** (solving locks to access greater secrets)
- **The Council Chamber** (spectral visions)
- **Potential ambush:** Cultist defenders (if The Courtier sent them to delay intruders)

**Information Gathered:**

- High Matron's identity (big revelation)
- Cult cell locations
- Names of corrupted officials
- The nature of the Rift (and why opening it is catastrophic)

**Moral Choice:**

- Destroying records vs. preserving them
- Freeing the spirit guardian vs. banishing it
- Sparing cultists vs. eliminating them

---

### **STAGE 4: THE RITUAL CHAMBERS (Level 3)**

**Objective:** Navigate the dangerous ritual space without triggering catastrophe

**Key Encounters:**

- **The Devil Avatar** (bound to the altar; can be negotiated with)
- **Magical hazards** (unstable ritual circles, echoing chambers)
- **The Courtier's Elite Guards** (if he's alerted)
- **Environmental puzzle:** Understanding the Rift and preventing its accidental opening

**Skill Checks:**

- **Arcana DC 17:** Understand ritual magic and avoid triggers
- **Religion DC 15:** Understand the spiritual significance
- **Strength DC 16:** Break magical seals (if brute force is chosen)

**Moral Choice:**

- The Devil Avatar can become an ally or enemy
- Should the ritual circle be destroyed? (permanent but dangerous)
- How do you handle The Courtier's guards?

---

### **STAGE 5: THE SANCTUM (Level 4) — FINAL CONFRONTATION**

**Objective:** Confront The Courtier, make a decision about his fate

**The Encounter:**

- The Courtier is present with 2-3 infernal servants
- The chamber is defensible but claustrophobic
- Escape routes exist (deeper tunnels, ritual platform)

**Combat OR Negotiation:**

- If combat: **The Courtier fights defensively**, attempting to escape
- If negotiation: He's willing to deal; the party must determine acceptable terms

**Potential Outcomes:**

**Option A: Capture and Interrogation**

- The party subdues The Courtier non-lethally
- Brings him back for trial
- He reveals cult secrets under magical compulsion or torture
- **Consequences:** Does this compromise the party morally?

**Option B: Execution**

- The party kills The Courtier
- Prevents him from reaching Zariel
- But loses the chance to learn more details
- **Consequences:** Is justice served, or is murder committed?

**Option C: Negotiated Deal**

- The Courtier trades information for his life
- Becomes a spy/informant for the party
- Or escapes with the party's aid (complicated alliance)
- **Consequences:** Can he be trusted?

**Option D: Escape & Pursuit**

- The Courtier uses ritual magic or devils to escape
- The party must decide to pursue or let him go
- Sets up a future confrontation

## **COMPLICATIONS & TWISTS**

### **Complication A: The High Matron's Agent**

An agent of the High Matron is secretly in the fortress, attempting to kill The Courtier before he can be captured and interrogated.

**How It Plays Out:**

- The party encounters this agent in the Sanctum
- Three-way combat: party vs. Courtier's guards vs. High Matron's assassin
- The assassin is a powerful NPC (use **Erinyes** or **Assassin Rogue** stats)
- If the agent kills The Courtier, the party can loot the body but loses interrogation opportunity

**Twist:** The agent was secretly working for someone else—perhaps another cult faction, perhaps Zariel herself

---

### **Complication B: The Ritual Activates**

A seal is broken (accidentally or deliberately by The Courtier), and the Grand Ritual Circle activates.

**What Happens:**

- A demon of significant power begins to manifest (Balrog, Nalfeshnee, or custom demon)
- The fortress starts to collapse or phase more completely into the Shadowfell
- The party must decide: fight the demon, escape, or complete the ritual for their own purposes
- **Environmental hazard:** The ground beneath their feet becomes unstable

---

### **Complication C: Secondary Pursuers**

Zariel's agents arrive during or immediately after the party's confrontation with The Courtier.

**Who Arrives:**

- A Pit Fiend commander with 4-6 Bearded Devils
- Captain Serath (if the party has previously crossed her)
- A competing faction of cultists seeking The Courtier

**The Problem:**

- The party must negotiate, fight, or surrender
- The prisoner (if captured) might be taken from them
- Multiple factions now know the fortress's location

---

### **Complication D: The Courtier's True Nature**

A revelation: The Courtier is not entirely human. He's partially infernal or bound to a devil.

**What This Means:**

- He's much harder to kill (regeneration, resistance to damage)
- He has infernal powers not previously evident
- His deal with Zariel might be more binding than assumed
- The party must find a new way to defeat or contain him

---

### **Complication E: The Rift is Closer Than Thought**

The ritual chamber's inscriptions reveal that the Rift isn't just a metaphor—it's an actual wound in reality, and opening it is more achievable than anyone thought.

**What This Means:**

- The cult's endgame is imminent
- The party now has a ticking clock
- They must choose between pursuing The Courtier or preventing the Rift's opening
- New quests are immediately available

---

## **QUEST RESOLUTION OPTIONS**

### **Resolution A: Triumph & Interrogation**

The party captures The Courtier alive and brings him back for interrogation.

**Outcome:**

- The cult is significantly weakened
- The party gains crucial intelligence
- The Courtier's trial becomes a political event
- New allies and enemies are made based on the trial's outcome

**Consequences:**

- Other cult members go into hiding
- Zariel's interest in the party increases (dangerous)
- The High Matron targets the party specifically

---

### **Resolution B: Righteous Execution**

The party kills The Courtier, preventing his knowledge from reaching Zariel.

**Outcome:**

- The cult remains a threat but is less organized
- Zariel is denied a major intelligence asset
- The party makes a dark choice (is this justice or murder?)
- Documents and artifacts are still recovered

**Consequences:**

- Some allies (especially Ciaran) might question the decision
- Zariel's interest is piqued (who dares deny her?)
- The High Matron knows The Courtier is dead

---

### **Resolution C: Negotiated Alliance**

The party makes a deal with The Courtier.

**What He Offers:**

- Information and assistance against the cult
- Access to cult resources and contacts
- Political influence in the Material Plane

**What He Demands:**

- His freedom or safe passage
- Protection from the High Matron
- A position of power in the party's organization
- Potential redemption or reform

**Complications:**

- Can The Courtier be trusted?
- Do allies accept this arrangement?
- What does the High Matron do when she learns?

---

### **Resolution D: Escape & Pursuit**

The Courtier escapes despite the party's efforts.

**What Happens:**

- He flees to another realm or location
- The party gains clues to his new hideout
- This becomes a multi-quest storyline
- A personal vendetta develops

**Consequences:**

- The Courtier becomes a recurring nemesis
- He gathers power and influence elsewhere
- The final confrontation is set up for a later session
- The party's reputation takes a hit (they failed to stop him)

---

## **INTEGRATION WITH BROADER CAMPAIGN**

### **How This Quest Connects to the Shadowed Branch Campaign**

**Before This Quest:**

- The party has encountered the Shadowed Branch multiple times
- Ciaran or an ally has revealed The Courtier's location
- The cult is growing more desperate/aggressive

**During This Quest:**

- The party learns the High Matron's identity
- They discover the cult's true endgame
- They find evidence of the Rift's location
- They encounter infernal forces interested in the cult

**After This Quest:**

- The cult is significantly weakened but not destroyed
- The High Matron becomes a personal enemy
- New plot threads emerge (the Rift, Zariel's interest, defecting cultists)
- The party gains political leverage they can use against other enemies

## **SCALING FOR DIFFERENT PARTY LEVELS**

### **Level 8 Version (Hard)**

- Fewer guards, weaker devil servants
- Simpler magical locks
- The Courtier relies on negotiation and escape
- Fewer complications

### **Level 10 Version (Deadly)**

- Full encounters as described
- Multiple complications possible
- The Courtier is a genuine threat
- Environmental hazards are more dangerous

### **Level 12+ Version (Deadly+)**

- Additional guardians and traps
- The devil avatar is fully realized and combatted
- Multiple factions pursuing The Courtier simultaneously
- Ritual chamber can actually summon something if triggered
