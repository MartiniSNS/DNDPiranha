Region claimed: [[Wathertia the Twin Harbours]] (south-western coast). 
Ruler: King Thorne Blacktide II, LE half-elf (use Bandit Captain + Noble traits; scarred from pirate days, now “legitimate”). 
Capital: Dockspear – two crescent harbors divided by a massive chain that can be raised in minutes. 
Flavor: Merchant-princes and old pirate blood run the realm. Ships fly the black-and-gold twin-anchor flag. The king’s court is loud, drunk, and dangerous—exactly the opposite of Apletersia.

**[[The Shadowed Branch]] hooks**:

- A ledger page (one of your Session 0 hooks) names King Thorne’s favorite mistress as a cult courier.
- The king offers the party a privateer letter of marque if they’ll quietly investigate why his tax ships keep vanishing in the Coastal and Island Features.

**Tie to [[The Verdant Fringe]]**: Wathertia claims the southern edge of the Verdant Fringe as “protectorate.” [[Blackford]] tollkeeper answers to a Wathertian harbormaster who just arrived with sealed orders.
