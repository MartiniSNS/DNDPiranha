the lowest tier of [[The Shadowed Branch]]

**Those Under Blackmail** — Not true members, but bound to serve.

- **Who:** Nobles, officials, merchants, even guards who possess secrets the cult has discovered.
- **Leverage:** Scandals, forbidden knowledge, family threats, financial ruin.
- **Usage:** Provide safe passage, hide evidence, misroute investigations, or spread disinformation.

**Example Unwitting Agents:**

- The Lord of [[Blackford]] (blackmailed, desperate, useful as a source of intelligence).
- The Steward of [[Thornwell]] (visited by a cloaked rider; now acts strangely and begs for secrecy).
- A minor guard captain whose past indiscretion was recorded and is now used against him.
