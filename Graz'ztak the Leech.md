Graz'ztak the Leech, A barbed devil who deserted the Blood War centuries ago. He appears as a tall, emaciated humanoid with rust-red skin, too many arms, and hooks instead of fingers. He wears a blood-stained apron and speaks with a raspy, overly polite voice.

Graz'ztak collects debts in soul coins or favors. He runs a side business in information brokering and minor soul trading.
