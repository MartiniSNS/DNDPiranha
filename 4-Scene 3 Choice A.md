[[4-Scene 3]]

Setup reminder

- Scene: The Whispering Oak. PCs are seated around the table with Ciaran; everyone has the anonymous letter and has just spoken quietly with him.
- Trigger: Two Cult Hands burst through the tavern door, weapons drawn, eyes on Ciaran. They move to seize him rather than murder — the cult wants prisoners.

Initiative (example)

- Initiative order (sample rolls): Thalia the rogue 18, Ronan the fighter 15, Cult Hand A 12, Cult Hand B 10, Elena the wizard 8, Sacris the sorcerer 6, Ciaran 4, Maela 2.

Opening chaos

- DM calls for the tavern crowd’s Dex save (DC 12). Two patrons fail and are shoved (1d4 bludgeoning); the rest duck behind tables.
- Maela yells, “No trouble in my house!” and slides between tables to block a path; she will call the watch if violence continues.

Round 1 (example actions & rolls)

1. Thalia (rogue) — Dash + melee: She leaps across a toppled bench and strikes Cult Hand A with a short sword. Attack roll hits (example 14 + modifiers); sneak attack applies. Cult Hand A is badly wounded and drops to one knee.
2. Ronan (fighter) — Interpose: Ronan charges Cult Hand B and uses the Attack to push it back (Athletics vs. Hand’s Dex or AC narrative). He succeeds at shoving (player roll beats DC 12); the Hand is knocked off balance and can’t reach Ciaran this turn.
3. Cult Hand A — Staggers to its feet and tries to grapple Ciaran (it wants a live prisoner). It misses as Thalia’s blade and Ronan’s presence make the attempt clumsy.
4. Cult Hand B — Swings at Ronan; hits for 6 slashing (example). Ronan holds the line.
5. Elena (wizard) — Quick control: Elena throws a small sack of flour into Cult Hand A’s face (improvised object; DM rules as a ranged attack). The flour blinds the Hand (disadvantage on attacks) for 1 round.
6. Sacris (sorcerer) — Defensive magic: Sacris uses Shield on Ciaran (reaction, example) when a Hand lunges; Ciaran’s AC increases and the blow glances off.
7. Ciaran — Scrambles for cover behind the bar and whispers a plea to the party: “Keep them off me. The ledger—don’t let them take it.”
8. Maela — Slams a tray down to create difficult terrain and moves any wounded patron out of danger.

Tactical choices & consequences

- Thalia can attempt a nonlethal takedown to capture a Hand for interrogation (rogue’s player chooses nonlethal damage). She succeeds and ties one Hand up with rope from behind the bar.
- Ronan can press the attack or escort Ciaran to the loft. He chooses to hold the door: the cult’s goal is to get Ciaran out, so denying egress is strategic.
- Elena and Sacris can focus on control (Sleep, Color Spray, or more flour/illumination) to prevent lethal escalation; the players choose not to kill, matching the cult’s preference for prisoners.

End of encounter (example result)

- Both Cult Hands are neutralized: one bound (Thalia), one knocked unconscious (Elena’s spell/combined attacks). No PCs are down, Maela sustained a graze (1–2 HP), two patrons are bruised from the scuffle.
- Town watch hears the ruckus and arrives just as PCs are tying the second Hand; Maela signals them to hold off and tells the PCs she can explain—this buys the party a minute of discretion.

Immediate fallout & clues

- Ciaran is shaken but alive. Grateful, he hands over a slip of paper he’d been hiding — a cipher key and a marked map pointing to Blackford Tollhouse (give players Clue B: cipher key / scribbled map).
- If the PCs spared the captured Hand and interrogate (Intimidation/Insight/Investigation checks), they learn: a meeting at the Archways of Bridgia next week and a courier runs bribes through Blackford. (Investigation DC 13 to get specifics.)
- Maela rewards the party with free lodging and offers a small favor: “You saved my house. One favor, when I need it.” She will also quietly warn them that the Branch will escalate if this becomes public.

Mechanical outcomes

- XP: treat as a single short combat (award XP appropriate to two cult Hands / bandit-equivalents) or grant milestone progress toward “first direct Hand encounter.”
- Wounds & resources: one or two minor HP losses, a few spell slots used (if any), and a tied captive who may be interrogated later.
- Reputation: +1 favor with Ciaran; +1 with Maela / Whispering Oak. Town watch now knows there was trouble (possible later scrutiny if PCs are loud).

Roleplay beat & hook to next scene

- Ciaran, more convinced the Branch is close, asks them to shadow the tollhouse at dusk or retrieve the rest of the ledger from Blackford. The party now has a tangible lead and a captive who might point toward the Branch’s local cell.
- DM to players: “You’ve shown you’ll protect a hunted man — how do you use the bound Hand? Drag him to the loft and question him, turn him over to Maela for safe-keeping, or hand him to the watch and risk alerting the Branch?”

Notes for the DM (how to run it)

- Keep it fast and dramatic: two cultists, a few hazards (overturned tables, hot oil lantern), and a chance to choose nonlethal restraint conveys the cult’s intent (capture, not slaughter).
- Reward nonlethal cleverness: sparing cultists can give later interrogation clues; killing them stalls that information.
- Use Maela as an ally that enforces discretion—if players blow up the tavern, she’ll lock them out later.
