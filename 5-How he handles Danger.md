[[Haldric Ironhand]]

- Practical and cautious. Prefers to avoid public fights that bring the town watch. If threatened in his forge:
    - First: ring the heavy bell to summon help (town guard / merc contacts).
    - Second: use nonlethal measures and make exits; he sets a scrap‑iron trip and a flare of coal‑dust to blind attackers briefly.
    - Third: if friends (Maela or party) are threatened, he fights—holds ground with a hammer, aims to disable rather than kill if there’s value in prisoners.
- Discretion rule: If the job could expose Maela or the Whispering Oak to the Shadowed Branch, Haldric will insist on secrecy checks (Persuasion DC 12 to convince him to speak openly; a favour or coin will buy discretion).
- Loyalty: he’ll repay Maela with a favour (free repair, quick armoring) and will back the party if they’re on good terms with her.
