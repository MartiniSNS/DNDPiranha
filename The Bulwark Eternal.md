_Legendary shield (requires attunement by a fighter)_

A towering tower shield of burnished bronze and celestial steel, etched with thousands of tiny screaming faces that fall silent when the shield is raised in defense. The boss is a single unblinking crystal eye.

**Properties**

- **+3 bonus** to AC when wielded.
- You have resistance to all damage while you are standing on the ground and not incapacitated.
- **Seal the Breach** (3/day): As a reaction when a creature within 30 ft casts a spell or attempts to teleport/plane shift, you can force that creature to make a DC 20 Charisma saving throw. On a failure, the spell or effect fails and the creature takes 8d10 force damage.

**Sentinel of the Veil** You cannot be moved against your will. You have advantage on all saving throws to resist being charmed, frightened, or possessed. Once per long rest you can cast _globe of invulnerability_ without a spell slot.

**Sentience** Intelligence 14, Wisdom 18, Charisma 20. Lawful Good (fallen). Stoic and protective. Demands you stand as the final wall against planar invaders.

**Curse**

- You cannot attune to any other shield or armor that grants AC bonuses.
- You are compelled to protect planar “boundaries” — if you ever flee from a fight involving planar creatures, you take 4d10 psychic damage and lose attunement for 24 hours.
- The shield grows heavier over time (eventually requiring 20+ Strength to lift).
