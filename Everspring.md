A bustling river city at the Fringe’s trade heart, Everspring boasts markets, guildhalls, a small garrison, and enough inns to swallow a marching party. Nobles and merchants meet here; libraries and old clerical archives hide scraps of lore. Hook: The city’s courts are a tangle of favors and secrets — a likely place to find agents of the Shadowed Branch, a patron who will pay for discretion, or a hidden archive hinting at the cabal’s true aim.

- The regional river city and trade heart of the Fringe: bustling markets, guildhalls, a modest garrison, and dusty archives where fragments of old lore sleep. It’s the place where goods, favors, and secrets flow together.

## Inhabitants

- Merchants, guild officials, clerks, scholars in small archives, and a city watch. Noble agents and discreet patrons walk the streets.
- Archivists and scribes who guard old records and forgotten tomes.

## Plot Hooks

- The city’s courts and guildhalls are tangled with favors and secrets — hunt for agents of the Shadowed Branch, find a discreet patron willing to fund your inquiry, or search archives for clues about the cabal’s aims.
- Scraps in Everspring’s records may reveal how the cult has traded influence over generations — or point to ritual knowledge that could be used, dangerously, to oppose the cabal.
- If your group considers wielding magic to halt the hidden cabal, Everspring’s archives and learned folk are the likeliest place to find rituals or fragmented lore — but such knowledge is risky and may attract unwanted attention.
